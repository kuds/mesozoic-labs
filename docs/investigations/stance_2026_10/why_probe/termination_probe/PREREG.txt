ITEM 2.  IS A LOWER TAIL ACTUALLY RISKIER?
==================================================================================================================
Event E_H(t) = 1 if the episode ends in tail_contact and T - 1 - t < H (the terminal transition is within the next H
transitions), H = 200 (primary) and 50.  Eligible states: every state of a terminated episode; in a truncated episode
t <= 1000 - H.
M2.1 (primary, episode-level, robust to few events): for each tail_contact episode in the NON-BLIND SET, the
     percentile rank of q3 at s_{T-H} (H steps before its terminal transition; if T < H, s_0) within the distribution of
     q3 over all eligible states of its condition.  Under no relation the ranks are uniform; B predicts low ranks.
     One-sided sign test (count of ranks < 0.5).  B SUPPORTED if p <= 0.05 at H = 200; NOT SUPPORTED if p > 0.05;
     UNTESTABLE if the NON-BLIND SET has fewer than 5 tail_contact episodes.  Reported also at H = 50, and for tip and
     low in place of q3 (secondary).
M2.2 (secondary): pooled logistic regression of E_H on one standardised feature (q3, tip, low) with a condition
     intercept (conditions without an event dropped); slope per SD with the cluster-bootstrap interval.  Report only.
M2.3 (the funding test, primary for B-GENUINE): does the realised tail_contact risk account for the critic's slope?
     D_t = 0.98^(T-1-t) if the episode ends in tail_contact, else 0; states t <= 800 (censoring at 1,000 then weighs
     < 0.018).  C_c = 100 / sigma_R + Vbar_c, the normalised value lost to a termination (fall penalty plus the
     continuation value; Vbar_c = median V over states 200 <= t <= 800 of the condition's truncated episodes).  Pooled
     OLS of C_c D_t on q3 with a condition intercept -> b; risk-implied slope s_risk = -b (per degree); the critic's
     slope s_crit = mean s(t) over the same states; funding ratio F = s_risk / s_crit; cluster-bootstrap interval.
     B-GENUINE (sufficient) SUPPORTED if F >= 0.5 with the interval excluding 0; NOT SUPPORTED if the interval's upper
     end < 0.5; INCONCLUSIVE otherwise.  If the NON-BLIND SET has fewer than 5 tail_contact episodes, F's interval is
     not trusted and the bound decides: Dbar_u = (Clopper-Pearson 95% upper count of tail_contact episodes) x
     sum_{j<800} 0.98^j / (number of eligible states); F_bound = mean(C_c) x beta* x Dbar_u / s_crit with beta* = 1.0
     per degree (an e-fold change of the discounted tail_contact exposure per degree of tail_3, which I regard as
     physically generous: 1 deg is 13 mm of tip height); NOT SUPPORTED if F_bound < 0.5, else INCONCLUSIVE; the
     beta needed for F = 0.5 is reported.  Prediction: NOT SUPPORTED.
     Secondary (report only): the same with D for ANY termination (F_any): a raised tail can relabel a backward fall
     (fallen <-> tail_contact) without changing whether the episode ends, and only the ending carries value.
M2.4 (descriptive): M_H(t) = min of low over s_t..s_{t+H}; its 0.1 / 1 / 5 percentiles per condition and the
     within-condition Spearman correlation of q3(t) with M_200(t).
