Q1  What starts the forward lean in the first 131,072 steps
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Observed. All three r10 seeds leaned forward by the first evaluation: whole-foot CoP 0.18 / 0.33 / 0.32 at 0.125M,
  against a held statue's 0.11; r9 (estimated from the pad) 0.04 / 0.18 / 0.17 against 0.10; policy std 0.218-0.219
  (V3 lines 103-105). By 0.25M the r10 leg DC is hip pitch +0.039 to +0.043 and ankle -0.044 to -0.048 (V3 lines
  106-109). s60 passed 0.8 at 0.88M, s52 at 1.75M; s42 peaked at 0.65 at 0.62M (V3 lines 121-125; REC section 7).
Hypotheses in the record.
  H1b  Noise-fall asymmetry: under exploration noise the r10 statue falls backward (tail contact), so a forward lean
       raises the noisy return. Withdrawn as stated: 20 new spawns gave -8 [-237, +229] at sigma 0.10, pooled +149
       [-67, +381]; the lean costs at sigma 0.15 and 0.21 (V3 section 4 item 3; REC section 11). What survives: at sigma
       0.10 a modest lean costs about nothing on r10 but about 807 on r9; the r10 statue falls backward more often.
  Correlate only: tail contact was 0.52-0.59 of r10's terminations in the first 131k steps against 0.30-0.38 on r9
       (V3 lines 110-120); shares, not rates (V3 section 6).
  Exploration: the push coincides with the high initial std; a lower log_std_init is "not shown to help" (V3 section 8).
  Keyframe pull: about half the incentive of the r9 control's mirror-even lean was leg_home_pose pulling toward the
       keyframe (REC section 5.1); under arm L's settled target the term is the main RESTORING slope (-106 / -109 per
       unit, V3 section 5), so it is not the driver in the r10 runs.
  Stated limit: V3's probe is open loop; a closed-loop policy's own noise was never tested (V3 section 4, H1b limits).
Discriminating measurements.
  M1a [OFF-LOG, stoch, per rollout of 8,192 steps, 0-0.5M] Termination RATES by cause: TensorBoard
      'terminations/total_count' (diagnostics.py:285, TensorBoard only) times diagnostics.npz term_* shares
      (diagnostics.py:450-453), r10 s42/s52/s60 (TB event files on Drive, DA section 1); r9 arm L from updates.jsonl
      episode lengths (T1: first 262k-step bin 82-86% of ended episodes early, then 1-3%). Then regress the per-eval
      change in leg DC (gate_progress action_dc_per_actuator, 131k cadence) on the preceding backward-fall and
      forward-fall counts. H1b-in-closed-loop predicts r10 backward-fall counts precede and scale the forward DC move;
      no relation favours a non-fall cause.
  M1b [OFF-SIM, stoch with fixed noise streams + det] Closed-loop version of V3's test on the EARLY r10 checkpoints that
      exist on Drive (s52 best_model ~131k and robust_best ~262k; s60 best/robust 131k; all seeds at 1M; DA section 1):
      paired spawns 3042-3061 and paired noise streams at the checkpoint's own sigma (about 0.218), policy as is versus
      policy with its own leg DC subtracted from its action (closed loop). Read survival, return, fall cause, whole-foot
      CoP. If removing the lean lowers the noisy return or survival, the lean is paid for under the policy's own noise;
      if not, H1b is dead in closed loop too. Same on r9 arm L 1M checkpoints (session scratch, DA section 2).
  M1c [TEL, for any future approved run, det + stoch, every PPO update for the first 0.5M] (i) a probe-set readout: the
      deterministic policy's per-actuator action on a fixed bank of about 256 recorded stance states, logged each update
      (no simulation; gives the DC trajectory at 8,192-step resolution, which no evaluation cadence can); (ii) a
      per-episode training-rollout record: length, termination cause, termination step and the window fore-aft CoM
      offset (cheap: subtree_com, as base_env.py:691-702 already computes laterally). The r9 scratch harness logged
      (ii) per episode (DA section 2); main does not (CC C1-C3).
  Note: the onset is inside the first evaluation interval on every r10 seed, so only [TRAIN] data at sub-131k resolution
  can time it; M1a/M1b can only test the mechanism after the fact.

Q2  Why seeds diverge: seed 42 came back, seeds 52 and 60 stayed on their toes
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Observed. s42's excursion (0.65 at 0.62M) came back under 0.2 by 1.50M; s52/s60 stood at whole-foot CoP 0.93 / 1.05 at
  4-6M, earned 97 / 163 less per episode than s42, and 28/115 and 55/121 of their 5-6M training rollouts had a
  termination against 5/112 (V3 section 2; REC section 7). Their own mean action DC held open loop survives 0/8 (legs)
  and 2/8 (with head and tail); an open-loop escape path shows no barrier (V3 section 3). s42 drifted back up later:
  0.32-0.42 in the last four evals before 6M (V3 section 2), over-bar fraction 0.035 over 0-6M against 0.169 over
  6-12M (EV/r10_screen/report_only_summary.json; REC section 6.7). s52/s60 later raised head and tail (4-6M neck/head
  DC -0.26 / -0.30, V3 section 2).
Hypotheses in the record.
  H1a  Permissive plant: on r10 nothing physical stops a drift between CoP 0.8 and about 0.9 (5-of-8 edge 0.89-0.94
       against 0.76-0.79 on r9) (V3 section 4 item 1; REC section 7). Permits, does not cause.
  H2   A worse basin held by closed-loop feedback: "the likely candidate is that a policy's balance feedback is tuned to
       its own operating point. Testing that needs the checkpoints" (V3 section 4 item 2).
  Not attributable: in the divergence window (0.6-1.6M) the eval terms only follow the posture (V3 section 6).
  Historical: one config, two basins, seed-sensitive (KI lines 71-143, r7/r8).
  Additional hypothesis NOT in the record (flagged as new): sensing. On r9 the passive toe angles predict pad CoP on
       held-out postures (rms 0.07-0.29) while foot_contact carries none (R^2 <= 0.13) (EV/run_a/observability.txt);
       r10 stiffens the MTP springs from 40 to 3000/4500/3000 N m/rad (REC section 6.1), which may remove that implicit
       load sensor, leaving the policy blind to where its weight sits (the observation has no CoP or CoM channel,
       base_env.py:813-866).
Discriminating measurements.
  M2a [OFF-SIM, det, closed loop] Basin-stability (perturb-and-release): on r10 s42/s52/s60 at 1M, 2M, 4M, 6M (Drive),
      add a constant leg offset of +/-lambda along the s52/s60 DC direction (V3 lines 132-134: hip pitch +0.110, knee
      +0.0045, ankle -0.083 per side) for steps 200-400, release, and record whole-foot CoP every step to 1,000. H2 predicts each policy
      returns to its own CoP (an attractor, different per seed); H1a alone predicts neutral drift or a fall.
  M2b [OFF-SIM, det] State transplant: start s52's policy from states recorded under s42's policy at step 400 (and the
      reverse). H2 predicts s52 carries s42's posture forward to its own operating point within a few seconds.
  M2c [OFF-SIM, det, no rollouts] Local feedback gains: finite-difference Jacobian of the deterministic action with
      respect to the pitch-related observation channels (projected gravity, gyro, toe angles) at each policy's own
      operating states; compare s42 with s52/s60 and across 1M-6M.
  M2d [OFF-SIM, det] Sensing: repeat run_a/observability.txt's fits on r10 (statue and held postures, and the r10
      checkpoints' panel rollouts): whole-foot CoP from toe angles alone and from the full observation. If toe angles
      lose their predictive power on r10, the r10 policies sense less of their fore-aft load than r9's.
  M2e [OFF-LOG, det, per eval at 131k] Divergence window 0.6-1.6M: per-actuator DC (gate_progress
      action_dc_per_actuator), digit share, toe margin, whole-foot CoP std and dominant Hz (pad_telemetry.py:298-319)
      and TB termination counts, on all three r10 seeds: which channel moved first on s42 when it came back.
  M2f [TEL] Keep checkpoints every 262,144 steps through 3M in a future run (the stage keeps 5 periodic checkpoints,
      stance.toml:914; the r10 screen kept every 1,048,576, DA section 1), so a divergence window can be replayed.

Q4  Reward-selected "best" checkpoints are early (or wrong) ones
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Observed. 1M probes: robust_best at 50k-350k steps, 34-39/40 clean; run A: robust_best the edge policy at 3.2M (REC
  sections 3.1, 3.4). r10: the certificate judged robust_best last written at about 4.3M / 262k / 131k
  (s42 / s52 / s60); best_eval_timestep 5,636,096 / 131,072 / 131,072 (REC section 6.7). r9 arm L: SB3 best at 0.39-0.52M
  and robust_best at 0.52-0.92M on all three seeds, best eval means 3973.5-3982.4 against the arm L statue's 3980 (T2).
Hypotheses in the record.
  The deterministic return peaks near the statue: without noise the trained policies earn 10.6-29.6 less than the statue;
  "(inference) PPO's return mostly prices surviving the noise"; the return is flat in CoP (REC section 3.3). Hence
  "reward-based selection is not a safeguard" (REC sections 3.4, 10, 12).
  Not in the record (flagged): selection noise. Each evaluation rolls different spawns (eval env seeded once,
  train_base.py:201, 211, 1222-1231), and an eval mean's standard error (7.5-11.3) is comparable to the change between
  consecutive evaluations (11.1-20.7) on the r9 arm L runs (T2), so a max over about 48-90 noisy means favours lucky
  evaluations.
Discriminating measurements.
  M4a [OFF-SIM, det] Re-score every retained checkpoint on ONE fixed panel (3042-3081): r10 at 131k, 262k and 1M-11M
      (Drive), r9 arm L at 1M-6M (scratch). If the fixed-panel return still peaks early, the record's hypothesis holds; if
      the fixed-panel ranking departs from the in-training ranking, selection noise contributes.
  M4b [OFF-SIM, stoch] The same checkpoints at their own sigma: the record's hypothesis predicts the noisy return rises
      with training while the deterministic one falls.
  M4c [TEL] Record each evaluation's reset seeds (or roll the same spawns every evaluation, which changes evaluation
      semantics: maintainer). Selection is in scope: the handoff, the certificate and every stance probe use the
      reward-selected checkpoint (checkpoints.py:108-122; stance.toml:1062-1066); a gate-metric selection (most clean, or
      lowest over-bar fraction on a fixed panel) is a proposal for the maintainer.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Observed. 6M joint-space falls 1 + 1 + 6 = 8/60 on r9 arm L (own-sigma 0/60) and 0 + 7 + 9 = 16/60 on r10 (own 2/60)
  (REC sections 5.4, 6.4). r9 s60 by checkpoint: 1 / 2 / 18 / 6 of 20 at 3-6M (EV/symmetry/verdict.json). The noise is

  about 0.09 at 6M (V3 section 7); the statue falls 20/20 at uniform 0.16 and 3/20 at 0.07
  (EV/cap_screens/screen_3m_noise_analysis.txt line 151). Zero-shot, four r9-trained policies fall 0/80 on r10 against
  9/80 on r9 (REC section 6.1).
Hypotheses in the record.
  Posture margin: drifted r10 seeds fall (7, 9) and s42 does not (REC section 6.4); the pilot criteria read a FALLS
  outcome against the parent's "6 of 20 joint-space noise falls" (EV/pilot/criteria.txt lines 258-261).
  Plant margin: the r10 foot helps r9-trained policies zero-shot (REC section 6.1), yet r10-trained policies fall more.
  The test measures what trained policies add over the statue (REC section 13).
  Against posture alone (reading of verdict.json, not a record claim): s60 falls 6/20 at 6M at even CoP 0.29 but
  1/20 at 3M at 0.55.
Discriminating measurements.
  M5a [OFF-SIM, stoch, fixed streams: numpy default_rng(episode seed) as the frozen judges] An amplitude sweep,
      k in {0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1.0, 1.25} x the control vector, per checkpoint: the k at which half the spawns fall (a
      continuous margin instead of a count), each fall's cause and step, and whole-foot CoP and toe margin over the 50
      steps before it; beside the same spawn's deterministic toe margin. Posture margin predicts k50 tracks toe margin
      across checkpoints and seeds; feedback quality predicts it does not.
  M5b [OFF-SIM, stoch] Per-channel noise (legs only, axial only, hip roll only): which channel's noise topples.
  M5c [OFF-SIM, stoch] Each checkpoint's mean action held open loop under the same noise: the closed-loop minus
      open-loop difference is the policy's added robustness.
  Runs: r9 arm L s42/s52/s60 at 3-6M (scratch), r10 s42/s52/s60 at 1M-6M (Drive), the pilot parent.
