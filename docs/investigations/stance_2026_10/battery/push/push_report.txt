  impulse_variants / render_impulse_probe / write_impulse_probe, driven by stage_artifacts._write_impulse_probe when
  a stage sets stance_probe_impulse_speeds (T. rex stance.toml: [0.5, 1.0, 2.0]). The shove is qvel[0:3] += delta_v
