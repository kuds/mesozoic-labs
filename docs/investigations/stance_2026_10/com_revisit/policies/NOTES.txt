6. A PRE-REGISTERED CoM CHECK ON TRAINED POLICIES: CoM-P1
==================================================================================================================
The rule (PREREG.txt, sha256 cc267c52... in PREREG.sha256, 13:45:05 UTC):
  - Window from 5 s, on a panel of deterministic episodes at the stance config's noise.
  - LEAN = com_lateral_mean_m or root_roll_mean_deg keeps one sign across the seeds (two-sided sign test p <=
    0.005: at least 17 of 20, or 30 of 40) with |median| >= 1 mm, or >= 0.10 deg, twice the resolution floors.
  - CREEP = at least 2 episodes whose feet's mean position moves at 1 mm/s or more by least squares over the window.
  - Report-only: the same rule on the load share and the window turn, and the creep direction.
check.py implements the rule and the pure helper in the patch (stance_track_helpers.policy_com_check,
feet_creep_speed) implements the same rule.
Measured (check_final.txt, check_r9.txt):
                                   LEAN     CREEP    keys at p<=0.005 ignoring the floor
  26 policies, M1 seeds 3042-3061   0/26     0/26     0/52
  26 policies, held-out 3062-3071   0/26     0/26     1/52 (s80_B_1401 roll 0+/10-, median -0.013 deg)
  statue, 6 panels of 20            0/6      0/6      0/12
  r9 checkpoint (positive control)  FLAG     -        2/2  (CoM 1+/19- med -1.30 mm; roll 19+/1- med +0.126 deg)
- False-alarm rate.  Measured 0/26 + 0/26 on policies and 0/6 on the statue null.  Under a mirror-symmetric plant
  and spawn the analytic rate is <= 0.0026 per key and <= 0.0052 for LEAN on 20 seeds.  The reset noise is i.i.d.
  uniform per joint and velocity (base_env.py:1609-1612), so its distribution is mirror-symmetric [inferred].
- Replication: no M1 flag to replicate.  Of the 52 unflagged keys, 1 had the M1 median's sign on at least 9 of 10
  held-out seeds anyway.  Without the floor, the sign test flags sub-resolution biases of deterministic policies:
  s80_B_1401 held-out 0.013 deg, s80_A_1301 0.018 deg at 9 s.  The floor is what keeps those from being alarms.
- Sensitivity on these policies' own spread (power.py -> power.txt).  A same-side bias added to the 20 values flags
  0 % at 0.5 mm / 0.05 deg (by design), 62-85 % at 1.5 mm, 88-92 % at 2 mm and 92-100 % at 3 mm.  For roll:
  0 / 96 % at 0.10 deg (against / along the policy's own sign) and 96-100 % at 0.15 deg.  On 20 seeds the CoM key
  is the weaker one (spread 1.35-1.75 mm); the gate's 40 seeds would sharpen it [inferred].
- Cost.  On the round's M1 panel CoM-P1 needs one 20-episode rollout per checkpoint, about 55 s on one core here
  [measured].  In the gate it costs nothing extra: the evaluation recorder already captures the CoM, the root
  quaternion and the feet (balance=True, track_from_episode_trace), and body telemetry already writes
  root_roll_mean_deg per evaluation episode (gait/body_telemetry.py).  com_lateral_mean_m is not yet a balance
  column (gait/balance_telemetry.py has only the fore-aft keys) [measured: grep].
