Spawns: the class's own make_env (reset_noise_scale overridden), neutral action, run_tracked_episode, the class's
  reduce, spread bars and support checks (as creeplib.run does).  Statue = make_env() (reset noise 0), seed 0.
  CoM tests' noise 0.02: seeds 0-29, 100-149, 200-399 (280).  Training noise, read from each stance config's [env]
  reset_noise_scale: configs/compsognathus_longipes/anatomical/stance.toml 0.01; configs/velociraptor_mongoliensis/
  anatomical/stance.toml 0.05 (its only reset key; the height channel is overwritten by _settle_root_on_ground): seeds
  0-199 (200).  All other env kwargs stay the test class's.
Detection (scanlib.py; creeplib.py copied unchanged from ../creep/, sha256 1363d927...):
  speed  : com_speed_end_rms_mps (the test reduction's RMS horizontal CoM speed over the window's last 2 s) > 1 mm/s;
           this is creeplib.summary's "creeps".
  travel : the largest horizontal travel of any foot reference site from its position at t = 1 s, over t >= 1 s
           (control-rate samples of the test's sampler) > 5 mm.
  oscill : per physics substep, each foot's floor normal load (the sampler's own attribution: floor contacts of the
           foot's leg geoms, mj_contactForce, through the env's _substep_probe_hook); over the last 2 s its linearly
           detrended standard deviation > 1 % of the body weight on either foot.  Frequency = the peak above 1 Hz of
           the Hann-windowed spectrum of that foot's load over the window.
  creep  = all three.  Each flag is also counted alone, with falls and spread-bar failures.
Scripts: scan_all.py (driver, resumable) -> scan.jsonl (962 rows), scan.log; scan_table.py -> scan_table.txt.

6. THE MECHANISM'S INGREDIENTS ON THE TWO MODELS (no creeper, so a precondition check, not a confirmation)
======================================================================================================================
6a Compsognathus anatomical [measured: mech.py -> mech.txt (seed 313 at 0.02, the block's largest end speed and foot
   travel); contacts.py -> contacts.txt]
   - The light DOF is in the load path: the ankle carries the metatarsus and the whole foot (plantar pad, three toes).
     The largest h*M^-1*D is 3.448 (r_ankle) at landing, 3.447 (l_ankle) at 10 s, matching the audit's 3.45
     (verifier v_audit.txt 3.449).
   - Landing (0.02-0.12 s): seed 313 stands on the plantar pad and toe d2 only (4.1-7.8 N per foot), on a contact
     line 58-60 mm long and 1.1-4.2 mm wide.  The load point sits 16.4 mm off the foot site's centre line,
     mirrored on the two feet.  Lateral friction is 1.77 / 1.67 N per foot (ratio 0.39 / 0.30) and the hip rolls are
     1.07 / 0.97 deg off.  The solver's v + h*qacc is off by up to 1.447 rad/s (l_ankle; median 0.035), and the
     realised step equals the (M + hD) formula to 5e-11.  The contacts do +2.79 mJ along v1 (+1.34 along v0) while the
     energy falls 4.81 mJ.  These are the same ingredients as the robot's resting spawn 141 at landing (+7.40 mJ,
     prediction error 2.46; mech.txt).
   - By 10 s: 10 contacts per foot over 73 x 34 mm, lateral 0.078 N (ratio 0.016), load point within 2.6 mm of the
     centre line, prediction error 0.0000, contact work -0.0014 mJ.  Every one of the 480 spawns rings down
     (section 4).
   [inferred: the lens's 6g said "its foot has no box-sole edge to rock on"] The anatomical foot does land on a narrow
   line with a pre-stress, so the edge is not what it lacks.  With the light ankle in the load path at a larger
   eigenvalue than the robot's, it still never locks into a cycle.  Per the verifier's C5, lock-in depends on more
   than h*M^-1*D (at least h*D/I together with omega*dt; here 0.73 at the ankle, audit_models_main.txt).  Which
   difference keeps this model clear was not isolated.
6b Velociraptor [measured: contacts.txt, mech.txt]
   - The light DOF (claw, 1.963) carries only the claw body, and the claw geom never touches the floor: the floor load
     sits on the metatarsus and toes d3 and d4, on the statue and on a spawn, at the first step and at the end.  That is
     why claw armature x3 changes nothing (5d).  The next DOF in the audit (tail_5_pitch 0.98) is not in contact
     either: no non-foot geom carries floor load in the last 2 s of any spawn (scan_table.txt) or at the instants in
     contacts.txt; earlier instants were not checked.
   - Its servos have no kv; the damping is implicit joint damping, the kind the verifier showed can creep (M4), but
     on DOFs heavy enough that the solver's prediction stays within 0.04 at landing (seed 126 at 0.05).

7. FOR THE MAINTAINER
======================================================================================================================
  [measured, sections 1-5]  Neither model has a creeper in its 481 neutral-action episodes (statue, the CoM tests'
  noise, its own training noise): 0 of 280 and 0 of 200 each.  The upper 95 % bound is 1.3 % at 0.02 against the robot's 3.6 %
  (1.7-6.5 %) on the same seeds.
  [inferred]
  - No plant fix is indicated for either model on this evidence; the decision on the robot (D1) is unaffected.
  - D4 (an eigenvalue limit as a plant check) is contradicted again: it would flag the anatomical Compsognathus
    (3.45), which does not creep on 480 spawns.  The scan here (scan_all.py with scanlib.py; about 30 min for
    both models, 19 of them the Compsognathus) is the empirical check the verifier proposed in C8/M1.
  - The Velociraptor's 0.05 travel flag is a 14 s friction relaxation that the 10 s horizon cuts off.  It is not a
    creep, and the CoM tests run at 0.02, where it stays under every bar (largest in-window foot travel 3.19 mm against
    the 5.6 mm width).  Whether that slow sideways relaxation matters for training at 0.05 (the drift penalty, the
    spawn-relative keys) was not examined.
  Not tested [open]: anything but the neutral action (pushes, policies, scripted stances), other MuJoCo versions,
  other seeds, the T. rex, Brachiosaurus and Dibothrosuchus.
