MY HARNESS (vs_lib.py) -- independent of the scan's scanlib.py / creeplib.py
======================================================================================================================
Shares only the code under test: the committed test classes' make_env, run_tracked_episode (stepping) and the test
reduction (for its own com_speed_end_rms_mps).  Computed here from raw MjData:
  - foot attribution by kinematic subtree (the root child that carries each foot's site), not Morphology.geom_foot;
  - body weight = summed mass of the animal's kinematic tree x g; CoM = mass-weighted xipos (not subtree_com);
  - my own end speed (forward differences), foot travel after 1 s, per-substep per-foot load sd (last 2 s,
    detrended), unloaded fraction, root vz, per-foot world lateral floor force, and per-geom floor contact over the
    WHOLE episode (every substep).
Same bars as the scan: speed 1 mm/s, foot travel after 1 s 5 mm, load sd 1 % of body weight; creep3 = all three.

======================================================================================================================
WHAT THE SCAN MISSED
======================================================================================================================
M1 [measured, F10] The Velociraptor's slide time constant is set by contact softness AND servo kp (kp x2 halves it;
   impratio 10 makes it 10x longer; noslip removes the slide and leaves a 7 N sideways pre-stress in place).  Any
   change to kp, impratio, noslip or solref (e.g. future domain randomization) changes this slide; the robot-fix
   analogues do not.
M2 [measured, vs_ext] The travel flag is horizon-dependent: at the tests' 0.02, seed 274 crosses 5 mm of foot travel
   after 10 s (6.80 mm by 60 s).  A 0.02 scan on a 60 s horizon would show travel flags too; 0/280 at 0.02 is "within
   10 s", not "never".
M3 [measured, F4] NOTES section 4's Compsognathus end-speed column is 1000x too large (conservative direction).
M4 [measured, F2] The headline pooled Fisher p (2.7e-5) mixes noise levels; like-for-like is 9.1e-4.
M5 [measured, F3] The detector also catches the other 8 known robot creepers on all three flags (the scan only checked
   111 and 143) with at least 3x margin per flag, and 32 resting robot seeds are clean: sensitivity and specificity
   on everything known.
M6 [inferred] Only neutral-action episodes on flat ground were scanned: pushes, policies, terrain and other MuJoCo
   versions stay open (the scan says so).
