Definitions (as THIS12 lines 297-337, unchanged in substance, with PRECEDENCE: no saturation counts, no T).
- TAIL ACTUATORS 11-14; TAIL PITCH ACTUATORS 11, 13, 14; TAIL-UP = a positive command on a tail pitch actuator.
- BAR: an episode's share of window steps at |command| >= 0.99 on an actuator exceeds 0.10.
- TAIL FLAG (M1): a tail actuator over the BAR in a FULL-HORIZON (1000-step) traced episode, with its sign; on an exact
  tie the flag counts once in each sign; flags in shorter episodes are FALL FLAGS (report only).
- ANY-FLAGGED EVALUATION: at least 5 of its 10 ledger episodes over the bar on any actuator (M5 (a)). ANY ALARM: the
  second of two consecutive any-flagged evaluations (D-D40 5a as r9 was read).
- TAIL-FLAGGED EVALUATION: at least 5 of its 10 episodes over the bar with a tail actuator named (M5 (b)). TAIL ALARM:
  the second of two consecutive tail-flagged evaluations.
- TAIL-UNRESOLVED EPISODE: over the bar, a non-tail actuator named, and saturated_actuator_ties >= 1 or
  actuators_saturated >= 2. An ANY ALARM without a TAIL ALARM at its step NEEDS RESOLUTION when counting the
  TAIL-UNRESOLVED episodes of either evaluation as tail episodes would make both tail-flagged; its RESOLUTION CHECKPOINT
  is the pair at the second evaluation's step when that step is a multiple of 262,144, else the pair at the next
  multiple of 262,144 at or before E, else stage1_final (C3 then fails when that pair is not in the manifest). It

  budget).
