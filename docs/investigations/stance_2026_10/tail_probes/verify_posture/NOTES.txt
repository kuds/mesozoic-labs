  (95% 0.07-0.27M) and the forward CoM about 0.4M later still.  At a fixed observation (the probe bank) the s42 tail
  command was already rising at 1.3-1.6M, so the first rise was a posture-independent change in the policy.
- Test 2's measurements reproduce closely on my own settled states, which come from different seeds (5001-5010).
  This covers the observation indices, the pitch / knee / hip pathway at the ends, the transplant, the closed-loop
  illusion and the cross-policy correlations.  Test 3's plant map and tail-up cost reproduce as well.
- Missed, and it changes the reading.  Neither test examined a checkpoint before 3,145,728 (the ECS "start", which is
  mid-rise).  The F2 copies hold 1,048,576 and 2,097,152 locally.
  - At 1,048,576 (before the rise) the s42 network had no nose-down-to-tail pathway (+0.005 per deg) and a NEGATIVE
    tail loop (G -0.25).
  - At 2,097,152 (early rise, cmd 0.14) the pathway was -0.020 per deg, about 1/8 of its 3.15M value.  G was still
    -0.39.  The s80 posture transplant removed only 0.058 of a 0.238 offset.
  - So the posture pathway and the positive loop grew AFTER the tail began rising.  They amplify the habit at 3.15M
    and later.  They are not where it started.
- Missed: the s60 run, which also parked, has the OPPOSITE pitch sensitivity at 5.2-8.4M: nose-UP raises its tail

- vp_sweep2.py -> vp_sweep2.txt / .json: network-only sweep on all 26 policies' own states.  G is computed both as

- vp_prerise.py -> vp_prerise.txt / .jsonl: the missed check on F2 checkpoints s42 / s80 at 1,048,576, 2,097,152 and

- vp_s60.py -> vp_s60.txt / .jsonl: s60 at 1.05, 5.24, 6.29, 7.34 and 8.39M; s80 at 5.24-8.39M.
