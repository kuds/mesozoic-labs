- The observation normaliser is SymmetricRunningMeanStd (mirror_kl_ppo.py l100-l140).  Each update adds the batch and
  its mirror image, so:
  - sagittal channels keep their batch mean: pitch joints, tail joints, g_x giving pelvis pitch, and leg pitch
    (left/right averaged);

  convention (+ nose up).

  - against ledger pitch: r +0.944 to +0.989;

4. COVERAGE GAPS (stated plainly)
==================================================================================================================
- r10 F2 diagnostics.npz (per-rollout training head_tip_z, tilt, pelvis height) is not local for s42 / s60 / s80.  It
  is on Drive (1.28 MB each) and was not fetched, under the rules.
  - So training-time head height for the parents is missing.  Head height there comes only from the deterministic
    ledger, every 131,072 steps.
- r9 has no recorded tail command per rollout and no named actuator in its ledgers.  The tail is read from the tail_3
  angle through a plant map measured at the statue posture.
  - r9 ledgers have no head, neck or tail columns.  Head and neck come only from the probe recovery.
  - r9 s42, s52 and s60 have no train_episodes.csv locally.
- stance4 (r9 LTS) has ledgers only.  No tail signal exists before the first over-bar episode, so timing cannot be
  ordered there.
- The r9 tail screen starts after r9 s42's alarm, and after r9 s80's rise had begun.  It has no CoM and no head
  height.
- No CoM height anywhere in the ledgers; pelvis height is the only height.
- Roll and lateral posture: the mirror-symmetric normaliser cancels lateral channels.  Lateral posture exists only in
  the r10 ledgers, which were not timed here.  REVIEW D-5 found hip roll within +-0.009 rad throughout.
- Resolution:
  - ledger every 131,072 steps; probe every 8,192 steps; "together" means within one 65,536-131,072-step bin.
  - Early training (< 0.5M) is excluded as reference noise; falls dominate there.
- Sample sizes and statistics:
  - one run per seed and plant (r10: 3 runs; r9 stance5: 5; stance4: 3; tail screen 3 per arm; ECS 12 per lineage);
  - thresholds are arbitrary, though pre-stated;
  - many signals were read, so single correlations near |0.5| mean little;
  - everything is correlational.  The interventions read here (r9 bound arms; the clamp test) are the only
    non-observational evidence, and the clamp test is not mine.
- Not done:
  - ECS's own end traces (r10round/ecs/traces) and the M1 / M2 panels beyond the REVIEW's tabulation;
  - gate_progress, which duplicates the ledger means.
