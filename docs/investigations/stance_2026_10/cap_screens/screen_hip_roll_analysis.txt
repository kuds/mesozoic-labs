10. RECOMMENDATION
- Stop authority_hr as predeclared. There is no r16 PR and no 12M Colab confirmation. The r16 spec and C1-C6 (screen3m_cop/analysis.json, seeds 42/52/60/48/56) stay on file unchanged. The spec is per-joint authority in the residual action map _scale_action: hip pitch x0.055, knee and ankle x0.025, and the hip roll x0.01 this screen tested, stance only. It has one declared quantity for the action-rate terms and #620's leg-DC telemetry, and the policy-interface revision r15 -> r16 moves task_sha256. The stance-to-locomotion handoff decision, now including hip roll, stays open.
- Do not rescue the result after the fact. H2 misses by one episode on one seed and the pooled count sits exactly at its limit. A 4.2-6.3M fit would pass s42's H3 (0.505) but not s60's (0.857). Neither near-miss is a pass under the predeclared rules.
- What the screen established. Bounding hip roll is safe for balance and helps it: panel falls 4 -> 0 of 120, joint-space falls 3 -> 1 of 60, and own-sigma falls 1 -> 0. It removes the commanded lean and the stance narrowing: full-horizon roll-rail failures 15 -> 6, H5 0.60 -> 0.18, v2 PASS 0/3 -> 2/3. The fore-aft edge holds at the panel (0/360 CoP>0.8 at 4-6M), but the in-training edge-pad creep is not bounded on 2 of 3 seeds.
- Next, per the predeclared branch (an H2 failure with a re-routed lean leads to (b)): calibrate the lateral-lean term on the existing rolls without training. These are authority_hr 6M, authority 6M and the statue. The term is w * exp(-(d/tol)^2) on the pelvis's lateral offset over the feet, with tol about 10 mm. Five of the 6 failures sit above 10 mm (12.5-16.4 mm) against a pooled |median| of 1.07 mm; the offset is d_lean x about 16 mm per deg. Because the residual lean is spread across channels, only a channel-agnostic term like this can price it. The data favour screening (b) on top of authority_hr rather than replacing the bound: the bound is not load-bearing and it removed the narrowing. That screen needs a new predeclaration and maintainer approval: H1-H5 unchanged, the term's cost on the statue <= 2, and the same 3M interim.
- H3 is not addressed by (b) as designed. (inference) If pricing the lean removes the incentive for s60's ankle and tail asymmetry, H3 could improve, but that has to be measured, not assumed. Report the edge-pad projection and the ankle L-R DC as the first read-outs of any (b) screen.
- Maintainer decisions the data now inform. (c) The T. rex hip-roll reset noise (0.05 rad, splay up to +-2.9 deg) is now the larger part of what fails: 62% of the failing roll is reset-set, and at nominal width the policies add about 1% and fail 0/42. Changing it is a task, gate and statue-reference revision (reset() is fingerprinted by home_reset), and it hides the response to splay rather than removing it. If the maintainer wants the full-panel number before deciding, the no-training proxy (re-judge the 6M checkpoints under nominal or half hip-roll noise, as roll_diag/proxy did for authority) is cheap. The anatomy route (load-bearing digits, longer support) does nothing for pad roll, which sits on the 9 cm width lever with no ankle roll DOF. It is the one lever aimed at H3's fore-aft margin, with projections of 0.76 and 0.82 pad half-lengths and the ankle drive pressing on its reach (s42 l_ankle 0.96, s60 r_ankle 0.91 of the clip). It stays a separate proposal with its own gate decision and a >= 3M screen against a control.
- Do not relax the bars (CoP 0.80, tilt 2.0 deg, corner 4 mm), and do not re-judge these runs under criteria chosen after seeing them.

11. CAVEATS
- One training run per seed. The 40 panel episodes of one policy are not replicates, and in-training evals are 10 deterministic episodes. authority_hr was trained fresh, so it is not paired with the authority arm's seeds.
- The 6M lineage is a resume from stage1_3145728_steps: one PPO update short of an uninterrupted run, with RNG and environments restarted. gate_progress continuity is verified: the first 24 rows equal the 3M file in every key, with 48 rows on the 131,072 cadence.
- H3's projection is linear by rule. s42's series is concave (t -2.97), so the linear value likely overstates it (inference). s60's is not concave.
- H2's per-seed failure is one episode over the limit, and the four s52 failures are 2.12-2.55 deg against the 2.0 bar.
- Sections 7-9 come from supplementary rollouts that were not predeclared: the published 22 conditions plus 6 sagittal-differential conditions, on the 19 diag spawns. Shares come from single-channel ablations and do not add, because closed-loop runs let the policy compensate. The sagittal-differential condition is new here: it replaces r and l with their mean for hip pitch, knee and ankle, and keeps the common mode.
- Correlations of in-training eval means against policy DC (section 9) are associations over 24 evals, not mechanisms.
- Nothing was trained, committed or pushed. The main checkout, the fix/screen2 worktree and every run directory were only read. New files are under foot/screen_hr/analysis_final/ and foot/screen_hr/analysis.{json,txt}.
