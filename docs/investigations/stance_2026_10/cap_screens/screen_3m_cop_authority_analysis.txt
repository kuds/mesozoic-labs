The PR if authority enters
  Change: T. rex anatomical stance: a per-joint authority on the residual action map, implemented in _scale_action as a declared per-stage vector rather than a wrapper. Hip pitch x0.055 (reach +/-3.575 deg), knee and ankle x0.025 (+/-1.25 deg); hip roll, neck, head and tail stay unscaled. Home is the range midpoint for every leg actuator, so these reaches hold on both sides. Under the in-env map, the action-rate terms (jerk, smoothness, saturation) and #620's leg-DC telemetry must both read one declared quantity. The screen's wrapper had the terms price the scaled command while the telemetry read the unscaled DC, 1/scale times too large. The PR should report the commanded degrees and the policy-space fraction of full scale.
  Digests and revisions: An action-mapping change is a policy-interface revision: T. rex anatomical r15 -> r16. It also moves task_sha256, because the map is in the stage fingerprint; the screen's experiment keys already moved it (control bde44225..., authority abef983e...). It does not move the plant identity, the physics revision (r9), the observation, or the gate (bars unchanged). r15 stance checkpoints cannot be handed to r16.   cop, a reward term, would have moved task_sha256 only. It is not proposed.

What the data suggests if authority fails too
  reward route: At this budget the reward route has not worked. Tried so far: a per-step clipped CoP margin (probe 1), a settled leg reference (probe 2), a capped leg-asymmetry penalty, and now a load-gated 0.5 s EMA centring bonus calibrated offline to z 6 at the control's noise (cop: 28/33/38 at 3M). Going toe-ward, the cop arm's deterministic bonus fell by 71-83 per episode between 0-1M and 2-3M (a shortfall of 112-129 from the 224 maximum), against a calibrated gap at training noise of 36-49 per episode. (inference) Whatever pays for the toe-ward posture in the stochastic objective is worth more than that, and this screen does not identify it. A larger term would outweigh foot_flatness and would need its own calibration against survival under noise. The kernel is also weak where it matters: exp(-u^4) still pays 88% at u 0.6 (CoP about 0.56) and 37% at the bar. That form should not be re-run as is.
  anatomy route: The foot review found that the T. rex foot is mechanically a 14 x 9 cm plate: the pad carries 83% of the load, the digits are loaded at their bases, and the toe-ward margin is 28 N.m. Load-bearing digits out to the digit III tip would raise the toe-ward capacity from about 88 to 125 N.m at 420 N (computed, inference) and fit tyrannosaurid tracks, which show wider, splayed tridactyl support. But the stiff-toe variant still read 0.924 under run A's held action, and this screen shows the drift does not stop at a fixed offset. The control saturates at gate CoP 0.98-1.00, cop gave up 71-83 per episode of deterministic bonus to go toe-ward, and authority's policy-space drive kept growing. (inference) A larger foot raises the offset needed to reach the bar; it does not remove the flat direction. It belongs in the anatomy accuracy proposals on its own merits, with its own gate decision (pad CoP or whole-foot CoP bar), and if pursued it should be screened against a control at >= 3M, not adopted as the fix.
  if authority fails too: Bounding the reachable antisymmetric offset is the only lever that has held the pads through 3M, with no reward change and no loss of deterministic return (authority eval return 3646-3768 at 2-3M, against the control's 3588-3759). If the creep continues through other dimensions, there are two candidates. (a) An offline-sized bound on tail_1 pitch, after checking that clean episodes do not need it. (b) A maintainer gate decision on what stance certifies beyond the statue, e.g. a noise-robustness criterion, so that the bar on a pad the plant can reach with a 3 deg offset is enforced by construction rather than by reward. Neither has been tested.

9. CAVEATS
- One training run per arm and seed; the 40 panel episodes of one policy are not replicates. The exact one-sided rank-sum at 3 vs 3 cannot go below p 0.05 (authority against control: 0.05 at every checkpoint; cop: 0.10-0.20).
- The control's 1M checkpoints repeat the 1M screen bit for bit (s52, s60; s42 reproduces exactly in the judge), so its 1M cells are not new samples.
- stage1_final (one PPO update after 3M; parameters differ, obs_rms equal) was not judged in any arm.
- The authority noise results in the judge are in policy space by design. The joint-space comparison is a supplementary test run by the seed agents with the same draws; it reproduces the judge's control result exactly on s42 3M.
- In-training evals are 10 deterministic episodes, so fractions move in steps of 0.1, and slopes over 8 evals carry SE 0.006-0.06/M.
- Wall times for seeds 42 and 52 were 11,852-13,379 s against the 7,422-8,104 s estimate, because their six trainers shared 4 CPUs with the judges; seed 60 ran at 7,004-8,149 s. All 9 trainers and 27 judges exited 0 at HEAD 94cab5ce with a clean tree, and every panel ran on the unmodified stage (task_sha256 bde44225...). The trainers are seeded and single-threaded, so the results are unaffected.
- The authority arm is a scratch action wrapper. A production map inside the env changes what the action-rate terms price and what the leg-DC telemetry reads, so the 6M extension still tests the wrapper, not the PR.
- Projections to 9-13M and the tail-saturation estimate are linear extrapolations (inference).
- Nothing was committed, pushed or merged; the main checkout and the fix/screen2 worktree were only read.
