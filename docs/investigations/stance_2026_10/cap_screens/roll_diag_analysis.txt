1. ANSWER
The authority policy does not merely hold the reset's lateral splay; it drives most of the roll that fails, through the hip-roll actuators, during the settle, and holds it actively through the window. But it does so only on spawns the reset splayed. On its 15 full-horizon roll failures (6M, 3 seeds, 14 focus spawns) the failing pad's window roll is 2.34 deg (median). Of that, 35% is reset-set: the statue on the same spawn and pad, 0.91 deg of hip-roll reset splay that friction holds. The other 65% (1.64 deg, range 0.57-2.79) is policy-added. 49% of the roll is a sideways sway: the hip-roll common mode at 1.42 deg commanded, which moves the pelvis 18.7 mm over the feet while pelvis roll stays within 0.2 deg, flattening one pad and rolling the other further. Another 16% is stance narrowing from an inward hip-roll differential DC of +1.98 deg, which makes the window width 13.7 mm narrower. Ablations: the policy's hip-roll command alone, replayed on the statue, reproduces the added roll (60% of the failing roll) and 17/18 focus failures, falls included. Tail and neck yaw alone reproduce 1. Zeroing hip roll in closed loop cuts focus failures 18 -> 5 (falls 3 -> 0), and removing its common mode cuts them to 2. Zeroing tail and neck yaw cuts them to 13 in closed loop and to 18 in open loop, so yaw has no mechanical path: s42 ties its lean direction to its tail yaw. A nominal-width reset cuts them to 1. The sway builds from step ~50 to ~200 (onset of a 0.5 deg excess at median step 45; about 82% of the final excess is there by step 195). Zeroing every command at step 200 brings every failing pad back under the bar, to within 0.6 deg of the statue (median 0.2). 1 failure is left, a fall already committed. 47 of 48 authority full-horizon roll failures at 3-6M are on the 15 most-splayed of the 40 panel spawns.
