8. CAVEATS
- 5 training seeds per arm, 1M steps against run A's 12M, and two checkpoints.
- The episodes of one policy are not replicates.
- The 10-episode in-training evals are coarse, in steps of 0.1.
- The noise-survival comparison is not at matched sigma.
- The low-noise arms change the exploration noise that the D-D27 per-step penalty budget in stance.toml was calibrated against.
- Edge drift is slowed, not shown to be stopped: on lownoise s60 the left pad went from 0.29 to 0.59 between 524k and 1M and was still rising.
