ANSWER
- Low noise (-2.5) delays the toe edge but does not prevent it.
  - CoP failures by seed (s42/s52/s60): 1M 4/10/2, 2M 3/13/23, 3M 16/19/22 (stage1_final 17/20/22).
  - Only s42 at 2M stays at <=3/40. At 3M no seed does.
  - At 3M it has 57/120 CoP failures, about the control's 61/120 at 1M.
- Middle noise (-2.0) neither prevents nor delays the edge. It behaves like the control:
  - CoP failures 36/25/25 at 1M, 40/24/38 at 2M, 40/32/40 at 3M;
  - its edge forms in training at 0.39-0.79M, against the control's 0.66-0.92M.
- Noise tolerance at matched sigma 0.16 (falls of 60 episodes on these seeds):
  - low noise 23 (1M), 35 (2M), 33 (3M);
  - middle noise 32, 58, 57;
  - control at 1M, same test: 4/60 (6/100 over five seeds; 10/100 with torch-sampled own noise).
  Middle noise is worse than low noise on both the edge and the noise test, so it is not a better balance.
- Gate: at 2M and 3M no seed of either arm reaches 37/40 clean.
  - That holds under the current bars and under every option tested: absolute bars up to 3.5 deg / 7 mm, both bars removed,
    and the statue-relative rails.
  - The CoP rail is what binds. With CoP removed as well (reference only, not an option), 2 of 3 seeds pass in each arm.
- Recommendation:
  - No PR for either log_std_init value; main keeps -1.5.
  - No gate decision is needed first.
  - No 12M Colab confirmation for these arms.
  - The edge does not change the training reward, and the reward has no fore-aft CoP term. The next lever is therefore a
    training-side CoP signal at the production noise level, screened at 3M on seeds 42, 52 and 60. That needs maintainer
    approval.

1. PREVENT OR DELAY?  LOW NOISE DELAYS THE EDGE; MIDDLE NOISE DOES NEITHER
Criterion: CoP>0.8 <= 3/40 per seed at 2M and 3M.
  lownoise: 2M [3, 13, 23] -> 1/3 (s42)   3M [16, 19, 22] -> 0/3   stage1_final [17, 20, 22]
  midnoise: 2M [40, 24, 38] -> 0/3        3M [40, 32, 40] -> 0/3   stage1_final [40, 32, 40]
- Low noise at 3M is no better than the control at 1M: [16, 19, 22] against [21, 28, 12] (overlapping).
- Low noise beats middle noise at 3M on every seed, with complete separation (one-sided exact rank-sum p = 0.05, the smallest
  that 3 vs 3 allows).

Reading:
- Low noise roughly halves the edge pad's early drift rate: +0.10 to +0.51/M against the control's +0.56 to +0.83/M.
- The drift goes on through 1-2M and ends in the same place.
- On s52 and s60 the 2-3M slope is about zero. The edge reached a plateau there; it did not recede. The panel still has 19-22 CoP
  failures at 3M and 20-22 at stage1_final.
- On s42 the drift is still rising at 3M (+0.17/M).
Leg DC (left minus right, deg) grows with the edge:
- low noise s52: knee +0.94 / +1.42 / +1.96, ankle +0.39 / +1.21 / +2.28;
- low noise s60: hip pitch +0.47 / +2.34 / +1.93, knee +0.16 / +1.20 / +1.95;
- low noise s42: ankle -0.31 / -1.28 / -1.91;
- middle noise: up to +5.3 hip / -3.4 ankle (s52) and +4.6 ankle (s60).

Why sigma alone cannot fix it:
- Reward does not register the edge. On full-horizon evals, total in-training reward changes by -38 to +21 (about +/-1%, with no
  consistent sign) from before the edge to after it. That holds in all six runs and in the control.
- foot_flatness moves -1.9 to +3.8 in the arms and +0.7 to +9.0 in the control. It prices tilt, and the edge pad stays flat.
- The reward has no fore-aft CoP term. CoP is priced only by the gate's max_sole_cop_fore_aft (D-D28, stance.toml).
- So the edge does not change reward, and a lower sigma only slows the drift onto it.

- Falls happen at steps 121-996.
- Both arms lose robustness at 0.16 as they train.
- At 3M, low noise falls about 8x as often as the control and middle noise about 14x.
- At their own sigma both arms survive:
  - low noise: sigma 0.070 -> 0.063, falls 0, 2 and 0 of 60;
  - middle noise: sigma 0.105 -> 0.089, falls 1, 0 and 0 of 60.
- At sigma 0.07 there is no cost at small perturbations:
  - the control falls 3/60 on these seeds (5/100 over its five seeds);
  - the 1M low-noise policies fall 1/100 at their own sigma of about 0.07.
- The CoP count at 0.16 is saturated (17-20/20 everywhere, statue 17/20). Only falls discriminate.
- Is middle noise a better balance? No.
  - It carries the edge from 0.4-0.8M.
  - It falls more than low noise at 0.16: [20, 20, 17] against [12, 14, 7] at 3M (complete separation, p = 0.05).
  - So it is worse than low noise on both axes, and worse than the control on both.

4. RECOMMENDATION
- PR: none.
  - Neither log_std_init -2.5 nor -2.0 should become a PR, and main keeps -1.5.
  - Neither meets the 1M screen's entry rule (<=3 CoP failures per seed at 3M, plus the clean bar):
    - low noise at 3M: CoP 16/19/22, clean 15-22/40;
    - middle noise at 3M: CoP 40/32/40, clean 0-8/40.
- Cost if adopted anyway:
  - matched-sigma 0.16 falls of 33/60 (low noise) and 57/60 (middle noise) at 3M, against the control's 4/60;
  - a change to the exploration noise that the D-D27 per-step penalty budget was calibrated against;
  - and the edge comes back by 1.4-3M anyway.
- Gate decision first: not needed.
  - No option changes any 2M or 3M outcome, because the CoP rail binds.
  - The tilt/corner question returns only once the edge is fixed. The diagnosis then argues for keeping the absolute bars: the
    residual failure is a policy lean of about 0.8x the statue's width roll, on 7 panel seeds.
  - Any statue-relative or asymmetry rail can be decided before the next screen's success criteria are predeclared.
- Next step (needs maintainer approval): a 3M scratch screen of a training-side signal for fore-aft pad CoP.
  - For example: a load-gated per-pad CoP-centring term on #620's per-foot pad CoP, priced the way foot_flatness is.
  - Train it at the production log_std_init -1.5, against an unchanged control taken to 3M.
  - Seeds 42, 52 and 60, judged at 1M, 2M and 3M with the same judge, panel and noise tests.
  - Calibrate it against the statue's CoP range (0.21-0.48) and the seed-44 edge props, as D-D27/D-D28 did.
- 12M Colab confirmation: none now for -2.5 or -2.0.
  For a future candidate:
  - Entry rule, at the 3M screen, all of:
    - CoP>0.8 <= 3/40 on each of seeds 42, 52 and 60 at 2M and 3M;
    - edge-pad telemetry slope over 2-3M <= 0, and no eval with a mean > 0.8;
    - matched-sigma 0.16 falls <= 10/60;
    - clean >= 37/40 on >= 2 of the 3 seeds at 3M, under the declared bars.
  - Seeds: 42, 52 and 60 (the edge formed on all three at every noise level tried), plus 48 and 56 (the original probe seeds).
    Five runs.
  - Config: production stance.toml except for the candidate change. Full 12M schedule: entropy reaches 0 at 7M, and the hop watch
    starts at 7M.
  - Judging: the screen judge at 3M, 6M, 9M and 12M/final, with the v2 panel on 3042-3081 and the own-sigma and matched-sigma 0.16
    tests. At final, also an out-of-sample panel on 7042-7081.
  - Success criteria, to predeclare:
    (a) v2 PASS at 12M on >= 4 of 5 seeds under the declared bars (2.0 deg, 4 mm, CoP 0.80): clean >= 37/40 (LCB >= 0.80),
        full horizon >= 0.95, hop-or-fall <= 1;
    (b) CoP>0.8 <= 3/40 on every seed at 6M, 9M and 12M;
    (c) in-training telemetry: no eval after 2M with the edge pad's mean gate CoP > 0.8, or half the episodes over 0.8;
    (d) matched-sigma 0.16 falls <= 10/100 pooled (control 6-10/100);
    (e) the out-of-sample panel passes on every seed that passes in sample.

CAVEATS
- Three seeds per arm, one run each. A one-sided exact rank-sum test at 3 vs 3 cannot go below p = 0.05.
- Adjacent PPO updates move a cell by up to 4 episodes. lownoise_s52 at stage1_1048576_steps is 26/40 with 10 CoP failures; one
  update later (the 1M screen's stage1_final) it is 30/40 with 6. stage1_3145728_steps and stage1_final agree within 1-2 episodes.
- The control was not run past 1M, so its CoP and matched-sigma comparators are at 1M.
- In-training evals are 10 deterministic episodes, so fractions move in steps of 0.1. lownoise s42's edge shows on the 40-episode
  panel (16/40 at 3M) but never meets the eval-level edge definition.
- Middle noise forming the edge faster than the control on s42 is a single seed. Over the three seeds its timing is
  indistinguishable from the control's.
