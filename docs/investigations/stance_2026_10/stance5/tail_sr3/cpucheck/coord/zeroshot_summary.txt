I dropped all 8 r9 checkpoints onto the SR3 plant unchanged and ran each one through the same harness on SR3 and on r9. Each checkpoint × plant cell got 40 deterministic and 40 stochastic episodes on reset seeds 3042-3081, and every r9/SR3 statue reference is 40 zero-action episodes on the same seeds. That is 32 policy panels and 4 statue panels; nothing was dropped (about 27 min at 2 processes). The harness runs each arm's own code, taken by git archive of 1af2d4d7 and cf80ecb1 and left unmodified. It uses the arm's stance_report._load_policy and run_panel(floor_truth=True) and the arm's stance_gate_v2 classify/evaluate functions, with a live statue reference rolled on each plant. Before any SR3 number I validated it on r9. Checkpoints 7 and 8 reproduce their panels.csv 'final' rows bit-for-bit (cmd, frac99, q3, falls, return_mean, plus every per-episode value in flags.csv).
