WHY
stance5's K3 diagnosis (report only, skeptic-checked) found s42's joint-noise falls driven by hip-pitch noise, mostly its left-right
differential part, with the tail habit not involved. It left two readings open: a seed-42 effect shared by both arms, or a band trade-off
(band runs less robust to left-right hip-pitch perturbation). These readouts are the ones that separate the two.

WHERE AND WHEN
- Runs: stance5_LTSB_s70 and stance5_LTSB_s80.
- Slots: 6,291,456 and final (12,001,280), each once its judge file exists and its "full" condition reproduces that judge file exactly.
- Reference values: the same readouts on stance5's band runs (s42 at 6,291,456, 7,340,032 and final; s52 and s60 finals) and, where k3diag measured them, the stance4 control finals.

WHAT IS READ (all on the judge's own joint-space noise loop, seeds 3042-3061, the run's judge sigma: the sidecar vector for seeds 70 and 80)
1. Falls of 20 under: the full noise (must reproduce the judge file); hip-pitch noise only; the full noise without hip pitch;
   the differential part of the actual hip-pitch noise only (d = (n_R - n_L)/2 as (d, -d)); its common part only (c = (n_R + n_L)/2 as (c, c)).
2. The deterministic left-right hip-pitch kick probe: +/-0.2 and +/-0.3 (right +, left -) for 0.2 s, falls of 20 (k3diag's definition).
3. The policy's direct response to a left-right hip-pitch angle difference (per rad), measured by nudging that difference in its input on the noisy states (k3diag verify_2's definition).
4. The policy's own action noise per actuator, exp(log_std), and the ratio of the judge sigma to it.
5. The heading-frame foot stagger sd under the full noise.
n = 20 per condition: differences of 2-3 falls are within binomial noise.
