COEF, PER BOUND (the rule is fixed now; the numbers are pins written before any training step)
- What the calibration measures (calib_tailscreen.py, a copy of P1's lambda_calib.py procedure; NO optimizer step, NO
  parameter change, nothing saved but coef.json):
  - For each start state (s42, s80): the run's training setup is built exactly as run_tailscreen.py builds it (rep seed 101).
    4 rollouts of 8,192 steps are collected with SB3's own collect_rollouts (stochastic policy, VecNormalize statistics
    updating in memory, GAE computed), with no update in between: the policy parameters and Adam state stay the checkpoint's
    (their digest is checked equal before and after).
  - For each rollout, one pass of 64 mini-batches of 128 (rollout_buffer.get(128), as train() draws them), at ratio 1 (the
    first epoch's situation), advantages normalised per mini-batch as SB3 2.9.0 train() does. Per mini-batch:
      G_pg = d(clipped surrogate) / d(action_net.bias[14]), the policy-gradient pull on tail_3's mean as a parameter;
      G_pen(b) = d(L_B at b, coef 1, on the mini-batch's actual rows) / d(action_net.bias[14]), for b = 0.90 and b = 0.60;
      share of rows with mu[:, 14] > b, for b = 0.90 and 0.60.
    Gradients come from torch.autograd.grad and are discarded; nothing is applied.
  - 256 mini-batches per state, 512 pooled.
- The fixed formula (P1's rule, scoped to one actuator and to the bound b):
      COEF_b = RMS(G_pg) / (2 x 0.11), RMS over the 512 pooled mini-batches (sqrt of the mean of G_pg^2, mean included),
      rounded to two significant figures by Python's format(x, ".2g").

  excess, 1.11 - 1.0 [measured, LAMBDA]); for a one-actuator mean-over-rows penalty it does not depend on b or on the batch
  size.
- STATED PLAINLY: neither term of the formula depends on b (G_pg is the surrogate's gradient; the reference excess is 0.11
  above whichever b), so the rule gives COEF_90 = COEF_60 by construction [inferred from the formula]. Both are written to
  coef.json separately and pinned separately; the scorer checks each against the rule. What differs between the bounds is the
  realised pull at the start, which coef.json records per state and per bound:

DECISION
Each bound is read against CONTROL on its own; BOUND90 and BOUND60 are never compared with each other for a verdict.

State readings, for each bound B in {BOUND90, BOUND60} and each state (the first line that applies gives the reading; every
line that applies is reported):
- PERSISTENCE (s42):
  1. RAIL: at least 1 B replicate is RAIL.
  2. PARKED: at least 2 B replicates are PARKED.
  3. OTHER: B's OTHER FLAG episodes at R1+R2 (out of 120; every episode counts, tail-up or not) exceed CONTROL's.
  4. NOT SEPARATED: at least 2 CONTROL replicates are CURED. (CONTROL is shared, so this line is the same for both bounds.)
  5. PASS: otherwise (then at least 2 B replicates are CURED).
- ONSET (s80):
  1. RAIL, 2. PARKED, 3. OTHER: as at s42.
  4. PASS: otherwise.
  Onset note (one per screen, from the shared CONTROL): INFORMATIVE if at least 2 of 3 CONTROL replicates had at least 1
  TAIL-UP FLAG at any of their 9 measured checkpoints; otherwise "onset not tested" (a PASS stands, with the note). The note
  states how many of the 9 checkpoints each s80 CONTROL replicate has measured; while any is missing for a replicate that is
  not LOST, the score is PROVISIONAL.
  The readings are monotone in CONTROL: a CONTROL replicate moving CURED -> PARKED -> RAIL, more CONTROL onset, or more CONTROL
  OTHER FLAG episodes never moves a bound's reading to an earlier line. Because the OTHER FLAG does not read the TAIL-UP FLAG,
  more CONTROL rail never lowers CONTROL's OTHER count (episode-level test, tools/tests/test_measure.py).

Bound verdict, for each B separately (the first line that applies):
1. Either state RAIL: B NOT ADOPTED (RAIL): it did not keep the rail away in deterministic play.
2. Either state PARKED: B NOT ADOPTED (PARKED): rail cleared but tail parked (ruling (3)); routed to the posture decision.
   No gate guard, no exemption.
3. Either state OTHER: B NOT ADOPTED (OTHER): more other-actuator flags or short episodes under B; the flagged episodes go to
   the maintainer as a harm / substitution review.
4. PERSISTENCE NOT SEPARATED: B NOT ADOPTED (NOT SEPARATED): the controls cured themselves; B is not credited.
5. Otherwise: B PASSED (with the onset note).

Screen outcome (the first line that applies; the scorer prints this line, both bound verdicts and every state reading):
A. BOTH PASSED. To the maintainer: both bounds as candidates for a from-scratch GPU round proposal (BRIEF section 4), each
   needing its own fixed criteria. The screen does not rank them (K = 3, one shared CONTROL); report-only comparisons (CMD,
   q3, returns, OTHER counts per arm) go beside. BOUND60's PASS is also posture evidence for the posture decision.
B. ONE PASSED (BOUND90 PASSED, BOUND60 NOT ADOPTED (<reason>); or the reverse). To the maintainer: the passed bound as the
   candidate for a from-scratch GPU round proposal with its own fixed criteria; the other bound's reason, routed as in C. If
   the passed bound is BOUND60, the result is also input to the posture decision (B with a tail-chain revision, C), since a
   0.60 bound is a posture intervention.
C. NO BOUND ADOPTED. To the maintainer, per bound, by reason:
   - RAIL: next candidate chosen by the maintainer: the deterministic-state variant (BRIEF 2A), a stronger dose (DEC1 D3(c)),
     or the posture decision (B / C).
   - PARKED: the posture decision (ruling (3)).
   - OTHER: the harm / substitution review of the flagged episodes.
   - NOT SEPARATED (then both bounds read it, unless an earlier line applied): more s42 replicates, or stop.
   A named combination is reported as such: BOUND90 RAIL with BOUND60 PARKED reads "the lower bound cleared the rail but not
   the posture"; both PARKED reads "neither bound moved deterministic play below the line".
In every case nothing trains on the outcome without the maintainer's approval, and the outcome changes no screen, verdict,
parent, criterion or stage config.

Report-only beside the outcome (never in a verdict): per state, whether the arm means of CMD at R1/R2 are ordered BOUND60 <=
BOUND90 <= CONTROL (dose ordering), with each arm's replicate classes.

Fails stay fails. No replicate is stopped, changed, extended or rerun because of a readout. Nothing in this screen is stopped
or changed on a readout: interim panels, telemetry and the 0.5M readings are recorded and shown, never acted on.
