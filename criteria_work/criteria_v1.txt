THE TEST (pre-registered; BS2's unchanged)
- An exact unpaired permutation test, one-sided (the phase arm lower), alpha 0.05. Statistic: the difference of arm
  means, control minus phase (with 4 per arm, equivalently the control arm's sum). p = the share of the C(8, 4) = 70
  ways to choose 4 of the 8 primaries as "control" whose statistic is at least the observed one (the observed split
  included; a tie counts against the reading). Exact rational arithmetic on the recorded values.
- Minimum attainable p = 1/70 = 0.0143. Attainable p <= 0.05: 1/70, 2/70, 3/70 (0.0429); 4/70 = 0.0571 is not. Size:
  3/70 = 0.0429 when the 8 primaries are exchangeable under no effect. If the phase input changes only the
  between-continuation spread (as BS2's arms differed: SD 0.166 against 0.036), P(REDUCED) with equal means is about
  0.06 (0.060-0.061 for control rises 0.29-0.58; 0.072 given the gate at a true rise of 0.29) [computed, WORK
  power_ps.out E]. Read a REDUCED with each arm's printed SD (BS2's S-3 lesson).
- Beside it, report only (never a line): the four paired differences control - phase (same seed), their exact
  one-sided sign-flip p (minimum 1/16 = 0.0625: it can never reach 0.05), and the as-counted p.
- THE FILL (missing never helps the hypothesis): a MISSING control primary counts as -infinity and a MISSING phase
  primary as +infinity (compared infinite part first). With 4 per arm any single MISSING continuation gives p >= 36/70,
  one missing in each arm >= 51/70, two missing controls >= 56/70 [<BS2>/criteria_work/power_bs2.out A; same rule], so
  REDUCED cannot be read once one is missing; the as-counted p is then printed (report only).

RUN HANDLING (as the runner and reader implement it; BS2's, unchanged; <BS2>/criteria.txt lines 349-387)

- STOPPED: CHECK passed, stop reason "collapse" at T < E. Not expected (it cannot fire in (S, E]: 16 seeded evaluations
  + 11 in the window give at most 8 patience observations < 10; <BS2>/criteria.txt lines 352-357). Kept as a guard:
  MISSING, filled.
- UNREADABLE: a COUNTED continuation whose 8 primary rows are not each present once with a finite value: MISSING,
  filled, cause printed.
- CRASH: the folder becomes runs/<name>_crashed<k>, kept and never read; the continuation reruns at once from its

  S + 131,072, or none) is not counted toward LOST and halts new starts until the coordinator has found the cause from
  run_meta's error and traceback alone and fixed it.

  coordinator writes it to lost.json with the cause and records it in RUNLOG). MISSING, filled. A full-read failure
  caused by read_bs.py itself is a tool defect: no line is read until it is fixed (the maintainer's word and a re-pin
