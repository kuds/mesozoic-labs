2. THE QUESTION AND WHY THIS ARM
==================================================================================================================
- The question (criteria THE QUESTION) is the task's item 1 verbatim in substance: does a critic that also sees the
  phase, and still sees the tail angle, reduce the climb? One-sided, lower.
- Why it is the root-cause test [inferred]: the why-probe's account has two parts: (1) a hidden phase that changes the
  true value (four terms gated on k >= 200; c_B1 -0.17 to -0.23 at matched posture [measured, phase_probe/analysis.txt
  M1 G at P2, C11a, C11E, C31a, C31E]); (2) a tail angle that tracks the phase (AUC 0.61-0.95) and so carries value
  for a phase-blind critic. Blinding (BS2) cut (2) at the channel. Giving the phase cuts (1)'s aliasing at the source:
  if the account is right the critic should stop paying for the tail even though it can still see it. If the climb
  continued with a phase-aware critic that has learned the phase (phase swing > 0) and still pays for the tail
  (SEC-MECH NOT SUPPORTED), the phase account would not be the whole story (the B-apparent height preference the
  verifier left unresolved, verify_why/NOTES.txt SUMMARY item 5, would gain weight).
- Alternatives considered and not chosen:
  (a) making the gated terms stationary (pay from step 0, or centre them): a reward change; the verifier's K5 found it
      removes only 87% (C11E) / 57% (P2) of the realised phase association [measured, verify_why/NOTES.txt SUMMARY
      item 6]; it changes the task, which a critic input does not.
  (b) phase for both actor and critic: changes the policy interface and lets the actor learn a phase-dependent action
      (a different habit could form); the maintainer's choice is critic-only (RUNLOG line 805).
  (c) a sham arm (an uninformative extra critic input) to separate "the phase" from "any new input": it would need a
      third arm (4 + 4 + 4 = 12 > the 8 the maintainer approved; about 4.3 h); left as a KNOWN LIMIT and to SEC-MECH and
      the phase swing.
  (d) reusing BS2's controls (the verifier's "cheapest decisive test", verify_why/NOTES.txt SUMMARY item 7): rejected
      for outcome-blindness (BS2's control outcomes are known: the gate would be decided in advance), for harness
      identity (PS's tools are a new build), and because the maintainer chose new controls.

4.1 The phase and the gate's off-by-one [measured]
  - shared/base_env.py step(): `self._step_count += 1` (line 1335) BEFORE `obs = self._get_obs()` (line 1349) and the
    reward (line 1352). reset() zeroes _step_count (line 1642). So the observation the policy acts on at its j-th
    decision (j = 0 after reset) is emitted with _step_count = j: k := j.
  - The four terms in the T. rex env gate on `self._step_count > self.stance_width_settle_steps` evaluated during the
    step's reward, i.e. on k + 1 > 200 (width: zeroed while `_step_count <= settle_steps`, line 1278; lean line 1315;
    stagger line 1339; CoP band line 1421); stance_width_settle_steps = 200 (code copy stance.toml line 212). So the
    step taken from the observation with count k pays them iff k >= 200, and settled = 1[k >= 200] is the gate exactly.
    The why-probe's verifier fixed the same off-by-one ("k=200 is the first gated step", verify_why/NOTES.txt
    18:09:59). The width target is set at _step_count == 200 (line 1270): during the step from k = 199.
  - Time limit: max_episode_steps 1000 (stance.toml line 400); SB3 bootstraps V on the terminal observation at
    truncation (TimeLimit.truncated; RUNLOG line 803); that observation has k = 1000 -> phi = (1, 1, 1). #655
    section 6.2 item 4: the time limit is not an aliasing source; no k/1000 feature is needed.
4.2 The features [inferred]
  - The settled flag 1[k >= 200] is the gate. The ramp min(k, 200)/200 (i) lets the critic represent the discounted
    anticipation of the gate before k = 200 (at gamma 0.98 the gated value enters over about 50 steps: the phase probe's
    primary phase control was R(k) = 0.98^max(0, 200 - k), PREREG.txt DEFINITIONS) and (ii) carries the time since
    reset, which the pop's lower G_rest in steps 0-50 depends on (M1 G_rest c_B0 -0.05 to -0.12 [measured,
    analysis.txt]; #655 section 1.3: the pop costs 14 of 18 points in steps 0-50). A [512, 256] MLP could form R(k)'s
    shape from the ramp and the flag; the builder added R(k) itself as a third feature ("discount"), which gives
    the first layer the value's own phase shape directly. I agree with that choice [inferred]: it costs nothing (a
    zero-initialised column) and matches the probe's primary phase control. All three equal 1 for k >= 200.
  - All features are bounded in [0, 1], need no normalisation and are mirror-invariant (time does not flip left and
    right), so the mirror map sends them to themselves with sign +1.
  - Not added: the action/filter history (#655 section 6.2 item 3): a second hidden variable this arm does not address
    (KNOWN LIMITS); it would need the previous actions in the critic's input, a different screen.
4.3 How the critic alone gets it: the widening precedent, applied to value_net.0 only [measured MAIN; inferred use]
  - MAIN widen_checkpoint.py (#655 line 864): every first-layer tensor whose in_features equals the parent width gets
    zero columns APPENDED (lines 451-511; _insert_zero_columns lines 451-456), and the Adam moments exp_avg /
    exp_avg_sq of those tensors are padded with zeros, matched to their parameters by order with shape assertions
    (lines 172, 512-560; "unpadded moments load but crash on the first optimizer step", line 37); the VecNormalize
    sidecar's obs_rms gains a reseeded slice (mean 0, var 1, the count carried: command_frame.pad_running_stats lines
    134-157); a self-verification proves the widened policy acts exactly as the parent (lines 773-830).
  - What fits a CRITIC-ONLY input: widen value_net.0 only (63 -> 63 + m columns, zero; its Adam moments padded); keep
    policy_net.0 at 63 inputs and feed the actor the first 63 normalised values (a slice in mlp_extractor.forward_actor,
    the same kind of class swap BS2's critic_blind arm used on the mlp_extractor instance), so the actor cannot see or
    learn from the phase (C1) and needs no zero columns at all; the critic is the parent's exactly at S (C2) and learns
    the phase from its gradient (C3).
  - What does NOT fit as it stands: the precedent's VecNormalize reseed (mean 0, var 1, count carried at 4,194,312
    [measured, parent_facts.out]) left UPDATING would hold the phase features' statistics almost fixed for the window
    but let them drift slowly; and reusing an always-zero command slot (60-62) with its own statistics would divide by
    sqrt(2.4e-11 + 1e-8) = about 1e-4 and clip at 10 [measured, parent_facts.out: var 2.3841812e-11 at S]. Hence C6:
    the features reach the value network unnormalised or through a FIXED affine map. The builder's form satisfies it
    (the slots reseeded to mean 0 / var 1 AND frozen: f / sqrt(1 + 1e-8) = f in float32). Of the three command slots
    only v_x is mirror-even in the stock map (v_y and yaw_rate flip sign); the builder's arm maps all three to
    themselves with sign +1 (bs_common.ARM_CHANGE), as C7 requires.
  - The builder's slot reuse versus my appended-column proposal [inferred]: both satisfy C1-C7. Slot reuse keeps every
    shape, the saved-file format and the optimizer state unchanged (the zeroed value_net.0 columns met only zero
    inputs, so their Adam moments are already zero and the critic at load is bitwise the parent's); the actor's
    first-layer columns on the slots also met only zeros and stay untouched because the actor's slots are held at
    0.0. The cost is that the actor's "never sees the phase" rests on the slot-zeroing hook, which C1 checks on every
    rollout and V5 checks for gradients.
  - #655 section 6.4 constraints (lines 873-877) apply to any later full run, not to this screen: width identity at
    every load, obs_rms carried and ret_rms reset at a stage transition (KNOWN LIMITS).
4.4 The contract C1-C7 (criteria WHAT IS TRAINED, MANIPULATION CHECK) states what is proved before launch (V1, V2,

  C5 the phase must be the env's own count, including across rollout boundaries (episodes span rollouts) and at the

  C6 scale (above).
  C7 every path that evaluates the critic: the probe's bank (bank_steps 199/599/599/599/400 [measured,
