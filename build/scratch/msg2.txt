Test the critic's episode phase: features, actor blindness, resume, switch off (screen, never merged)

test_critic_phase.py (numpy only, the test matrix): the features at
k = 0, 1, 199, 200 and 1000 (S 200, gamma 0.98, written out), that
`settled` says the next reward is gated in (k + 1 > S) and `discount`
weighs the first gated reward, the spec's and the switch's
refusals, the record's round trip, and that no committed stage turns
the option on.

test_critic_phase_ppo.py (the SB3 job; added to its step):
- the training env appends each env's own phase (0 after the VecEnv's
  reset, phase(horizon) on a truncated episode's terminal
  observation), normalises bitwise as the stock VecNormalize, keeps
  the phase in [0, 1] where running statistics would blow up, and
  saves a sidecar byte for byte the stock one's; the real T. rex
  stance env through create_vec_env: k == _step_count, `settled`
  turns 1 at k = 200, the first gated reward is step 201, and the
  horizon's truncation carries phase(1000) = (1, 1, 1);
- a time-limit truncation bootstraps on the terminal observation's
  phase (recorded predict_values inputs and the buffer's rewards);
- the actor is exactly blind to the phase (own, zero, 1e6, noise and
  NaN phases) on every path: get_distribution, forward,
  evaluate_actions, predict (66 and 63 inputs), the K3 panel's
  forward pass and MirrorKLPPO's mirrored KL term; its gradient never
  reaches the phase columns, the critic's does;
- a fresh model is the stock model of its seed plus zero phase
  columns (same first rollout); one throwaway update teaches the
  critic the phase and never the actor;
- MirrorKLPPO maps the phase columns to themselves, the statistics
  stay the env's width and symmetric;
- the policy probe gives each bank state its own phase;
- the checkpoint loads on the deploy path (load_sb3_checkpoint with
  the env's own 63-value observation) and acts as in training;
- save, load and resume through train_base keep the policy, the
  spec, the buffer, Adam and the statistics, and two resumes from one
  pair continue bitwise alike; every load whose critic reads another
  phase, or none, is refused, both ways and in both load modes;
- with the switch off, train_base builds SB3's MlpPolicy and
  RolloutBuffer and trains bitwise as a directly built PPO.
