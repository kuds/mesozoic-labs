Give the T. rex stance critic the episode phase behind [ppo] critic_phase (screen, never merged)

A training option, off by default: with [ppo] critic_phase = true the
PPO critic, and never the actor, also reads the episode phase of the
env's own step counter k (_step_count when the observation was made,
0 at reset): ramp min(k, S) / S, settled 1[k >= S] and discount
gamma ** max(0, S - k), S the env's stance_width_settle_steps (200 on
the T. rex stance) and gamma the stage's PPO discount (0.98), the
phase screen's definition (phase_tuple), the same float arithmetic.
No off-by-one: step() increments _step_count before it builds the
observation and the reward, so the observation with k is followed by
the reward of step k + 1, which the stance gates pay iff k + 1 > S.

A side input of the critic, not part of the observation: the policy
interface stays the env's 63 values (the model's observation_space,
the VecNormalize sidecar, every evaluation, probe, K3 panel, judge
and deploy load), and the three command slots stay free for the
walking stages.  environments/shared/critic_phase_ppo.py:
- CriticPhaseVecNormalize (the TRAINING env only) normalises as stock
  and appends the phase as three raw columns on reset, step and every
  terminal_observation (the counter of the episode's last step, so a
  time-limit truncation bootstraps on phase(1000)); never normalised
  (bounded in [0, 1]; running statistics would see `settled` constant
  0 first and blow it up to the clip); save() writes the stock
  VecNormalize pickle;
- CriticPhaseRolloutBuffer: PPO's buffer, 3 columns wider;
- CriticPhaseActorCriticPolicy: MlpPolicy whose actor's first layer
  reads the 63 observation columns alone (forward_actor slices them,
  so rollout, evaluate_actions, get_distribution, MirrorKLPPO's
  mirrored KL term, predict, the probe and the K3 panel are exactly
  independent of the phase) and whose critic's first layer reads 66.
  A fresh model is the stock MlpPolicy of its seed, bitwise, plus
  three zero critic columns (widened after SB3's initialisation, no
  random draw); predict() takes the env's own 63-value observation.
train_base reads the switch (popped like the symmetry keys, so
stage_config.json and hyperparameters_sha256 record it), builds the
training env and the model with it, records mesozoic_critic_phase on
the checkpoint, and refuses any load whose critic reads another
phase, or none, than the stage declares, both ways.  MirrorKLPPO maps
the phase columns to themselves (+1); the policy probe gives each bank
state its own phase (kept step + 1); the diagnostics' obs statistics
read the observation's columns only.
