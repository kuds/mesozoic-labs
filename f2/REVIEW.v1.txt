- E7. The code [measured, copy_8737cd3d environments/shared/mirror_kl_ppo.py; SB3 distributions.py, as RO cites]:
    The loss is policy_loss + ent_coef * entropy_loss + vf_coef * value_loss.
    log_std is one state-independent parameter per actuator (no use_sde).
    A diagonal Gaussian's entropy depends on log_std only.
    The symmetry KL uses sigma = exp(log_std).detach().clamp_min(0.01), so it passes no gradient to log_std.
    The saturation cost is on the clipped raw command and is flat beyond |a| = 1.

4.3 THE INFERRED ANSWER [inferred, by the readouts analyst from E1-E7; this review agrees on the same basis]
- The bonus does not drive these habits directly. Its gradient reaches log_std only, the same push on all 15
  actuators, and never the mean action that the ledger and M1 measure.
- It is not necessary for them. The s60 drive crossed the bound, and the s42 rail re-formed twice, with ent_coef
  exactly 0. r9's railed runs kept rails after 7M under the same schedule.
- A role through the noise level is not supported and not excluded. The railing actuator was the least noisy, and
  std shows no break at 7M. But the bonus is below 0.0007 from 6M on, so its removal is gradual.
- A role in the timing of the early onsets cannot be judged. Every first onset on both plants (r10 s42 3.67M, s80
  3.80M; r9 s42 3.15M, s80 4.33M) fell while the bonus was above 0, and every run shares one schedule. There is no
  contrast in these records.
- One caveat this review adds. s60's mean tail_3 command was already rising while the bonus was small but not 0
  (rollout mean +0.13 in 4.2-5.2M, +0.44 in 6.3-7.3M). So "the s60 drive formed at 0" holds for its bound crossings
  and over-bar episodes, not for the whole rise [measured, RO E5 bins].
- A mechanism that needs no entropy was offered as a reading, not a test: once the mean passes the bound, every
  sample on that side clips to the same command; the saturation cost stops growing at |a| = 1; and the clipped
  surrogate has little to pull the mean back [inferred, RO, from E7].

4.4 ITS LIMITS
- n = 3 here and 5 on r9, all on one schedule.
- GPU training is not bit-reproducible, so a seed fixes the start, not the run.
- The r9 records lack per-update std.
- No run contrasts ent_coef. So the timing question is open, not answered "no" [inferred, from 4.2].
