THE ONE PRE-REGISTERED STATISTIC (everything else is report only)
- A continuation is POSITIVE when M5 reads at least one ANY-FLAGGED evaluation (at least 5 of its 10 episodes over
  the bar, CRIT6 M5 (a)) with timesteps in (S, E]. Otherwise it is NEGATIVE.
- Per start, a of nA arm-A and b of nB arm-B continuations are POSITIVE (nA = nB = 6 when all are counted).
- Test (D5, D6): the exact stratified test over both starts (the exact Cochran-Mantel-Haenszel test, conditional on
  each start's total; with equal arms it equals a within-start permutation test). T = a(s42) + a(s80).
  P_UP = P(T >= t), P_DOWN = P(T <= t). Two-sided: 0.025 per side.
- The reading (the first line that applies):
  1. B LOWER (P_UP <= 0.025): "REMOVING THE REMAINING ENTROPY BONUS LOWERED THE ANY-FLAG RATE FROM THESE STATES".
  2. B HIGHER (P_DOWN <= 0.025): "REMOVING THE REMAINING ENTROPY BONUS RAISED THE ANY-FLAG RATE FROM THESE STATES".
  3. NO DIFFERENCE DETECTED: "NO LARGE EFFECT OF THE REMAINING BONUS DETECTED FROM THESE STATES; a modest effect and
     the early phase are not tested".
- Every line prints: each start's table (a of nA against b of nB), P_UP and P_DOWN, each start's one-sided Fisher p
  (report only), the INFORMATIVE note, the TABLE E power figures, and every STOPPED, LOST or VOID continuation.
- INFORMATIVE note: a start where fewer than half of arm A's counted continuations are POSITIVE (fewer than 3 of 6)
  prints "START NOT INFORMATIVE (arm A a of nA)". It is a note only. It never changes a p or a line, and no start
  is dropped.
- Report only, never tested: each continuation's first ANY-flagged step (censored at E), its number of flagged

RUN HANDLING (lean, for CPU runs; no Colab rule carries over: no relaunch window, no PRESENCE, no fetch)
- Fails stay fails. No continuation is stopped, changed, extended or rerun because of a readout.
- COUNTED: it reached E (stop reason "end"), C6 passed and R4 passed.
- STOPPED: one of the run's own early stops ended it before E (D14; in these windows only the collapse stop can act),
  with C6 to its last evaluation and R4 passed.
  POSITIVE and counted if an ANY-flagged evaluation came before the stop; otherwise censored: not in the test, printed.
- CRASH: the process died (exception, out of memory, killed) or the container or session restarted mid-way. The
  folder is moved to <name>_crashed<k> (never deleted, never read). The continuation reruns from its start pair with
  the same seed and arguments, and the rerun is the continuation. After a container restart (RUNLOG records several;
  scratch was kept, l458, l659) the wave runner is relaunched and treats every half-way folder as a CRASH.
- DETERMINISM decides what a rerun is. If V5 passed, a rerun is expected to be bit-identical: the crashed attempt's
  checkpoints are compared with the rerun's at the same steps (report only; the rerun governs). If V5 was not run or
  failed, a rerun is a new draw from the same start and seed, and the crashed attempt is reported beside.
- LOST: three crashes of its own (a container restart does not count), a continuation that cannot run, or one that
  reaches E with C6 failing. Declared in RUNLOG. Not in the test; printed with what its records show.
- VOID: R4 fails, or a run_meta shows another code digest, start sha256, arm config or seed. The screen stops and goes
  to the maintainer. Nothing is read until he decides.
- A refusal by preflight, before any optimizer step: the cause is fixed and it starts again under the same name.
- Order: the fixed order of BL item 8 (arms and starts interleaved, 3 at a time), so neither arm finishes first.

KNOWN LIMITS
- The starts are mid-rise. The screen tests the crossing of the bound, not the rise or the first 3.1M steps. A null
  leaves the early-phase question open, and it is not evidence against NEXT Option 3 [inferred, DS 4].
- A null rules out only a large effect: P(B LOWER) is 0.99 at A 0.9 against B 0.1, 0.90 at 0.25 and 0.43 at 0.5
  [computed, TE 4]. Arm A's rate at these states is unknown on r10: 0.75-0.95 is inferred from DD43's r9 control,
  which kept the original timing in 3 of 3 from a flag-free mid-rise start (DS 2).
- A positive is a causal effect at these two states, on CPU, with this harness. It is not a rate from scratch, not a
  GPU result and not a fix. The binary outcome reads "no" for any delay past 917,504 steps: prevention against delay
  is read only from the first-flag step, report only.
- Round context: 3 runs, and these 2 states come from 2 of them. Each original window is one realisation.
- Arm B removes on average about 0.0022 (s42) and 0.0014 (s80) of bonus [computed, DS 1.4]. The bonus acts on
  log_std only (REVIEW 4.3), so any effect runs through the noise level.
- Determinism holds, if at all, on this container's CPU and stack. A rebuilt container may differ [inferred, HS 5].
