D3  CONTINUATIONS PER ARM PER START (k)
    (a) RECOMMENDED k = 6 (24 continuations). P(B LOWER), two-sided at 0.025 per side, with A 0.9 against B 0.1 /
        0.25 / 0.5: 0.993 / 0.897 / 0.428 [computed, TE 4]. A single start can decide alone (5/6 against 0/6). About

    (b) k = 4 (16; NEXT's number): 0.932 / 0.700 / 0.220 [computed, TE 4]. About 17-21 CPU-hours, 6.5-8 h.

D4  SEEDS
    (a) RECOMMENDED: distinct seeds per arm. A: 1101, 1111, 1121, 1131, 1141, 1151; B: 1201, 1211, 1221, 1231, 1241,
        1251; the same lists at both starts. Why: the exact test assumes exchangeable continuations, and distinct
        seeds make that exact. Pairing buys no power for this yes/no outcome: the exact test grows conservative under
        positive pair correlation, and the paired sign test is about equal at k >= 4 [computed, POW 6b]. V3 and V5 give
        the determinism check that pairing would give for free. Spacing 10 keeps the 4 training envs' seeds
        (seed + rank) and the evaluation seeds (seed + 1000) apart, and away from M1's 3042-3061.
    (b) Paired seeds (seed j in both arms; DS D-8, DD43's pattern): the first rollout is bit-identical across arms, a
        free manipulation check. Keep the stratified test (valid, conservative) or name the paired sign test now.

D5  THE PRIMARY OUTCOME
    (a) RECOMMENDED: at least one ANY-flagged evaluation in (S, E], as M5 reads it. Why: it catches both habits
        (tail_3 + at s42, neck_pitch - at s80); it fired in both original windows with a 917,504-step margin; it is
        specific: 0 of 364 evaluations of the four non-alarm runs (r10 s60, r9 s52, s60, s70) were ANY-flagged
        [measured, DS 2].
    (b) An ANY ALARM in (S, E]: more specific, smaller margins (786,432 s42, 262,144 s80), so a lower arm-A rate.
    (c) A TAIL-flagged evaluation: never fired at s80 (its habit is on the neck), so the test would rest on s42.
    (d) (a) plus the first ANY-flagged step (censored at E) as a co-primary, at half the alpha each. It answers
        "when", but its spread across continuations is unmeasured, so it cannot be sized now. Under (a) it is printed,
        report only.

        at A 0.9 against B 0.1, 0.897 against 0.944 at B 0.25 [computed, TE 4; POW 3].
    (b) One-sided, B lower, at 0.05 (NEXT's choice).
