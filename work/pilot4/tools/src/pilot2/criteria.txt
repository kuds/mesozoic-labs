The one change, and why
  At the warm start, and only there (initialize_next_stage; never on a RESUME of the same stage, never on a fresh
  start, never on a root, and never for a stage without the key), every one of the 15 actuators' log_std is set to
  -1.5, std exp(-1.5) = 0.2231, after the parent's parameters are loaded and before the first update.
    The value: the T. rex stance stage's own starting noise is log_std_init = -1.5 (configs/tyrannosaurus_rex/
    anatomical/stance.toml, [ppo.policy_kwargs], decision D-D27), std 0.2231, which the maintainer was told as
    "0.22". log(0.22) = -1.514; the pilot uses -1.5 exactly, std 0.2231 (0.0031 above 0.22), so the noise is the
    stance's own starting noise and not a new number.
    Where: configs/tyrannosaurus_rex/anatomical/locomotion.toml, [curriculum] warm_start_log_std = -1.5 (pilot-only;
    production and pilot 1 have no such key and keep the parent's log_std as loaded). train_base applies it when the
    node is warm-started across its edge (the same rule as the stage-entry warm-up and ramp), in place, keeping the
    parent's Adam moments for log_std as every other parameter's are kept (train_base._apply_warm_start_log_std:
    the moments are gradient-scale statistics that a new value does not invalidate; the parent's first moment is at
    most 3.9e-4 against a second-moment root of 0.0009-0.0014 and decays by 0.9 per minibatch; clearing the moments
    would be a second change). It prints "Warm start: policy log_std set to -1.5 (std 0.223) on all 15 actuators
    (the parent's std: mean 0.095, min 0.018, max 0.164; Adam moments kept)" (to stdout, not through logging, so the
    line does not depend on how the Colab kernel's logging is set up; commit 8c0015ba) and stage_config.json records
    curriculum.warm_start_log_std = -1.5 and run.warm_start_log_std = -1.5 (a RESUME keeps the run record, like the
    edge), and the recipe digest run.hyperparameters_sha256 covers the key (sha256:ec9925f9..., pilot 1's
    af78e500...). Task and gate digests are unchanged (23d50a6d... / 02602cb0...).
    Why: pilot 1 never stepped and its noise stayed at about 0.1, under half the stance's starting noise, with four
    actuators at 0.018 in the parent and still 0.022-0.024 at 4,194,304. A policy whose training rollouts barely
    move off the parent's standing actions rarely produces the displacement the forward term pays, so the reward
    cannot pull it toward stepping. Resetting the noise to the stance's starting value restores the exploration the
    stance itself began with.
    What it does not change: the policy's mean action (deterministic behaviour at the warm start is the parent's,
    so the panel before any update would read the parent's STILL), the value network, the VecNormalize statistics
    (obs_rms carried, ret_rms reset, as in pilot 1), and the entropy schedule (warm-up ent_coef 0.02 for 300,000
    steps, then 0.005 -> 0.001 anchored at 4,000,000). The panel evaluation is deterministic (mean actions), so the
    noise acts only through what training learns.

What each outcome means relative to pilot 1 (the maintainer decides what follows; no decision table)
  Pilot 1 is the reference: same parent, plant, stage, seeds and judging, without the reset. It is a sequential
  comparison, not a randomized control: two pilots on the same seeds differ only in the key, but GPU training is not
  bit-reproducible, so a seed-for-seed difference is read as the change's effect only at the level of the outcome.
  WALKS  The noise reset was sufficient: from this stance, on today's walk reward, the policy starts an alternating
         gait by 4.2M once its noise is reset to the stance's starting noise, confirmed on video, where pilot 1 (same
         parent, seeds and judging, without the reset) stood still. Within the WALK label's known limits. It does
         not show that the reset was necessary, or that exploration was the only thing in the way: the comparison is
         sequential and not bit-reproducible (above). A possible next run is the full production node (8M) from
         the same parent with the same reset, judged by the same probe at 8M (both September hops strengthened from
         4M to 8M); it would again be a manual node from an uncertified parent, never published or reused.
  HOPS   The walk reward pays the hop from this stance once the reset widens exploration: the September two-footed
         hop reproduces from the arm L stance, and the gait the reset produces is the hop, where pilot 1 (without
         the reset) stood still. That points at the locomotion reward (the gait plan's section 5: gait-r1, GQ-5,
         GQ-15), not at the stance.
  FALLS  The policy falls in at least 5 of 40 deterministic episodes. The fall episodes' traces say which: falls
         while standing mean the wider noise broke the stance (training at std 0.22 drove the learned mean policy
         off the parent's balance; read against r9 s60's 6 of 20 joint-space noise falls in the screen's K3 test);
         falls while stepping mean stepping started and fails (the policy steps and cannot keep its balance).
  STILL  Again, as pilot 1: exploration at 0.22 is not enough, or the walk reward does not pay stepping from this
         stance by 4.2M; this pilot cannot tell the two apart. The report-only std and forward-term trajectories
         below say which is likelier (noise that stayed near 0.22 with a forward term still near pilot 1's points
         at the reward and the stance; noise that fell back toward 0.1 points at the policy gradient pulling the
         noise back to the standing optimum), but neither decides.
  MIXED  Report the per-episode labels; the maintainer reads them (a seed made MIXED by the video review says so).
  INCOMPLETE, PENDING_VIDEO_REVIEW  Not outcomes: judge or restart the missing seeds; finish the video review.

Known limits of this pilot (in addition to pilot 1's)
  - One change against pilot 1, three seeds, 4.2M: an outcome says what this reset does by 4.2M on this stance, not
    what any other noise level, a longer run or another stance would do.
  - The reset is to the stance's starting noise, not to the noise the September walkers explored with (their
    parents' std is not measured here); a STILL at 0.22 does not rule out a larger noise.
  - The value network and the Adam moments are the parent's; the reset adds noise to a policy whose critic has
    never seen it, so the first updates after the warm start may be noisier than pilot 1's. The warm-up's clip 0.02
    for 300,000 steps bounds how far the policy can move then, as in pilot 1.
  - The deterministic panel cannot see the noise itself; only what training made of it.
