The one change, and why
  At the warm start, and only there (initialize_next_stage; never on a RESUME of the same stage, never on a fresh
  start, never on a root, and never for a stage without the key), every one of the 15 actuators' log_std is set to
  -1.5, std exp(-1.5) = 0.2231, after the parent's parameters are loaded and before the first update. It stays the
  one change against pilot 1's RECIPE; since 2026-10-21 the parent differs from pilot 1's as well (decision 7a (i)).
    The value: the T. rex stance stage's own starting noise is log_std_init = -1.5 (configs/tyrannosaurus_rex/
    anatomical/stance.toml, [ppo.policy_kwargs], decision D-D27), std 0.2231, which the maintainer was told as
    "0.22". log(0.22) = -1.514; the pilot uses -1.5 exactly, std 0.2231 (0.0031 above 0.22), so the noise is the
    stance's own starting noise and not a new number.
    Where: configs/tyrannosaurus_rex/anatomical/locomotion.toml, [curriculum] warm_start_log_std = -1.5 (pilot-only;
    production and pilot 1 have no such key and keep the parent's log_std as loaded). train_base applies it when the
    node is warm-started across its edge (the same rule as the stage-entry warm-up and ramp), in place, keeping the
    parent's Adam moments for log_std as every other parameter's are kept (train_base._apply_warm_start_log_std:
    the moments are gradient-scale statistics that a new value does not invalidate; this parent's first moment is at
    most 2.83e-4 against a second-moment root of 0.00057-0.00091, step 681,229 (pilot 1's parent: 3.9e-4 against
    0.0009-0.0014), and decays by 0.9 per minibatch; clearing the moments would be a second change). It prints
    "Warm start: policy log_std set to -1.5 (std 0.223) on all 15 actuators (the parent's std: mean 0.064, min 0.009,
    max 0.125; Adam moments kept)" (this parent, measured 2026-10-21 through the branch's own load path without
    training; on pilot 1's parent the same code prints mean 0.095, min 0.018, max 0.164) (to stdout, not through
    logging, so the line does not depend on how the Colab kernel's logging is set up; commit 8c0015ba) and
    stage_config.json records curriculum.warm_start_log_std = -1.5 and run.warm_start_log_std = -1.5 (a RESUME keeps
    the run record, like the edge), and the recipe digest run.hyperparameters_sha256 covers the key
    (sha256:ec9925f9..., pilot 1's af78e500...). Task and gate digests are unchanged (23d50a6d... / 02602cb0...;
    re-confirmed for the new head, above).
    Why: pilot 1 never stepped and its noise stayed at about 0.1, under half the stance's starting noise, with four
    actuators at 0.018 in pilot 1's parent and still 0.022-0.024 at 4,194,304. A policy whose training rollouts
    barely move off the parent's standing actions rarely produces the displacement the forward term pays, so the
    reward cannot pull it toward stepping. Resetting the noise to the stance's starting value restores the
    exploration the stance itself began with. This parent's noise is narrower still (exp(log_std) mean 0.0645, min
    0.0089 at the knees, ankles 0.0142, hip pitch 0.0177, max 0.1255), so the same reset is a larger relative change
    on it (mean x3.46, knees x25.1; on pilot 1's parent x2.34 and x12.3). The value -1.5 is not changed for it.
    What it does not change: the policy's mean action (deterministic behaviour at the warm start is the parent's,
    so the panel before any update would read the parent's STILL), the value network, the VecNormalize statistics
    (obs_rms carried, ret_rms reset, as in pilot 1), and the entropy schedule (warm-up ent_coef 0.02 for 300,000
    steps, then 0.005 -> 0.001 anchored at 4,000,000). The panel evaluation is deterministic (mean actions), so the
    noise acts only through what training learns.

What each outcome means, and what it says relative to pilot 1 (the maintainer decides what follows; no decision
table; reworded 2026-10-21, the outcome definitions unchanged)
  An outcome says what this parent (stance3 Run 2's final) does with the noise reset by 4,194,304 steps, on today's
  walk reward, under pilot 1's stage, seeds and judging. Pilot 1 is no longer a same-parent reference: pilot 2
  differs from pilot 1 in two things, the parent and the noise reset, so a difference between the two pilots'
  outcomes cannot be attributed to the reset alone or to the parent alone. Pilot 1 stays the record of what its
  own parent (r9 arm L s60 6M) did without the reset, and the comparisons below are with that record. It is also a
  sequential comparison, not a randomized control: GPU training is not bit-reproducible, so even on the same seeds a
  difference is read only at the level of the outcome.
  WALKS  From this stance, on today's walk reward, the policy starts an alternating gait by 4.2M once its noise is
         reset to the stance's starting noise, confirmed on video. Within the WALK label's known limits. Pilot 1
         (another parent, without the reset) stood still; this pilot does not show which of the two differences
         mattered, that the reset was necessary, or that exploration was the only thing in the way (the confound,
         and the comparison is sequential and not bit-reproducible; above). A possible next run is the full
         production node (8M) from the same parent with the same reset, judged by the same probe at 8M (both
         September hops strengthened from 4M to 8M); it would again be a manual node from an uncertified parent,
         never published or reused.
  HOPS   The walk reward pays the hop from this stance with the reset: the September two-footed hop reproduces from
         stance3 Run 2's final (arm L+T), and the gait this parent produces with the reset is the hop. Pilot 1
         (another parent, without the reset) stood still; which difference let the hop appear is not separated here.
         That points at the locomotion reward (the gait plan's section 5: gait-r1, GQ-5, GQ-15), not at the stance.
  FALLS  The policy falls in at least 5 of 40 deterministic episodes. The fall episodes' traces say which: falls
         while standing mean that training from this parent with the reset, on today's walk reward, drove the
         learned mean policy off the parent's balance; whether the wider noise or the parent is the cause is not
