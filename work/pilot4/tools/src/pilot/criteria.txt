How it is judged
  The frozen 2026-09 gait-audit probe (docs/investigations/gait_2026_09/gait_probe.py, run on today's API by the
  scratch adapter tools/gait_probe_pilot.py, no measurement line changed), on the pilot branch's tree, at the
  periodic checkpoints 2,097,152, 3,145,728 and 4,194,304 of each seed with their VecNormalize sidecars: 40 episodes,
  deterministic actions, resets seeded 3042-3081 (panel scheme), gait window from 1 s (100 steps) after reset, every
  contact metric on the floor-truth channel (whole-leg floor normal force above 0.1 N on at least half the substeps;
  2-step debounce for touchdown and lift-off events; left and right touchdowns within 2 steps are one simultaneous
  event; duty and support fractions use the raw per-step contact, before the debounce).
  tools/pilot_judge.py runs it and binds the run: the pilot budget, the seed, initialize_next_stage, the plant, the
  tree's hyperparameter digest and commit, and the parent checkpoint's sha256 (run.parent_checkpoint_sha256 must
  equal sha256:70f3a4447794a01036c27bca5e0377a17db96d0e3d7bfb2d179e9ce94e6f353e). tools/pilot_score.py applies the
  rules below; tools/pilot_video.py renders the panel episodes the video review watches.

Episode labels (first that applies)
  FALL   the episode ends before 1,000 steps
  HOP    at least 0.5 touchdowns per second (all feet) and a simultaneous-touchdown fraction >= 0.5, at any speed
         (the probe's classify_biped hop rule; every September T. rex walker episode met it: 0.865-1.0)
  STILL  window displacement speed below 0.10 m/s
  WALK   all six:
           alternation index >= 0.6, lead swaps per stride >= 1.0, flight fraction <= 0.2 (the gait plan's section
           5.5 bar for a T. rex locomotion pilot; the September hops read 0-0.055, 0-0.05 and 0.32-0.45);
           jointlag_frac_hip_pitch in [0.35, 0.65] (the probe's own hip-pitch joint lag: the lag in [0, P) that best
           correlates the left and right hip pitch, as a fraction of the floor stride P; walk ~0.5, hop ~0);
           floor_stride_frequency_hz <= 5.0 (per foot, 1 over the mean time from one debounced touchdown to the next
           of the same foot, averaged over the two feet); and
           floor_duty_L <= 0.80 and floor_duty_R <= 0.80 (the duty guard: the fraction of the gait window that foot
           is on the floor, from the raw per-step contact).
  OTHER  anything else (staggered hop, scissor hop, short-lift shuffle-hop, staggered scoot, shuffle, irregular)
  A metric the probe cannot define (too few touchdowns, no oscillation to phase) fails the test that reads it.

  Why the guards, and not floor_gait_class == 'walk' (evidence: tools/fixtures/gait_cases.json, rows computed by the
  frozen probe's own analyze_episode through tools/make_gait_cases.py with three generators: the round-1
  integer-step traces, the round-2 verifier's continuous-time traces and the round-3 verifier's event traces;
  tools/test_pilot_score.py recomputes every row from the probe and asserts each label; the grids behind the duty
  cut are in finalwork/duty_grid_*.json and finalwork/duty_cut_summary.json):
    - The probe merges left and right touchdowns only within 2 steps (20 ms). A two-footed hop whose second foot lands
      30 ms or more after the first reads alternation 0.87-1.0 and simultaneous 0-0.08 in the cases; its lead swaps
      read ~2 per stride and its flight stays under 0.2. The section 5.5 contact bar passes it, and the probe's own
      floor_gait_class reads 'walk' for it, so floor_gait_class is no guard (reported, never decides).
    - The hip-lag band rejects such a hop only while the offset is under 35% of its cycle (at 7.7-8.3 Hz, 30-40 ms
      read 0.23-0.33, out; 50 ms reads 0.38-0.42, in; at 9.2 and 10 Hz 40 ms reads 0.36 and 0.40, in; a scissor hop
      with anti-phase hips reads 0.5, in).
    - The stride cap rejects those hops above 5 Hz while their feet lift for several control steps (the cases at
      7.7-10 Hz). The September T. rex hops cycled at 7.64-7.67 Hz (seed 42) and 9.21 Hz (seed 44): audit CSV means.
    - The duty guard closes the case the round-3 verifier found: a hop whose feet lift for only 1-2 control steps.
      The debounce fills a one-step lift, so the stride reads far below the real cadence (a 6-12 Hz hop read
      0.7-4.9 Hz), the inflated stride period can move the hip lag into the band, and the round-2 rule read WALK.
      Such a hop keeps its feet down most of the time. The cut is taken from the data:
        the round-3 verifier's false WALKs: 332 (262 of its 11,088 event-generator hops and 70 of 896 trace_ct hops,
          a true 6-12 Hz): both feet's duty 0.842-0.929;
        this round's extension (12,128 more hops, both generators: 13-25 Hz at duties 0.5-0.9, a dense grid at
          14-25 Hz and duties 0.74-0.82, and 6-12 Hz hops with one foot at duty 0.85-0.9 and the other at 0.6-0.8,
          none of which read WALK): 138 more distinct false WALKs, all at 13-22 Hz, duty 0.740-0.856; the lowest duty
          a false WALK reaches falls with its cadence (about 0.82 at
          14 Hz, 0.800 at 16 Hz, 0.789 at 17 Hz, 0.780 at 18 Hz, 0.762 at 19 Hz, 0.740 at 22 Hz), as the arithmetic of
          one-step lifts says (duty about 1 - 0.01 x cadence in Hz);
        walks: the audit's real walker (compsognathus 20260921_203149, 30 episodes) reads 0.489-0.624 (higher foot
          0.600-0.624); the probe-computed walks in the fixtures 0.59-0.75; the audit's real T. rex recovery
          shuffle-hop read a lowest-foot duty of 0.954 (audit CSV mean).
      At 0.80 the guard rejects all 332 of the verifier's cases and every one of these 470 false WALKs at a true
      15 Hz or below (not every hop: see "Known limits", the mixed-lift hop),
      and keeps the real walker 30/30. Lower cuts close more of the fast residual and cost slow walks (0.78: 28 of
      the 470 distinct false WALKs still WALK, all at 18-22 Hz; 0.75: 2, at 22 Hz); 0.85 (the first proposal) lets
      10 of the verifier's 332 through. 0.80 is chosen because: the residual then starts at 16 Hz, nearly twice the
      fastest T. rex hop the audit saw (9.2 Hz); it leaves room for a creeping walk with a foot down up to 80% of
      the time (at the pilot's expected 0.1-0.3 m/s a genuine walk may be that slow, and an OTHER cannot be turned
      into a WALK by the video review); and every episode it rejects that is not STILL is on the video review's
      watch list (foot duty > 0.8).
    - Walks pass: the real walker reads hip lag 0.385-0.500, stride frequency 3.97-4.10 Hz and duty 0.489-0.624 on
      all 30 episodes; 30/30 WALK. Probe-computed walks at 0.7, 1.0, 1.2 (with a 2f pelvis term), 1.6 (with joint
      noise) and 4.0 Hz, a walk timed 40/60, an event-generator 4 Hz walk at duty 0.62 and creeping 1 Hz walks at
      duty 0.70 and 0.75 are WALK.

  Known limits of the WALK label (what the tests show; nothing stronger is claimed)
    - Short-lift chatter at about 16 Hz and faster can still read WALK. A foot that lifts for one or two control steps
      at a high cadence keeps its duty near 1 - 0.01 x cadence; at 16-22 Hz that is 0.74-0.80, under the guard. Of
      the 470 distinct false WALKs of the round-2 rule found over 6-25 Hz, 61 still read WALK at the 0.80 cut, all at
      a true 16-22 Hz (fixture rows ev_chatter_16Hz_d0.80 and ct_chatter_22Hz_d0.74 read WALK; tested). Faster than
      22 Hz nothing was found (25 Hz tested at duty 0.5-0.9), and the arithmetic does not exclude it. Every one of
      these short-lift false WALKs, at any cadence, has a mean floor swing of 0.020-0.021 s on both feet (two
      control steps), against
      0.092-0.126 s for the real walker and at least 0.045 s for every probe-computed walk that reads WALK (fixtures
      and this round's 297-walk grid; the shortest is a 4.5 Hz walk at duty 0.80); pilot_score.py flags such
      episodes (short_swing, report only) in the video review's watch list. A minimum-swing guard would separate every
      case found, but it is not part of this fixed rule.
    - The 5 Hz cap is on the MEASURED stride frequency (debounced touchdowns), not the true cadence: a hop the probe
      measures at 5 Hz or below is inside it however fast it really cycles. A hop that mixes lift lengths, every
      other lift one control step (filled by the debounce) and the rest 4-6 steps, legs in opposite phase, at a true
      6-12 Hz, is measured at 2.7-4.5 Hz and reads WALK at duty 0.68-0.80 with a mean swing of 0.04-0.06 s, so it
      is NOT starred short_swing (42 such cases, round-4 verifier's my_cases.py). The video review is the check.
    - A staggered or scissor hop at 5 Hz or below that passes the hip-lag band, with normal swings, reads WALK
      (tested at 4.5 Hz: a scissor hop with feet landing 30 ms apart, lag 0.5, and a staggered hop offset 80 ms, lag
      0.36; its feet are down 70% of the time, so the duty guard does not reject it). The audit's closest T. rex case,
      the recovery stance's two-footed shuffle-hop at 3.07 Hz, was mostly synchronous (simultaneous fraction 0.59,
      lead swaps 0.13 per stride) and its feet were down 95% of the time (audit CSV means).
    - A walk can read OTHER (all conservative against WALK, and the video review cannot undo them):
        a creeping walk with a foot down more than 80% of the time (tested: a 1 Hz walk at duty 0.85 reads OTHER,
          at 0.75 WALK);
        a genuine alternating gait faster than 5 Hz (tested: 5.5 Hz reads OTHER; the cap is absolute, not scaled by
          leg length; a T. rex walk dynamically similar to the compsognathus walker would cycle near
          4.05 x sqrt(0.244 / 0.877) = 2.1 Hz, scaling arithmetic, not a test);
        an asymmetric walk: timed 30/70 it reads hip lag 0.30 and is OTHER (tested); 40/60 reads 0.40 and is WALK
          (tested); the real walker's lowest lag, 0.385, is 0.035 inside the band's edge;
        a walk whose feet re-touch within a stance: the stride frequency doubles, alternation falls to 0.50-0.51 and
          the hip lag to 0.94-0.97, so it reads OTHER at any cadence (tested by the round-3 verifier at 1.5, 2.5 and
          3 Hz with a 30 ms lift mid-stance);
        any undefined metric.
  Report only: per checkpoint, the OTHER episodes that pass the contact bar, by the guards that reject them
  (hip_lag, stride, duty and their combinations: a staggered or scissor hop fails the band or the cap, a short-lift
  shuffle-hop the duty guard), and the WALK episodes with a short swing.

Video review (REQUIRED for WALKS; decision 3)
  A seed's WALKS counts only when the maintainer has watched its videos and agrees they show a walk. Without the
  review a WALKS seed is "pending", and the pilot outcome is PENDING_VIDEO_REVIEW whenever the review could change
  it. Not required for HOPS, FALLS, STILL or MIXED.
  Which videos, for each launched seed:
    (a) The notebook's replay of the final model. The manual cell plays two videos at the end of its output, this one
        first ("Playing: tyrannosaurus_rex_anatomical_ppo_stage2_final.mp4"), then the selected one; it saves both
        to Drive:
          MyDrive/mesozoic-labs/logs/tyrannosaurus_rex/anatomical/ppo/pilot_walk_s<seed>/03_locomotion/replays/
          tyrannosaurus_rex_anatomical_ppo_stage2_final.mp4   (pilot_walk_s<seed>_b for a restarted seed)
        It shows stage2_final, the policy at the end of training: for an uninterrupted run the 4,194,304 checkpoint
        plus the last PPO update (SB3 saves the periodic checkpoint during the last rollout, before that update). One
        deterministic 1,000-step episode (10 s, 100 frames per second), reset seed 2002 + the training seed (2044,
        2054, 2062), the default camera. The ..._stage2_selected.mp4 beside it is the in-training best checkpoint,
        which may be an earlier step: context only. The notebook's section 8 cell "Replay the chain's videos" lists
        only certified nodes and never shows the pilot node (the Colab steps do not run it). A seed with no notebook
        replay is reviewed on (b) alone: a resumed seed (the RESUME path does not evaluate), or a seed whose runtime
        was lost after its 4,194,304 checkpoint but before the final save (its outcome is read at 4,194,304 as
        usual; no rerun).
    (b) The panel episodes pilot_score.py lists under "VIDEO REVIEW REQUIRED", at the checkpoint the seed's outcome
        is read at (4,194,304, or a collapse-stopped seed's newest periodic checkpoint): every WALK-labelled episode,
        and every episode that is not STILL with a foot's floor duty above 0.8. These are the probe's own episodes
        (reset seed 3042 + episode), rendered by tools/pilot_video.py from the judged checkpoint pair on the judging
        tree. The tool re-rolls the panel through the probe's own rollout and calls a video the probe's episode only
        if every metric of the re-rolled episode equals probe.json's; it writes a real-time and a quarter-speed mp4,
        each frame marked with the time and two boxes, L and R, filled while that foot is on the floor.
  What to look for:
    A walk (agree): the feet take turns. One foot stays planted and carries the body while the other lifts clearly
      off the floor, swings forward and lands ahead; then they swap. In the boxes, L and R are each empty for a
      visible stretch of every step, never both at once, and the empty box alternates L, R, L, R. The body moves
      forward over the planted foot.
    Not a walk (disagree): both feet leave the floor together or land together, even when one lands a moment after
      the other (a two-footed hop or skip: both boxes empty together, or both refill within a frame or two); the feet
      hardly leave the floor, the boxes blinking off for a frame or two while the body slides or bounces forward (a
      shuffle or short-lift shuffle-hop: watch the quarter-speed copy; a starred episode in the watch list has this
      signature); one foot always ahead and the other only catching up (a scoot); one leg doing all the stepping.
  The verdict per seed: "agree" only if the final replay (a), where the seed has one, shows a walk and at most (WALK
  count - 30) of the seed's WALK-labelled panel episodes (b) are not walks, so that at least 30 of its 40 episodes
  remain walks on video; otherwise "disagree". Record it in a JSON file, {"42": "agree", "52": "disagree", ...} (a
  note may be added: {"42": {"verdict": "agree", "note": "..."}}), and pass it to pilot_score.py --video-review. A
  disagree makes the seed MIXED ("WALKS by the rule, not on video"). The duty > 0.8 episodes in (b) are watched for
  what the policy does; they change no verdict.

Seed outcome at a checkpoint (40 episodes; first that applies)
  WALKS  WALK >= 30 and FALL <= 4
  HOPS   HOP >= 20
  FALLS  FALL >= 5
  STILL  STILL >= 20
  MIXED  otherwise
  (A seed of staggered or scissor hops cycling above 5 Hz with normal swings reads OTHER per episode, so MIXED; so does
  a short-lift shuffle-hop whose feet are down more than 80% of the time. The report-only counts name which guard
  rejected them. For what can still read WALK see "Known limits of the WALK label".)

A seed's outcome, and which judgements count
  The seed outcome at 4,194,304, then the video review if it is WALKS. Exceptions:
  - Collapse-stopped seed: the collapse backstop (EvalCollapseEarlyStop, the only callback that ends the manual node
    early; at the pilot's cadence it cannot stop a run before step 3,801,088) leaves a final model short of
    4,194,304 and no 4,194,304 checkpoint. Its outcome is the seed outcome at its newest periodic checkpoint (judged
    the same way), reported as collapse-stopped, and it counts in the tally.
  - Off-schedule run: a run resumed (the notebook's RESUME cell) from a checkpoint before ramp_timesteps (2,000,000;
    every periodic checkpoint below 2,097,152) trained part of the forward-weight ramp at the full weight and skipped
    any remaining warm-up (a resume attaches no stage-entry shaping). pilot_judge.py refuses it; judged anyway with
    --report-off-schedule, it is written to OUT/off_schedule/<the run copy's directory name>/s<seed>/<steps>/ (never
    to OUT/s<seed>/<steps>), and pilot_score.py lists it as reported and never scores it. The Colab steps restart such
    a seed from the parent in a fresh RUN_ID instead; the fresh run is the seed's run and is judged into
    OUT/s<seed>/<steps> as usual.
  - Missing: any launched seed with no scored outcome by the rules above (a lost runtime not restarted, an unjudged
    checkpoint, a run that fails a binding check). Its judged checkpoints are reported, not scored.

Pilot outcome
  pilot_score.py --seeds 42,52,60. The outcome shared by at least 2 of the launched seeds; MIXED when every launched
  seed has an outcome and no two share one; INCOMPLETE (no outcome yet) when no two share one and a missing seed could
  still decide it; PENDING_VIDEO_REVIEW (never WALKS) while a WALKS seed's review is pending and its verdict could
  change the outcome. 2,097,152 and 3,145,728 are scored and reported as the trajectory but do not decide.

What each outcome means (no decision table; the maintainer decides what follows)
  WALKS  Today's walk reward, from this stance on this plant, starts an alternating gait by 4.2M, confirmed on video,
         which neither September walker did (both hopped; their in-training airborne duty already exceeded their
         single-support duty by 4.2M, below). A possible next run: the full production locomotion node (8M) from the
         same parent, judged by the same probe at 8M, since both September hops strengthened between 4M and 8M. It
         would again be a manual node from an uncertified parent, never published or reused.
  HOPS   The September hop reproduces from a new stance: the walk reward pays the hop whatever the stance, which points
         at the locomotion reward (the gait plan's section 5: gait-r1, GQ-5 and GQ-15), not at the stance. No stance
         experiment follows from it.
  FALLS  The policy falls in at least 5 of 40 deterministic episodes. The fall episodes' traces say whether it fell
         while stepping or while standing; falls while standing would point back at the stance's robustness (the
         screen's K3 noise test), the one outcome here that can. The parent is the r9 checkpoint behind r9's K3
         failure (6 of 20 joint-space noise falls): read FALLS against that.
  STILL  The policy is not moving by 4.2M (the September walkers moved about 0.19 and 0.29 m/s by 4.2M, estimated
         as their certified speed times their forward-reward term at 4.2M over that at 8M). The pilot was too short to
         say anything about the gait.
  MIXED  Report the per-episode labels; the maintainer reads them (a seed made MIXED by the video review says so).
  INCOMPLETE, PENDING_VIDEO_REVIEW  Not outcomes: judge or restart the missing seeds; finish the video review.

The locomotion gate (recorded, not a pilot criterion)
  The manual cell judges the node's handoff on reward_and_length/v1 (mean reward >= 100, mean length >= 750, mean
  speed >= 1.0 m/s) and records the verdict without enforcing it. Expected: FAIL on speed (the September walkers
  reached 1.05 and 1.57 m/s only by 8M). A PASS changes nothing above: the gate reads no foot contact.

Report only (never part of the outcome)
  Median speed, alternation, simultaneous fraction, lead swaps, flight, hip-pitch lag, single support, stride
  frequency, foot duty, foot swing and stride length over the leg length L = 0.877 m; the probe's own gait classes;
  the OTHER episodes that pass the contact bar, by the guards that reject them; the short-swing WALK episodes;
  left-lead fraction (the September hops kept the left foot ahead 99.9% of the time); falls by step; the in-training
  telemetry in gate_progress.npz (touch: airborne duty, two-foot duty, the forward-reward term, whole-foot CoP)
  beside the September walkers' at the same steps; the gate verdict.
