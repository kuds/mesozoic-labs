The question, and what an answer can and cannot claim
  Question: does a visible heading (the reserved command inputs obs[60:63] carrying a fixed straight command whose
  desired heading is the spawn heading) plus a heading price make pilot 3's walking gait straight in a 2,000,000-step
  fine-tune of each seed's own 8M checkpoint, without losing the gait?
  The claim a STRAIGHT WALKS pilot outcome supports: on at least 2 of the 3 seeds the fine-tuned walker is WALKS by
  pilot 3's unchanged rule and video, and STRAIGHT by the three pre-registered parts below (end heading, weave,
  recovery from a 15-degree offset that only the command inputs show), all on the pilot-4 env's panels.
  What it cannot show:
  - A rate. Three seeds give existence, not a rate [P3 L901-903]; the existence reading is pre-registered for that.
  - Anything from scratch. It is a fine-tune of walkers that already walk, from a parent trained without the command
    or the price; whether the stance parent with the command and price from the start would walk straight is open.
  - Which change did it. The command, the price and the prey on the spawn line are applied together; there is no
    ablation arm.
  - The beacon route. The prey is not held ahead (the beacon of decision (20) is not built); the heading reaches the
    policy through obs[60:63] (obs[62] linear in the error within 11.46 deg and +-0.5, the sign only, beyond it;
    obs[60] the cosine, the size only) and, before the prey is passed, through the prey block. Nor the
    follow_direction behaviour route: its tracking reward, turns and 2,500-step episodes are not used, and a
    pilot-4 checkpoint cannot be prepared for it today [measured as recorded: hybrid check screen lane finding 17,
    prepare_behavior_checkpoint refuses a nonzero command-slice mean].
  - Turning commands, other commanded speeds, a commanded heading other than the spawn heading, terrain.
  - Straightness in pilot 3's env (report only); certification. Nothing trained is certified, published or reused;
    main and its digests are unchanged [P3 L1424-1425].

What each outcome means (the maintainer decides what follows)
  An outcome says what a 2,000,000-step fine-tune of these three walkers does under R6 with the live straight command,
  the prey on the spawn line and the price 0.2, judged in that env. Pilot 3 differs in parent, entry, command, prey
  line, price and budget, so no difference from pilot 3 is attributed to any one of them.
  STRAIGHT WALKS  A visible straight command plus the price straightens the gait without losing it on 2 or 3 seeds,
         including a return from a 15-degree offset that only the command inputs showed (the prey turned with the
         walker), so the walkers use the command as heading feedback. Next (the
         maintainer's): a route that keeps the command (turning commands, follow_direction, which needs a live-parent
         path first [hybrid screen lane finding 17]), or the same recipe from the stance parent.
  STRAIGHT NO RECOVERY  Straight from a straight start, but a return after an offset is not shown (it failed, or could
         not be read because the walker had not passed the prey by 6.5 s; printed which). The gait straightened, but
         the recovery test does not show that the command is used as feedback. A gait whose own drift happens to be
         small could pass (a) and (b) without the command [inferred]; the heading-free hand-built walks do not (yaw
         p95 16.7, D p95 17.9 deg, the evidence table). Next: offsets during training (random headings or turns)
         before any turning route.
  CURVED WALKS  The gait survived but the fine-tune did not straighten it to the bars by 2M. The STRAIGHT parts, the
         price per episode, the obs[62] saturation share and the trajectory checkpoints say whether it is moving
         (budget), stuck (the price too weak against R6's other terms, or the walker not using obs[62] and obs[60]
         beyond obs[62]'s linear range), or curving after the prey pass (the post-pass regime) [inferred]; none
         decides.
  NOT WALKS  The fine-tune lost the gait (FALLS, HOPS, STILL or MIXED, as labelled). Candidates: the restarted [ppo]
         schedules (learning rate and entropy 5 times pilot 3's end values), the fresh ret_rms, the price; the
         trajectory checkpoints say when [inferred].
  MIXED  The maintainer reads the seeds. INCOMPLETE, PENDING_REVIEW: not outcomes; finish the runs and reviews.

Known limits of this pilot
  - Three seeds give existence, not a rate; one recipe, one price, one budget; the 2-of-3 outcome is pilot 3's rule,
    kept for comparability [P3 L901-903].
  - A fine-tune, not from scratch: each walker brings its 8M gait, its curve and (s42) its limp; the command columns
    start at zero and learn only in 2M steps.
  - The [ppo] schedules restart at learning rate 5e-5 and entropy 0.005, five times pilot 3's end values, on a gait
    tuned at 1e-5 and 0.001 (Q17).
  - The price was screened offline on recorded trajectories rotated, not on simulated straight walks: what PPO does
    with it is unmeasured [measured as recorded: hybrid screen lane problem 3]. It prices heading, not course, so a
    constant crab angle stays nearly free (s42 crabs about 3.7 deg) [measured as recorded: veer screen candidate H].
  - The STRAIGHT rule reads heading, not course: a walker straight in heading can crab off the commanded line (the
    course numbers are report only).
  - (a) and (b) still admit a heading zig-zag of period 0.5-1.5 s up to 16.5-18.5 deg amplitude (the W0 cap, less
    the stride's own oscillation), of 2-2.5 s up to 6-7.5 deg, of 3 s up to 13.5 deg and of 4-5 s up to 8.5-12 deg
    (a lateral path amplitude of about 0.06-0.42 m at 2.5 m/s), and a turn and back of up to 18.5 deg over 0.25-0.5 s
    each way, 15 deg over 1 s and 12.5-13 deg over 2-3 s [measured: postfix/text/out/zigzag_w0.txt, synthetic traces
    through (a), D and W0, 40 random phases]. A constant crab of any angle passes (a), (b) and (c) [inferred: every
    part reads heading]. Only the straightness look sees these (its criterion: Q27).

    passes it (a synthetic 1.01-s, 20-deg zig-zag after the nudge reads median r 0.2 deg [measured as recorded:
    SP/work/pilot4/tools/out/review_validity/logs/adversarial.txt]); only the look's recovery heading curves see it

    cosine (the size, unsigned), so the recovery offset (15 deg) starts outside the linear range. The price
