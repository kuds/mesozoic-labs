RUN HANDLING (lean, for CPU runs; no Colab rule carries over: no relaunch window, no PRESENCE, no fetch)
- Fails stay fails. No continuation is stopped, changed, extended or rerun because of an outcome.
- Outcome-blind while running: until all 24 are decided, nothing reads a continuation's ledger, M6 arrays, logs of
  its evaluations or end state (no M5, no R2, no M1). The runner reads only exit codes, run_meta and CHECK. The
  process logs <ECS>/logs/<name>.attempt<k>.log print every evaluation's return and are such logs: a crash's cause
  is read from run_meta's error and traceback, and only a refused attempt's log (exit 2: it never reaches learn())
  may be read for its reason.
- COUNTED: stop reason "end" at E, CHECK passed.
- STOPPED: the run's collapse stop (D8; in these windows the only early stop that can act, and only from a window's
  10th evaluation, its patience being 10 [measured, stance.toml l1041]) ended it at T < E, CHECK passed. POSITIVE if
  an ANY-flagged evaluation came in (S, T]; otherwise censored.
- CRASH: the process died (an exception, out of memory, killed) or a container restart left it half-way. The folder
  is renamed <name>_crashed<k>, kept and never read. The continuation reruns from its start pair with the same seed
  and arguments, and the rerun is the continuation. A container restart does not count toward LOST. An exception
  raised by the training itself is a crash too, as in DD43 (criteria_tailscreen_final.txt l338); the fill keeps a
  LOST continuation from helping a line. A crash before the continuation's first evaluation in the window (the
  last step run_meta records, its heartbeat or last_step, below S + 131,072, or none) is not counted toward LOST:
  no evaluation of the window exists, so nothing of the outcome is known. The runner starts no new continuation;
  the cause is found from run_meta's error and traceback alone and fixed (a fix that changes a pinned file or this
  text needs the maintainer's word and a re-pin), then it starts again under its name.
- LOST: three crashes of its own, C6 (as R1 counts it) failing up to T, or R1 unable to run on its ledger (the
  ledger missing or unreadable, M5's INPUT ERROR, or M5 not finishing within 900 s twice). The runner (crashes) or

  against each line. Any other failure of R1 (M5 exiting otherwise, or M5's output failing the reader's own checks)
  is a tool defect: no line is read; it is fixed as a VOID's cause is (the maintainer's word and a re-pin, asked
  before the scorer runs), and then every COUNTED or STOPPED continuation is read again.
- VOID: CHECK fails, including a stop reason other than "end" or "collapse" and a run whose own post-run code-seal or

KNOWN LIMITS
- The starts are mid-rise. The screen tests the crossing of the bound, not the rise or the first 3.1M steps, and s80's
  start comes after its first neck over-bar episode. A null leaves the early-phase question open, and it is not
  evidence against NEXT Option 3 [inferred, DS 4].
- A null does not rule out an effect. If arm A's rate is near 0.9, a large effect would probably be detected: P(B
  LOWER) is 0.99 at B 0.1, 0.90 at 0.25 and 0.43 at 0.5; at arm A 0.6 it is 0.64, 0.29 and 0.04 [computed, TE 4; CHK
  2]. Arm A's rate on r10 is unknown. 0.75-0.95 is inferred from DD43's r9 CONTROL, 3 of 3 from a flag-free mid-rise
  start, counted on M1 tail flags in 20-episode traces on another plant, not on the ledger's ANY flag; 3 of 3 has a
  95% interval of [0.29, 1] [computed, CHK 4].
- "The bonus protects" (B HIGHER) can show only if arm A fires well below 0.75: at A 0.9 even B 1.0 reads B HIGHER
  with probability 0.004, at A 0.75 0.16 [computed, CHK 2]. A null says nothing about a protective effect at the
  expected rates.
- The outcome is binary at E: a delay of the first flag shorter than about 917,504 steps leaves both arms POSITIVE,
  and a delay past E reads as prevention. No line tells them apart.
- Only ent_coef is varied. log_std_init (its analogue is add-on S, off), symmetry_sigma_floor and
  action_saturation_weight are untested here (NEXT 0.3 gives the reasons). s60's late habit is outside the core
  (add-on C, off).
