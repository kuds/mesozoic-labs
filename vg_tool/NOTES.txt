WHAT THE TOOL MEASURES AND HOW (step 1: worked out from the PS tool code, never from PS/runs)
==================================================================================================================
Where the pairs are [measured, code]: run_bs.py makes runs/<arm>_<seed>/01_stance/ (stage_dir), copies the start
  pair there as models/stage1_2097152_steps.zip / stage1_vecnormalize_2097152_steps.pkl (copy_inputs, lines 861-878),
  and train_base's checkpoint callback writes models/stage1_<s>_steps.zip / stage1_vecnormalize_<s>_steps.pkl at
  s = 2,359,296 ... 3,407,872 (bs_common.SAVED_STEPS, periodic_names) and the final pair models/stage1_final.zip /
  stage1_final_vecnorm.pkl at E (bs_common.FINAL_ZIP / FINAL_PKL: E = 3,538,944 is not on the 262,144 save grid,
  bs_common lines 96-99); read_bs.saved_pairs (lines 1085-1096) reads exactly these.  Confirmed on BS2's finished
  runs (same harness family): runs/control_2501/01_stance/models holds those 13 files [measured, ls].
  The COUNTED list and its STATUS come from score_bs.py's score.json: status "final" / "PROVISIONAL" / "incomplete",
  classes [{name, arm, seed, cls COUNTED|STOPPED|LOST|UNDECIDED, T, cause}] in the queue order, root (score_bs.py
  lines 1054-1080, 1360-1391) [measured, code].
How read_bs loads a pair and feeds the phase [measured, code]: read_bs.Net (load_sb3_checkpoint: the model and the
  VecNormalize frozen); for critic_phase read_bs.phase_prepare (at S: value_net.0.weight[:, 60:63] := 0 and the
  statistics' slots reseeded to (0, 1); at later pairs the slots must already be (0, 1), else InputError; then the
  actor's mask class); read_bs.with_phase writes phi(k) (bs_hooks.phase_matrix) into slots 60-62; critic_slope casts
  to float32 and calls norm.normalize_obs, then policy.predict_values: the "effective" critic of critic form
  "phase+raw+settled" (raw = slots zero, settled = (1, 1, 1)).  The on-policy states come from read_bs.onpolicy_states:
  the stage config, create_vec_env with 4 envs seeded seed + rank, VecNormalize.load frozen with norm_reward True,
  load_sb3_model with the stage's [ppo] kwargs, enable_mirror_kl, vn / torch / numpy seeded, one collect_rollouts.
What vg_sigma.py reuses (imported read only from PS/tools by sys.path insertion; -B, no bytecode written [measured:
  no __pycache__ under PS/tools or vg_tool]): read_bs.Net, read_bs.phase_prepare, read_bs.with_phase (the phase
  feeding), bs_hooks.critic_blind_class (BS2's blinded critic, --pair --form blind only), bs_hooks.phase_slots_frozen,
  bs_common's paths, pins and names.  It does NOT call read_bs.setup_simulation or loop_markers (they write under
  PS/tmp and PS/reads/_cache); its own setup_sim does the same steps with its TMPDIR under --out.  The collection is
  read_bs.onpolicy_states' build line for line (that function cannot be reused as is: it records a fixed 2,048-step
  rollout and neither the rewards nor the raw terminal observation), with complete episodes.
The episode loop: SB3 2.9.0 collect_rollouts' action path (policy forward on the normalised observation, sampled,
  clipped), stepping the inner DummyVecEnv and applying the same VecNormalize's normalize_obs / normalize_reward (its
  step_wait with training False is exactly that, vec_normalize.py lines 175-205), so the RAW time-limit terminal
  observation survives for the phase feeding (VecNormalize overwrites it with its normalised copy).
G: returns_to_go in float64 of the float32 normalised rewards (the buffer's units), bootstrapped with gamma^(T-k) V(s_T)
  of the arm's own critic at a TimeLimit.truncated end (k_T = 1000, the terminal observation fed phi(1000) for
  critic_phase), none after a termination: SB3's rewards[last] += gamma V(terminal) (on_policy_algorithm.py lines
  236-245) unrolled.  V: the arm's form through read_bs.Net (batched, 8,192 rows).  Buckets by the state's k.
Sigma: exp(float64(log_std)) of policy.pth (zip, torch.load weights_only) for actions 13 (tail_2_pitch_act) and 14
  (tail_3_pitch_act); the policy applies no floor when it samples (SB3 DiagGaussianDistribution std = exp(log_std),
  distributions.py line 165); MirrorKLPPO's symmetry_sigma_floor 0.01 enters only its mirror-KL loss
  (mirror_kl_ppo.py lines 21, 201), so the floored max(sigma, 0.01) is printed beside it with a "binds" flag.

DECISIONS (each also in SPEC.txt)
==================================================================================================================
D1 Complete 1,000-step episodes (not a 2,048-step rollout): every state's G is a full return-to-go, with the
   truncation bootstrap only where training has it.  The first 8 complete episodes per env (why-probe convention).
D2 N = 32 per pair.  Basis [measured]: at S with the why-probe's 48 episodes the per-bucket SE across episodes is
   0.003-0.011 (val/why_offline.log), so about 0.003-0.014 at 32; the effects of interest are 0.02-0.12.  Cost 2.2-2.6
   s CPU per episode [measured].  48 would cost +50% for SE x 0.82.
D3 Seeds: one base for every pair (common random numbers): PROD 6271 (envs 6271-6274) for --root, VAL 6281 for --pair
   and every validation; both checked new (val/seeds_check.out: 1,371 named numbers, 1,181 text files, 7,737
   distinct numbers / range members; RESULT NO COLLISION; sanity: every known seed found).  PROD was never run.
D4 V and the phase: the reader's own functions, not a re-implementation (task step 1); the plain form for control,
   phase (own phi(k), phi(1000) on the terminal observation) for critic_phase, and S read in both forms (they must
   agree bitwise: zero phase columns after the start transform).  Report-only "raw" variant for critic_phase.
D5 Statistics: per run per bucket the episode-weighted mean of V - G and |V - G| with the SE across episodes (the
   episode is the independent unit), plus the why-probe's state-pooled means; per arm the median over runs and the
   control-minus-phase difference of medians; per-seed differences.  No test, no p-value (report only, 4 per arm).
D6 The guard opens only score.json and SCREEN.json; it requires status exactly "final" (PROVISIONAL refuses), the
   score's root equal to --root, the classes equal to the queue in order, each COUNTED / STOPPED / LOST, COUNTED with
   T = E, and at least one COUNTED; --episodes / --seed / --form are refused in --root mode (pre-registered).
D7 Outputs: vg_sigma.json + vg_sigma.txt deterministic (no clock; the same inputs give the same bytes [measured: b2
   rerun cmp-identical]); run_info.json holds the times.  --out is refused inside the root or PS, or if it already
   holds vg_sigma.json.  TMPDIR / MPLCONFIGDIR = --out/_tmp, removed at the end.
D8 Self-checks become PROBLEMS (exit 3), never silent (1.1 adds: the phase feeding with nonzero critic phase columns,
   SCREEN.json against APPROVED; G with another gamma refuses): num_timesteps, k sequence, truncation at k 1000,
   Monitor length, loop forward values vs the batched plain critic (<= 1e-4; measured 1.43e-6), the S forms, the
   markers' loop row at E (sha256 of zip and pkl, critic form), each run's start copy sha256, the saved pairs
   present, the imported PS tools against APPROVED's tool_sha256.  Wrong actuator names, gamma, floor or horizon
   refuse (exit 2).
