  - Seeds 3042-3061 (n = 20), 1,000 steps.
  - Deterministic predict on normalizer.normalize_obs.
- Nudges (constant from step 1, applied separately):
  - BIAS b: action[14] = own14 + b before env.step.  This is the clamp test's override path, D-9 style: the env clips,
    low-passes and maps it.  The command is clip(own14 + b).
  - OBS d: the policy sees obs[6] + radians(d) (tail_3 angle); the normaliser clips at z = 10.  The command is
    own14.
- Parking rule: the window is steps 501-1000.  A railed step has command >= 0.99.  An episode is PARKED when more
  than 250 window steps are railed.
- Per-seed threshold T_s, by bisection:
  - BIAS on (0, 2.0].  2.0 parks by construction (SB3 predict clips own14 to [-1, 1]).  7 halvings give a step of
    0.0156.
  - OBS: 12 deg first.  If it does not park, the seed is unreachable.  Otherwise 7 halvings on (0, 12] give a step of
    0.094 deg.
- M = the 11th smallest T_s of 20 seeds.
- Predictors, all fixed before the run (wm_predict.txt, PREREG.txt section 3):
  - b*_P = 1 - G_P - c0, from ew_series.csv (seeds 5001-5010 states).
  - b*_L from vew_analyze.txt section G.
  - b*_S from wm_static.py: network only, the 60 early-warning own states, tail_3 input 0-12 deg.

6. LIMITS
==================================================================================================================
- n = 7 checkpoints from 3 runs; 2 independent positive onsets (s42, s60) and 1 late negative (s80).  The predictor
  ranking (Q3) holds on this set only.
- 20 seeds per checkpoint; the bisection resolution is 0.0156 (bias) and 0.094 deg (obs).  Q1's "as close" rests on
  a 0.05 tolerance that I fixed in advance, not on a test of equality.
- Deterministic episodes from reset noise 0.05.  Training rollouts are stochastic and keep changing the policy; the
  margin measures distance for a FROZEN policy, not the probability or time to rail under training.
- b*_P comes from seeds 5001-5010 states; the closed loop uses seeds 3042-3061 (baselines agree to 0.026).
- The bias nudge adds to the raw command, which is also what the reward's raw-command terms price; that does not
  matter here, because the policy is frozen.
- "Unreachable" on the observation channel depends on the checkpoint's normaliser cap (4.28-9.68 deg), a training
  statistic, not only on the network.
