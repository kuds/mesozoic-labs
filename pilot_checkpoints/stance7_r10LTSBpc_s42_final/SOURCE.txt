    locomotion stage uses none of them: stock PPO on the production locomotion [env] with walking pilot 3's R6
    walking reward (the maintainer's decision (9) of 2026-11-04; pilot-only, never merged).
  - Not a certified stance; the tie with s80 was broken by the maintainer's choice, not by a measurement.
  - The reset to std 0.1353 is a large relative jump from this parent's noise (about x2.4 on the mean, about x19 on
    the smallest actuator and x1.2 on the largest, from the printed three-decimal values; at -1.5 it was x3.9 and
    x32), and it has never been trained.
