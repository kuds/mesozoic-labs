RESUME (step 3)
==================================================================================================================
WHAT IS SAVED WHEN (code read): a periodic pair at P = S + 262,144 k is written by SB3's CheckpointCallback on the last
step of the rollout ending at P, BEFORE that rollout's update (the probe's row at P, recorded at that rollout's end,
describes the same policy).  The records reach the disk on their own clocks: eval_episodes.csv, evaluations.npz and
gate_progress.npz at every evaluation (P is one, and the EvalCallback runs before the CheckpointCallback in the same
step); arm_evidence.jsonl (this harness) at every rollout end; but policy_probe.npz and train_episodes.csv only on the
checkpoint calls ("rewritten on the checkpoint cadence") and at training end, which a crash never reaches.  So at the
save of P the probe file holds rows up to P - 8,192 only, and the newest pair of a killed attempt is complete only
once a later checkpoint call has written its rows.
THE RESUME POINT (bs_resume.resume_point): the newest saved pair P (S < P < E, on the 262,144 grid) of the attempt's
folder such that (1) the pair passes the code's own curriculum.checkpoints.checkpoint_pair_problem (sidecar present;
zip testzip; SB3's "data" and policy.pth in the outer archive; the pkl unpickles) and its data's num_timesteps is P;
(2) every earlier saved pair is beside it (the reader reads them); (3) records_through: the probe holds exactly one row
at every 8,192 up to P (the parent's 256 seeded rows and the window's), the evidence file's first (P - S)/8,192 lines
are one per rollout end in order (a cut or unparsable line fails), the evaluation ledger holds exactly 10 rows at every
131,072 up to P (no off-grid row, no (timesteps, episode) twice), evaluations.npz and gate_progress.npz one row at every
131,072 up to P, and the training ledger holds an episode after P (so it was written after P and holds every episode
that ended by P); (4) [version B] no record is still byte-equal to the copy the attempt started from when P is after
the attempt's own load step (a never-rewritten record holds rows that are not the attempt's: the parent's, or the
attempt before's).  Checked by content, never by timing.  Usually the second newest pair; none: a fresh start from S
(BS2's rerun).  The start pair copy must be at its pinned sha256, else no resume at all.
THE NEW ATTEMPT (run_bs.py --resume-from <runs>/<name>_crashed<k>; the runner passes its own retired folder): a fresh
runs/<name>/ whose models/ holds hard links to the last attempt's start copy and saved pairs up to P (a copy where a
link cannot be made; each sha256-checked; no extra disk), whose stage directory gets copies of the last attempt's five
records, and whose arm_evidence.jsonl gets the last attempt's lines up to P byte for byte (the in-memory evidence list
pre-filled from them, so the run_meta summary covers the whole window).  It loads P under the same arm (the hook
re-installed after the load checks), the same seed and total = 12,000,000 - P: the code's own same-stage resume
(seed_resume_eval_state, max_timesteps = P) keeps each record's rows up to P (the ledgers' kept cells as the text the
file holds) and the session appends after P -- the mechanism that starts every continuation at S from the parent's
records.  The load checks are the start's with P for S (num_timesteps P, the training env's statistics equal to P's
pkl, ent_coef 0.005, gae_lambda the arm's); V5 compares stage_config.json with run.timesteps 12,000,000 - P and the P
zip as the lineage.  The last attempt's folder is never changed.
WHAT A RESUME CHANGES (the resumed run is NOT bitwise the uninterrupted one) [code read; measured in V4 (e)]:
  - the work after P of the last attempt is discarded: the rollouts after P, and the update on the rollout ending at P
    (its probe row at P stays in the stitched record, as the parent's row at S does for every continuation, but the
    loaded parameters are the pair's, saved before it);
  - the training envs are re-created and reset at P: the episodes running at P are cut (their training-ledger rows are
    never written) and the envs restart their seeded streams (seed + rank, as at S: the first episodes after P start
    from the same initial-state draws as the first episodes after S);
  - the global torch / numpy generators are re-seeded at the load (SB3 set_random_seed(seed)): action sampling and the
    minibatch order after P differ from the uninterrupted run's;
  - the evaluation env is re-created (seed + 1000) and its stream restarts at P;
  - the VecNormalize statistics are P's pkl (exact), then move with the new rollouts;
  - the collapse backstop's patience counter and the hop watch's cursor restart at P (their history is seeded);
  - diagnostics.npz (DiagnosticsCallback, not seeded by the code) covers (P, T] only; (S, crash] stays in the last
    attempt's folder; progress.json is the new session's.
  So a resumed run is a valid continuation of the same arm, seed and schedule with one discarded update and fresh draws
  from P on, not a replay.
HOW IT IS LABELLED: run_meta.json "resumed_from" (P; null for a run that never resumed), "load_step" (S or P),
"attempt" (k), "attempts" (each earlier attempt's folder, status, exit, resumed_from, load_step, last_step, times;
no outcome), "resume" (the candidates checked and why each newer one was refused, P, the pair's sha256, the links and
copies, the seeded sha256, the stitched-row digests), "digest_start" (the S pair's), "digest_load" (P's);
check.json "resumed_from" / "load_step"; markers.json "resumed_from" / "attempt"; the runner's log "start <name>
(<name>.attempt<k>.log) --resume-from <name>_crashed<j>" and logs/crash_ledger.tsv.  Whether a resumed run counts as
such, or is reported apart, is the criteria lane's.
CHECK ON A STITCHED RUN (read_bs.check_run; every run): exactly one probe row per update step S + 8,192 k in (S, T),
none at or after T; ent_coef and learning_rate equal to the parent's F2 probe at every one of them; one evidence line
per rollout end; one evaluation of 10 episodes at every 131,072 up to T in the ledger (no (timesteps, episode) twice,
no row off the grid or after T), evaluations.npz and gate_progress.npz one row each; for a resumed run also: the
resume fields consistent (P on the grid inside (S, T), the crashed folder of the same run, the loaded pair = the
resume point's, attempts), and bs_resume.stitch_digests recomputed on the finished files equal to those recorded from
the last attempt at the resume (the probe's six CHECK columns, the evidence lines, both ledgers' rows in the last
attempt's header order, evaluations.npz's timesteps / results / ep_lengths, gate_progress.npz's timesteps; rows up to
P): the stitched part is the last attempt's, unchanged.
THE RUNNER: an own crash (not early) is rerun at once with --resume-from its retired folder; a relaunch finds the
newest retired folder of a pending name in logs/crash_ledger.tsv; the early-crash rule (not counted, halt) is "last
step below the attempt's load step + 131,072"; three own crashes still write lost.json.  Cost of a crash: the
discarded steps after P, on average about 1.5 x 262,144 = 393,216 steps (48 rollouts, about 22 min at 4 at once;
at most 2 x 262,144 + 8,192) instead of everything since S (up to 9.9M steps, about 9.6 h) [inferred from the flush
rule and TIMING].
