THE TEST (pre-registered; unchanged from BS)
- An exact unpaired permutation test, one-sided (the blind arm lower), alpha 0.05. Statistic: the difference of arm
  means, control minus blind (with 4 per arm, equivalently the control arm's sum). p = the share of the C(8, 4) = 70
  ways to choose 4 of the 8 primaries as "control" whose statistic is at least the observed one (the observed split
  included; a tie counts against the reading). Exact rational arithmetic on the recorded values: two sums tie only if
  equal as exact rationals of the recorded doubles.
- Minimum attainable p = 1/70 = 0.0143. Attainable p <= 0.05: 1/70, 2/70, 3/70 (0.0429); 4/70 = 0.0571 is not. Size:
