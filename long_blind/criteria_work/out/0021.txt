- After F2 and every re-fetch, once each run's C1-C7 and each LOST run's p1-p3 are decided: until then "incomplete"
  and no line is read.
- PROVISIONAL, and not acted on, while any of these stands (the scorer prints each): a pin unfilled or a tool's sha256
  other than its pin; this text's, THIS12's or EVID's sha256 or the branch HEAD other than APPROVED's (and, for the
  branch HEAD, the LAUNCH lines' head=); a
  class input measured on a measuring copy of another commit, with other seeds or on a model whose physics_revision is
  not 10; a read file whose sha256 differs from the fetch manifest; V0-V5, V8 (i)-(ii) or V9 not passed. Each is
  cleared only by filling the pin or measuring again, never by a ruling. A configuration or session fact (C1, C4, p1,
  p2) is not PROVISIONAL: it decides COMPLETE, LOST or PRESENCE, and no RUNLOG note or ruling changes a class, A, n_A
  or the line.
- A reading neither incomplete nor provisional is final and goes to the maintainer at once. The durable copy (D17,
