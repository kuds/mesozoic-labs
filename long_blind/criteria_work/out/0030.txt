## 7. Open questions and the candidate next steps already proposed

Who decides: the maintainer. Nothing trains, and no gate, reward, plant or
criterion changes, without the maintainer's approval. Any training change
needs criteria approved before the first training step, as for D-D43, D-D51
and the entropy screen.

**Done since the first draft.** The tail normaliser probe (chosen at 17:28
UTC on 2026-10-29, no training) [L151, L152] and the three posture tests
(logged at 19:55 UTC) [L160] are done and verified. COMREV §6 item 4
framed the probe's question: "re-run the end policies with the start's
normaliser to see whether the loop gain falls back. If it does,
normalisation drift is the lever to study, not entropy." It does not fall
back. It rises (closed loop 0.632 -> 1.086, 12/12), and the weights carry the
growth; whether the drift shaped training is untested [L158, L159] (§4.8).
The posture tests found a learned posture-to-tail pathway in the s42 lineage
from mid-rise on, no mechanical push, and no nose-down or CoM-forward shift
before the tail [L161-L164] (§4.11).

**Open questions**

1. Why does the positive loop form in training, and why does its gain grow
   (0.27 -> 0.63 over 1,572,864 steps) [L145, L146]?
   - The earliest event found is now a posture-independent tail bias. In r10
     s42 the policy's tail_3 command mean began rising at about 1.20M, about
     0.17M before the nose-down pitch, and at a fixed observation the tail
     command was rising by 1.3-1.6M. Around then the network had little or
     no posture-to-tail pathway (+0.005 per degree at 1,048,576; -0.020 at
     2,097,152, about 1/8 of its 3,145,728 value) and a negative loop (G
     -0.250 at 1,048,576, -0.386 at 2,097,152); both grew between 2.1M and
     3.15M [L161, L164]. What changed in the policy at about 1.2M is not
     known. The fixed-observation reading goes through each checkpoint's own
     normaliser, so it does not separate the drift [L164].
   - The weights, not the normaliser, carry the gain's growth: it sits in the
     second hidden layer and the output layer, not in the first layer's
     tail_3 column [L158, L159]. Which reward gradient grew them is not
     known.
2. Why in some lineages and seeds, and not others [L064, L106, L120]?
3. What drives the outward drift of the training-row mean while the open-loop
   reward falls (-0.122 ± 0.037 per mini-batch on tail_3's bias) [L063]?
4. Did the normaliser's drift shape training [L104]? At fixed weights it is
   an effect of the rail and lowers the gain (12/12 in every sweep range)
   [L157, L158]. Whether PPO grew the downstream weights partly in answer to
   the compressed input (x4.3-4.65 per normalised unit, against the x2.29
   that full compensation needs) needs a training counterfactual: a
   continuation with frozen statistics (ECS add-on N) [L116, L159].
5. Does the entropy schedule matter in the first 3.1M steps, or for when an
   onset comes [L105, L127]?
6. Does r10 change the rail rate [L100]?
7. Is the railed s42 lineage's 5 mm backward CoM drift the rail's doing? It
   exists only in the tail-up state, with one start checkpoint [L133, L148].
8. What is tail-up worth at r9 s42's onset (3.1M-3.5M) under full noise, and
   under the discounted, normalised objective [L071]?
9. Did r9 s42's 1.3-1.7M forward COM drift track the tail command? Answering
   it needs `gate_progress.npz` or the 1,572,864 checkpoint, and neither was
   fetched (SATDIAG §3, §8) [L038].
10. Why does the hold-0 value change sign at r10 s42's final (-64.46) when it
    is positive everywhere else (§8.3) [L103]?
11. Which actuators were never named?
    - Arm C (narrative only) [L025].
    - The 2026-09 nodes (8, 1 and 40 episodes) [L019].
    - The saturated ledger episodes of every r9 run without an alarm but

    - stance3 Run 1 was never checked for an alarm [L064].
12. What do the ECS's per-actuator training-row shares for tail_3 show? They
    are stored in ECS `score/read/<name>/read.json` and were never summarised

13. Clamp variants never run: tail_1 or tail_2 clamps, stochastic clamps,
    other reset-noise levels, and more than one start checkpoint per lineage
