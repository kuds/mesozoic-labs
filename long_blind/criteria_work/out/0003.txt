RUN HANDLING (as the runner and reader implement it; no Colab rule carries over) -- as BS
- Fails stay fails. No continuation is stopped, changed, extended or rerun because of an outcome.
- COUNTED: CHECK passed, stop reason "end" at E. Its primary and secondaries are read from its markers.
- STOPPED: CHECK passed, stop reason "collapse" (the run's own collapse stop, patience 10) at T < E. Not expected: the
  stop cannot fire in (S, E] (collapse_min_evals 20, patience 10; the resume seeds the parent's 16 evaluations at or
  before S, so the 11 window evaluations give at most 8 patience observations < 10, and without the seeding none)
  [measured: F2 evaluations.npz.copy1 (16 at or before S, 11 in (S, E]), stage_config.json.copy1; inferred from
  <BS>/code environments/shared/curriculum/early_stopping.py lines 129-130, 188-193 and 254, and checkpoints.py lines
  387-429]. Kept as a guard: if it occurs, MISSING, filled (primary and secondaries).
- UNREADABLE: a COUNTED continuation whose 8 primary rows are not each present once with a finite value: MISSING,
  filled, cause printed.
- CRASH: the process died (an exception, out of memory, killed) or a container restart left it half-way. The folder
  becomes runs/<name>_crashed<k>, kept and never read; the continuation reruns at once from its start pair with the
  same seed and arguments, and the rerun is the continuation. An EARLY crash (last step run_meta records below
  S + 131,072, or none) is not counted toward LOST and halts new starts until the coordinator has found the cause from
  run_meta's error and traceback alone and fixed it.
- LOST: a third crash of its own (the runner writes <BS2>/lost.json), or the full read refused for a cause in the
  continuation's own files (its probe or ledger unreadable; the coordinator writes it to lost.json with the cause and
  records it in RUNLOG). MISSING, filled. A full-read failure caused by read_bs.py itself is a tool defect: no line is
  read until it is fixed (the maintainer's word and a re-pin if a pinned file changes) and every continuation is read
  again. A full-read failure confined to the loop markers, with the probe and ledger readable, is NOT LOST: it is a
  tool defect as above, and a reader change is a pinned-file change after which the reading stays PROVISIONAL.
- TRAINING ATTEMPTS: each continuation trains at most 3 times through crashes of its own (the third makes it LOST); a
  rerun after an EARLY crash, after a container restart that left it half-way without the status "crashed" (not
  counted), or after a VOID replaces an attempt that is never read. The screen reads at most 8 continuations, each
  from one completed attempt. Every VOID rerun needs the maintainer's word first (RUNLOG).
- VOID: CHECK fails (the manipulation check included), or exit 5 with CHECK failing. The folder becomes
  runs/<name>_void<k>, kept and never read; the runner starts nothing new and halts (logs/HALTED). The cause is found
  from the CHECK record alone and fixed; then, with the maintainer's word, the voided continuation reruns from its
  start pair under its seed (and, if the fix changes a pinned file, every continuation run before the fix). A fix that
  changes a pinned file or this text needs the maintainer's word and a re-pin first. Unfixed, the screen reads VOID:
  no line, and any rerun is a new pre-registration.
- REFUSED by preflight (exit 2, before any optimizer step): fixed and restarted under the same name; a pinned-file
  change needs the maintainer's word and a re-pin; unfixed, VOID. Its cause is found by re-running, read only,
