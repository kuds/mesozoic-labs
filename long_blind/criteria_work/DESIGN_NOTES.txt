D2 ARMS 3 + 1, NOT 2 + 2 (the task's default; argued from BS2):
   - What BS2 showed: the control side is predictable from this state (4 of 4 climbed, rise +0.29 to +0.67 in 1.44M;
     2 of 4 over the bar by 3,407,872); from the lineage's later state 12 of 12 flagged within 1.57M (ECS). So the
     control's long-run rail is the expected outcome (the record's one-sided 95% lower bounds 0.78 from 12 of 12, 0.47

   - The open part is the blind arm's long run: whether the critic recovers the angle (other inputs, a phase proxy),
     whether a park or a habit elsewhere forms, whether the gate holds. Q1-Q3 are all about it. A third blind run
     raises the chance of seeing a late escape that happens in a share q of runs (0.51 -> 0.66 at q 0.3; 0.75 -> 0.88
     at q 0.5) and tightens what a clean arm bounds (95% upper bound on that share 0.78 -> 0.63) [design_numbers B].

     0.05-0.3 from the record) is covered by line 3: a non-railing control withholds every "held" line rather than
     letting a clean blind arm read as a success. With 2 + 2 a guard failure would be rarer (both controls failing:
     0.0025-0.09), but Q1-Q3 would rest on two draws.
   - Neither layout gives a test (1/4 vs 1/6, D1). 3 + 1 is the task's default; I found nothing in BS2 that argues
     for 2 + 2.

D7 THE READING'S RULES.
   - "Missing never helps the hypothesis": a blind continuation that is not READ TO 12M (STOPPED without a rail, LOST,
     COUNTED but not measured) never counts as off the rail, as a gate pass or as free of a habit; it counts only
     against (NOT READ / NOT MEASURED / NOT SHOWN), and any missing blind continuation keeps lines 3-5 from being read
     (line 2). A rail it showed before it stopped or was lost counts as RAILED (monotone: it can only move a reading
     to line 1, as the round's PRESENCE rule). A missing control makes line 3, which withholds lines 4-5.
   - Line order: an observed blind rail (line 1) is decisive whatever else happened; then missing (2); then the
     control's failure (3); then the best (4) and the rest (5).
   - Q1 counts PARKED as off the rail (the round: PARKED is not RAIL) but keeps it out of line 4: a park means the
     drive came back without saturating, which is not "the effect lasted".
   - Q2 is the round's GATE CLASS; because the certificate counts saturated episodes as hop_or_fall, a blind run with a
     late habit can fail the gate through it: Q2 is not independent of Q1/Q3 (KNOWN LIMITS).
