RUN HANDLING (the harness's rules; one continuation = one queue entry)
- Fails stay fails. No continuation is stopped, changed, extended or rerun because of an outcome.
- ONE CONTINUATION = one queue entry (arm, seed): its decided attempt in runs/<name>/ together with the rows its records
  carry over, stitched, from earlier attempts up to its resume point. The study reads one decided attempt per entry.
- COUNTED: CHECK passed, stop reason "end" at E.
- STOPPED: CHECK passed, stop reason "collapse" or "hop_watch" at T < E (the stage's own stops; the collapse stop can
  fire from 3,801,088 on, the hop watch from 7,077,888 on; the parent never came near either over this window: 0 of 72
  collapse observations dropped, hop duty 0.0 [measured, <LB>/logs/notes_design.txt EARLY ENDS]). Read to T by the
  round's rules: TAIL CLASS UNREAD unless RAIL, GATE FAIL, NEGATIVE; in THE READING it is NOT READ TO 12M unless RAILED.
- RESUMED: a COUNTED or STOPPED continuation whose decided attempt loaded a resume point P > S (run_meta resumed_from,
  load_step, attempt, attempts). It is one continuation and counts in every line, printed "RESUMED from P (attempt
  k)". A resume is not a replay: the work after P is discarded (the update on the rollout ending at P included), the
  envs, the evaluation env and the random generators restart at P under the same seed, the collapse and hop-watch
  counters restart, and diagnostics.npz covers (P, T] only [measured, notes_design.txt WHAT A RESUME CHANGES]. The
  resume point is chosen by content, never by outcome (the newest saved pair whose pair and records are complete,
  bs_resume.resume_point).
- CRASH: the process died (an exception, out of memory, killed). The runner renames the folder runs/<name>_crashed<k>
  (kept; never opened by anyone; the resume's own reads of it are the pinned tools' work) and reruns the entry at once
  with --resume-from that folder: from its newest complete saved pair, else from S. An EARLY crash (the attempt's last
  recorded step below its load step + 131,072, or none) is not counted and halts new starts until the coordinator has
  found the cause from run_meta's error and traceback alone. A container restart that leaves attempts half-way (their
  run_meta not "crashed"): the coordinator relaunches the runner, which resumes them; not counted as own crashes
  [measured: <LB>/tools/run_waves_bs.sh header lines 4-7 and 60-63].
- LOST: a third own crash (the runner writes <LB>/lost.json), or the full read or the measurement refused for a cause
  in the continuation's own files (the coordinator writes it to lost.json with the cause and records it in RUNLOG).
  Never READ TO 12M; its ledger as it stands is read by the pinned M5 once all are decided (RAILED, Q3, Q4 above).
  A failure caused by a tool (read_bs.py, measure_lb.py or a round tool) is a tool defect, not LOST: no line is read
  until it is fixed (the maintainer's word, and a re-pin if a pinned file changes) and every continuation is read
  again.
- VOID: CHECK fails, or exit 5 with CHECK failing. The folder becomes runs/<name>_void<k>, kept and never read; the
  runner starts nothing new and halts (logs/HALTED). The cause is found from the CHECK record alone and fixed; then,
  with the maintainer's word, the voided continuation reruns from its start pair under its seed. A fix that changes a
  pinned file or this text needs the maintainer's word and a re-pin first. Unfixed, the study reads VOID: no line.
- REFUSED by preflight (exit 2, before any optimizer step): as BS2 (fixed and restarted under the same name; its cause
  found by re-running, read only, cd /tmp && nice -n 10 S2/venv/bin/python -I -B <LB>/tools/run_bs.py --arm ARM --seed
  N --preflight-only; a pinned-file change needs the maintainer's word and a re-pin; unfixed, VOID).
- TRAINING ATTEMPTS: at most 3 own crashes per entry (the third makes it LOST); an EARLY crash, a container restart or
  a VOID rerun is not counted. Every VOID rerun needs the maintainer's word first (RUNLOG).
- MEASUREMENT: a measurement crash is rerun on the same files (ROUND lines 428-434; measure_lb.py reruns once); a
  measuring tool's own refusal is final: a refused M3 at the final pair is GATE FAIL with its message (ROUND line 282);
  a refused M1 at a label C7 names leaves the continuation not READ TO 12M (cause printed). A measurement not made for
  lack of disk is not a refusal: the reading waits (INCOMPLETE).
- The runner is restarted only by the coordinator (after a container restart or a fixed halt); it re-reads its
  ledgers (logs/decided.tsv, crash_ledger.tsv, lost.json).
