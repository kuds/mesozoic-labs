The term (reward_stance_cop_band: environments/tyrannosaurus_rex/base_env.py reward section 8i, which runs the arithmetic of reward_functions.cop_band_load_guard and reward_functions.reward_stance_cop_band):
- Per control step and per foot k (r, l), over the step's 5 physics substeps:
  - c_k, the whole-foot CoP: every floor contact of the limb, force-weighted, in the sole frame of its own substep, along the foot's fore-aft axis, in span half-lengths (-1 heel line, +1 toe line; on r9, x = 60 + 70 c mm ahead of that foot's MTP III). It is the per-step reading whose mean over the window's loaded steps is the judge's K2b statistic <label>_foot_cop_fore_aft. Measured: it equals the recorder's trace byte for byte on 16,000 foot-steps.
  - p_k, the pad's own signed CoP along its forward axis, in pad half-lengths (EpisodeTrace.sole_cop[t, k, 0]). K2a's max_sole_cop_fore_aft is the larger over the feet of the window mean of |p_k| (1 on an unloaded step).
  - u_k = max(e(c_k; -0.6, +0.4), e(p_k; -0.65, +0.65)), where e(v; lo, hi) is how far v lies outside [lo, hi] (0 inside). The whole-foot band is +18 to +88 mm ahead of MTP III, inside K2b's -5 to +100 mm. The pad band keeps load moved onto the digits from paying the band: the digits move the whole-foot CoP but not the pad's.
  - s_k, the load share: the limb's mean floor force over the substeps divided by the two limbs' sum (0 when the sum is 0).
  - g_k, the guard (a ramp): 0 when the foot is unloaded (no finite c_k, or no substep with more than 0.1 N on the limb), stands off its pad (no finite p_k, or no sole contact above 0.1 N on any substep) or s_k is not finite; otherwise min(max((s_k - 0.25) / 0.10, 0), 1). So the guard is 0 at a share of 0.25 or less, 1 at 0.35 or more, and linear between.
  - q_k = g_k x exp(-(u_k / 0.357)^2). The tolerance, 0.357 span half-lengths, is 25 mm. A foot whose guard is 0 scores 0, and its readings are not read.
- Each step pays -0.30 x ((1 - q_r) + (1 - q_l)) / 2 after the settle (step > 200) while the command is zero, and 0 otherwise.
- So each foot pays 0 to 0.15 per step, and the term at most 0.30 per step (240 per 800-step window). A foot pays the full 0.15, whatever its readings, when it is unloaded, stands on its digits alone, or carries 25% or less of the two limbs' floor load.
- Every reading is fore-aft, the guard is per foot and the mean over the feet is symmetric, so the term is exactly mirror-invariant (measured: mirrored rollouts agree in reward and info to 1e-9).
- The doors (SP/foot/stance4/copband/design.md sections 4 and 5). Lifting a foot or standing on the digits pays the full per-foot price. Load moved onto the digits is priced by the pad band; a small free zone between the two bands remains (design.md section 4.2; inferred).
- The lighten-a-foot door is BOUNDED by the ramp, not closed. design.md section 5's "closed" row described the prototype's step at 35%, not this ramp. At fixed readings a lighter foot never pays less. A foot that sheds load to a share s between 0.25 and 0.35, and so brings its CoP back into the band, pays 0.15 x (1 - g(s)). That is less than the 0.15 x (1 - exp(-(u / 0.357)^2)) it pays fully loaded with its CoP u out of band only when g(s) > exp(-(u / 0.357)^2), that is when s > 0.25 + 0.10 x exp(-(u / 0.357)^2): s above 0.334 for u 0.15, above 0.299 for u 0.3, above 0.264 for u 0.5, and never at a share of 0.25 or below. The judge sees the door too, through two rails of the v2 gate ([curriculum] in stance.toml): min_foot_load_share_windowed (0.35: the lowest foot share over the window's 1 s blocks) fails an episode in which a foot carries less than 35% of the load over a 1 s block, and min_foot_load_share (0.40: the lighter foot's share of the whole window's floor load) fails one in which a foot carries less than 40% of it over the window. So an episode that keeps a foot in the door for a whole block is not clean (inferred from the rails' definitions).
- The twist route (design.md section 5, last row, "the main risk"): each foot's CoP held in band by asymmetric ankle torques while the feet stay staggered. The band alone does not close it. In this arm the stagger term prices the stagger the route needs (the reason for D-D40 2A; inferred, not tested), and nothing prices the ankle asymmetry as such. It is watched (READOUTS).
- Like the stagger term, the band is not in DiagnosticsCallback.REWARD_KEYS, so the training logs do not carry it.

CRITERIA, PER RUN (unchanged from stance4 and stance3)

K1 gate held:
- v2 PASS at 6,291,456 and at final. PASS means clean >= 37/40 with exact Clopper-Pearson LCB >= 0.80, full horizon >= 38/40, hop-or-fall <= 1, and the repository verdict PASS.
- Across J, at most one checkpoint below 37/40 clean and none below 33/40.

K2 fore-aft held, at every checkpoint in J:
- K2a, pad rail: at most 3 of 40 episodes with max_sole_cop_fore_aft > 0.80. A non-full-horizon episode counts as over.
- K2b, signed per-foot whole-foot CoP: at most 3 of 40 episodes in which either foot's <label>_foot_cop_fore_aft lies outside [-0.929, +0.571] span half-lengths. The statistic is the mean over loaded steps in the window, steps 200-999. On r9 the range is [-5, +100] mm ahead of that foot's MTP III, with x = 60 + 70 w mm. A non-full-horizon episode counts as outside.
- K2c, no drift: the panel median of the both-feet whole-foot CoP (which equals the COM projection to within 0.5 mm) lies within [-60, +30] mm of the statue's median on the same seeds, and its range over the six checkpoints is at most 30 mm.

K3 robustness, at 6,291,456 and at final: joint-space noise falls <= 2 of 20, and own-sigma falls <= 1 of 20.

K4 roll, at 6,291,456 and at final: at most 3 of 40 full-horizon episodes with max_sole_tilt_deg > 2.0 or max_sole_corner_lift_m > 0.004.

Run verdicts:
- HOLDS: K1-K4 all hold.
- FORE-AFT HOLDS: K1 and K2 hold.

CREDIT RULE (FIXED NOW)
The band is ADOPTED (proposed to the maintainer for committing) only if both hold:
(a) at least 2 of the 3 band runs HOLD (K1-K4 all hold);
(b) the band arm does no worse than the control on every K test: for each of K1, K2, K3 and K4, the fraction of band runs passing it is at least the fraction of control runs passing it.
With the control's actual results (K1 0/3, K2 0/3, K3 2/3, K4 2/3), (b) is implied by (a). Under (a) at least 2 of 3 band runs pass every K test, so every band fraction is at least 2/3. That is at least each control fraction: 0/3, 0/3, 2/3 and 2/3.
(b)'s specific form, with these control results: at least 2 of the 3 band runs pass K3, and at least 2 pass K4 (K1 and K2 need nothing). (a) implies it.
Both forms are stated here. The scorer computes the general form from the control's recorded passes in stance4's score_final.json and prints the specific form beside it.

DRIFT OR SPLIT (FIXED NOW; read only to choose between decision lines 5 and 6)
For each band run that fails K2, the scorer classes the K2 failure from that run's six J judge files:
- The reference m0, per J judge file: the median, over the statue's full-horizon episodes with finite readings in that file (statue_episodes_detail; 39 episodes, seed 3081 falls), of (r + l) / 2, where r and l are the statue's <label>_foot_cop_fore_aft. Measured on all stance4 J files: +0.0577 span half-lengths (64.0 mm ahead of MTP III), the same in every file.
- The checkpoints counted: the J checkpoints at which K2a or K2b fails (more than 3 of 40 episodes over or outside; the scorer's own K2a and K2b results, score_stance3.py's code). The episodes at a J checkpoint where K2a and K2b both pass caused no K2 failure and are not classed.
- The episodes classed: at each counted checkpoint, every full-horizon episode that fails K2a or K2b and has finite r and l readings (the per-foot values K2b reads). An episode that fell, or lacks a reading, is not classed; it already counts against K2a, K2b and K1.
- An episode is classed only when both feet lie clearly off the reference: min(|r - m0|, |l - m0|) >= 15/70 span half-lengths (0.2143; 15 mm on r9). It is then COMMON when both feet lie on the same side of m0, (r - m0) x (l - m0) > 0, and SPLIT when they lie on opposite sides. Equivalently, with the common offset |(r + l) / 2 - m0| and the half split |r - l| / 2: the two differ by at least 15/70, and the larger names the class. An episode with a foot within 15 mm of m0 is NEAR-REFERENCE (a one-foot excursion, or both feet near the reference) and is not classed: the sign of a foot that close to m0 carries no information. The margin lies above the statue's own scatter around m0: in the stance4 and stance3 J files every statue foot lies within 13.5 mm of m0, and each statue episode's nearer foot within 12.7 mm (measured; the band changes no dynamics, so the LTSB statue reads the same). On the known runs the nearer-foot distances of the K2-failing episodes at the counted checkpoints leave a gap from 11.9 to 20.3 mm, and 15 mm lies inside it (measured).
- n_c, n_s and n_u: the run's COMMON, SPLIT and NEAR-REFERENCE episodes, summed over the counted checkpoints.
- The run's K2 failure is:
  - OTHER when any of its six J judge files is missing or invalid (with --runs-ended);
  - otherwise DRIFT when K2a and K2b pass at all six J checkpoints: the run fails K2 through K2c, the drift test, alone (no checkpoint is counted);
  - otherwise DRIFT when n_c > n_s, or when n_c == n_s and K2c fails;
  - otherwise SPLIT when n_s > n_c, or when n_s == n_c > 0 and K2c passes;
  - otherwise OTHER (n_c == n_s == 0 and K2c passes: K2a or K2b failed only through falls, missing readings or near-reference episodes).
- A band run that passes K2 is not classed (the scorer prints "not classed (K2 passes)").
- D = the number of band runs whose K2 failure is DRIFT; S = the number of band runs whose K2 failure is SPLIT.
The scorer prints each failing run's class with n_c, n_s, n_u, the checkpoints counted and the K2c result; its JSON lists every classed and near-reference episode with r, l and the nearer foot's distance from m0.

DECISION TABLE (exclusive and exhaustive; H = number of band runs that HOLD; K2p = number of band runs passing K2; D and S as above)
1. H >= 2 and (b) holds: ADOPT. Propose committing the band option (off by default) plus its config keys, with the stance stagger term, to the maintainer as the T. rex stance recipe; the best holding band final is offered as the stance parent.
2. H >= 2 but (b) fails: not adopted. The band holds fore-aft but may cost robustness; replicate on 2 more seeds before any commit. With the control's stated passes this line cannot be reached (CREDIT RULE). If it is reached, the control check has failed and the decision is PROVISIONAL.
3. H == 1: not adopted; not separated from seed luck; replicate on 2 more seeds before any commit.
4. H == 0 and K2p >= 2: not adopted; the band holds fore-aft from scratch but the runs fail elsewhere (K1, K3 or K4). The next round targets those failures. The scorer names each failing run's tests and K1 failure modes, and the maintainer chooses among:
   - for K1 on the actuator-saturation rail, the tail saturation habit. Evidence (measured, report only; SP/foot/stance4/satdiag, 20-episode rollouts): stance4 s42 held tail_3_pitch_act at +1 (tail raised). Its mean command was +0.89 to +1.00 at every rolled checkpoint from 2,097,152 to final, with |command| >= 0.9 on 74% to 100% of window steps, against +0.07 and 0% at 1,048,576. No other actuator reached 0.9 on any window step. stance3 Run 2 held the same actuator at the same sign at 3,145,728 to 5,242,880 (90% to 100% of window steps);
   - for K1 through other new failure modes (hop, nosedive, head contact), stance3 line 4's reading: the dose may be too strong;
   - for K3 or K4, the leg exploration-noise floor of stance3 line 2 (log_std >= ln 0.08 on the hip-pitch, knee and ankle actuators).
5. H == 0, K2p <= 1, D >= 2 and S == 0 (the TRIGGER LINE, D-D40 4a): not adopted. At least two band runs fail K2 on drift, and none fails it mainly on split: each K2-failing band run is DRIFT (it failed K2 through K2c alone, or its COMMON episodes outnumber its SPLIT ones at the checkpoints counted, or tie with K2c failing) or OTHER. The criteria read this as D-D40 4a's "the band fixes the split but runs fail K2 on drift (a common fore-aft shift)". How far the split is fixed is not tested beyond this; the scorer prints each run's n_c and n_s beside the control's. The next round tests the v6b ankle spring held in reserve: ankle stiffness 40 -> 250 N.m/rad, damping 45 -> 90, the spring reference at the statue's settled 78.6 deg (a plant revision).
6. H == 0, K2p <= 1, and D <= 1 or S >= 1: not adopted; the band does not hold fore-aft from scratch, and fewer than two band runs fail K2 on drift or at least one fails it mainly on split. The next round chooses between a stronger band (w 0.50, design.md section 3.4's named alternative) and another candidate the maintainer names (with D >= 2, the v6b spring of line 5 among them); the maintainer decides.
Note: in lines 2 and 4, "holds fore-aft" means passes K2. FORE-AFT HOLDS is reported, not used in the table. D and S are read only at H == 0 and K2p <= 1 (lines 5 and 6): at H >= 1 or K2p >= 2 lines 1-4 decide, and D, S and every band run's class are still printed, report only.

Line 1's stance parent: the best holding band final under stance3's rule (most clean panel episodes at final, ties to fewer joint-space plus own-sigma falls). If holding band finals tie under the rule, every tied final is named and the maintainer chooses.
Parent comparison (report only, in every case): the best band final under the same rule is reported beside stance3 Run 2's final (40/40 clean, 0/0 noise falls) and beside the best stance4 final (s52: 39/40 clean, 0/0 noise falls). Fails stay fails. Runs are never stopped or changed on their readouts, nor on a saturation alarm (D-D40 5a). Early reads at 1M and 2M are report-only.

WHEN THE DECISION IS READ
- The decision is read once all three band runs have ended: finished at 12,001,280, or lost past recovery and declared ended by the maintainer.
- Until then, while a band J judge file is missing, the decision is "incomplete" and the scorer gives no line.
- Once the runs have ended and every J checkpoint that exists has been judged, a J judge file still missing or invalid fails every criterion that needs it. The table is read with those fails (score_stance5.py --runs-ended), and the scorer lists the files counted as fails. Such a run's K2 failure is OTHER.
- The decision is PROVISIONAL, and is not acted on, while any of these stands (the scorer prints each one):
  - a judge or scorer placeholder is not filled (HEAD, this file's sha256, the band-on task_sha256, the judge's sha256), or this file's sha256 is not the pinned one;
  - the scorer runs with --validation, or with a HEAD other than the pinned one;
  - a band J judge file has a task_sha256 other than the band-on one above, or a judge_sha256 other than the recorded judge's;
  - before the runs have ended, a band J judge file is invalid;
  - with --runs-ended, a band J checkpoint placed under SP/foot/stance5/runs (its 01_stance/models pair) has no judge file: not every J checkpoint that exists has been judged;
  - the control check fails: stance4's score_final.json missing or not its pinned sha256, its passes not the ones stated above, or the control's verdicts not reproduced from SP/foot/stance4/judged (task_sha256 3fa46708 included).
- A decision that is neither incomplete nor provisional is final.
