3.1 HOW IT IS APPLIED IN TRAINING [measured, code]

  _maybe_ent_coef_decay_callback (TB l1059-1077). On r10: ent_coef 0.005, ent_coef_end 0.0, ent_coef_decay_timesteps

- _train_stage_body appends the callback after the core callbacks and before stage_callback (TB l1524-1533), with
  the decay anchor absolute (the comment at l1519-1523).
- EntCoefDecayCallback (SCH l147-216):
    _on_training_start captures initial = model.ent_coef (l195-203). It defers only while a warm-up marker is set,
    and a same-stage resume clears a stale marker (TB l680-686). The start zips carry none [measured, data keys].
    _on_step: ent_coef = initial + min(1, num_timesteps / 7,000,000) * (end - initial) (l213-215), every env step.
- At the load, kwargs override data (BC l738-739), so model.ent_coef = the config's 0.005, not the zip's stored
  value. The zips store 0.0027530542857142856 at 3,145,728 and 0.0005061057142857145 at 6,291,456, the exact float64
  schedule values [measured, zip data]. D-D43 recorded ent_coef_after_load 0.005 in all 18 runs [measured].
- What train() uses. The value set at the rollout's last step: collect_rollouts adds 4 to num_timesteps per step (OPA
  l220), calls on_step (l224) and on_rollout_end (l266), and learn then calls train() (l337). Nothing runs between
  them. The loss is policy_loss + self.ent_coef * entropy_loss + vf_coef * value_loss (MK l379).
- The update grid. The update after the rollout ending at t uses ent_coef(t). On the original runs: 0.00275305 at
  update 3,145,728; 0.0027472 at 3,153,920; 0.0005061 at 6,291,456; 0.00050025 at 6,299,648 [measured, s42
  policy_probe.npz.copy1]. It is 0 from update 7,004,160 (RO E2).

3.2 THE ARMS IN A CONTINUATION [inferred, from 3.1]
- The checkpoint stage1_<S>_steps.zip is saved inside the last step of the rollout ending at S, before that rollout's
  update (CB l301; OPA l224, l337). So a continuation from S starts from the parameters that collected that rollout.
    It never makes the original's update on that rollout, the one at ent_coef(S).
    Its first update is at S + 8,192.

  hides the change from the config record. Not recommended.

5. (4) CPU DETERMINISM: ARE TWO IDENTICAL CONTINUATIONS BIT-IDENTICAL?
======================================================================================================================
- From the code [inferred]: every source is deterministic given the same inputs.
    One torch thread. Fixed by torch.set_num_threads(1) and OMP/MKL = 1. Note _train_stage_body does not set it
    itself; the harness must.
    MuJoCo, single-threaded.
    DummyVecEnv, sequential.
    Seeds through create_vec_env, eval_env_seed and set_random_seed at the load (OPA l116-117).
    The probe and K3 panel guard the global generators (PP; K3 l683-688).
    Adam and clip_grad_norm on CPU; the target_kl early stop on a deterministic approx_kl.
  Nothing reads the clock into training.
- Measured on this container:
  (a) Training with updates, test scale. The in-suite inertness proof (test_curriculum_balance_telemetry.py l1-41,
      l221-291) trains the real T. rex stance twice: 768 steps, one thread, stock PPO and the MirrorKLPPO pair. It
      asserts bit-equal evaluations.npz, every checkpoint tensor (policy and optimizer) and the VecNormalize
      statistics (l351-373). Recorded as passing here (RUNLOG l520, l534, l630).
  (b) Full-length rollouts without updates. Run-branch HEAD against the port, in separate processes:
        40 rollouts across a 262,144 checkpoint;
        129 rollouts (1,056,768 steps) with in-run evaluations and the K3 panel on.
      Identical rollout buffers, evaluations.npz, gate_progress.npz, policy_probe.npz, and ledger cells column for
      column [measured, RB build/equiv/rollout_digest_pins.txt; review/eq/rb40/compare_records.txt,
      compare_stage.txt; RUNLOG l640]. These ran "updates 0".
  (c) D-D43's gate test T2: same-seed reproducibility with optimizer steps, on a toy model only (BS l254). It passed
      at the gate (RUNLOG l553-554).
  (d) A warning. The torch thread count changes float32 rounding of actions and so whole trajectories (RUNLOG l595).
      Threads must be equal across replicates and reruns.
  (e) No full-scale rerun with updates exists. D-D43 had no crash (RUNLOG l567-572 "no crashed attempt") and its
      logs hold no *_rerun_compare.json [measured, ls TS/logs].
- What "bit-identical" should mean. The .pth members, not the zip bytes: SB3 zips carry system_info.txt and member
  timestamps. Also the VecNormalize mean, var and count, evaluations.npz, and the ledger cells.
  progress.json, run_meta times and the probe's bank_build_seconds differ by design (RB compare excluded
  bank_build_seconds).
- Not covered [inferred]. A different container image or CPU (the container has restarted before, RUNLOG l217, l458,
  l659) may change MKL kernel paths. A rerun after re-provisioning may not be bit-identical.
- A built-in check, free. With the same training seed in arms A and B, the first rollout comes before any update. It
  should be bit-identical across arms: the policy probe's first-row rollout_* arrays equal, ent_coef different. The
  rows diverge from the second on. This proves both determinism across processes and that the arms differ only from
  the first update.

6. (5) THE ADD-ONS' MECHANICS
======================================================================================================================
N (VecNormalize statistics frozen at the start)
- VN: obs_rms and ret_rms update only when `training` is on (step_wait l186-195, _update_reward l209-212, reset
  l301-307).
- MirrorKLPPO installs SymmetricRunningMeanStd at every learn() (MK l235-248, l100-164), and its update() is the
  symmetric one.
- learn() resets the env before any callback hook (BC l421-423, then OPA l319). With training on, the reset's 4
  observations enter obs_rms (the K3 panel's hold_restored_normalization exists for this, K3 l657-674).
  So an exact freeze must happen before learn(): after _create_or_load_model, before TB l1561.
    _train_stage_body exposes no hook there for a stage_callback: its hold loop runs only over the core callbacks (TB
    l1514-1518), and stage_callback receives only eval_env and eval_callback (l1532).
    So the harness must wrap train_base._create_or_load_model, which is looked up at call time (TB l1395), or
    re-implement the body.
- Two forms:
    N1: train_env.training = False. One line. It freezes obs_rms and ret_rms, so the reward scale is frozen too.
    N2: obs_rms only. Replace it with a SymmetricRunningMeanStd subclass whose update() does nothing. It pickles as
        the stock class (MK l149-152), and install_symmetric_obs_rms keeps it because it is already the symmetric
        class (MK l157-161). About 15 lines.
- Proof from the run's records: obs_rms_mean, obs_rms_var and obs_rms_count constant in every probe row after S (and
  ret_rms_var for N1), and every checkpoint pkl's statistics equal to the start pkl's.
S (log_std reset to -1.5 on every actuator)
- policy.log_std is one state-independent parameter per actuator. Fill it with -1.5 under no_grad after the load and
  before the first update; exp(-1.5) = 0.2231.
- The mirror tie needs mirror pairs equal at learn() (MK l174-186, l244). An all-equal fill keeps them equal.
- Precedent: pilot 2's planned reset, "after the parent's parameters load and before the first update, with the
  optimizer's moments kept" (COPY docs/investigations/TREX_STANCE_SYMMETRY_AND_FOOT_2026_10.md l1394-1407; NEXT
  l459-461). Its code lives on claude/pilot-walk-r9-b, not in this tree.
- Where: in the same _create_or_load_model wrapper, or in a stage_callback's _on_training_start. The latter is before
  the first rollout (OPA l319-324) and after the tie hook is registered; an in-place fill keeps the same Parameter.
- Proof: the probe's first row has std_per_actuator 0.2231 on all 15 (it is taken before the first update).
