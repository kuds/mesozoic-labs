# M2: integrated 60 cm T. rex — planned

Replace the M1 fixed payload allocations with the actual head/neck, fixed tail,
small arms, sensors, covers and routed harness. Decide head yaw/nod and jaw
actuation only after the mass and torque budgets support them. The tail stays
fixed unless a later design decision explicitly changes that requirement.

Repeat clearance, balance and behavior evaluations with measured installed
mass/inertia. Qualify single support, walking, turning, perception/control
latency, cooling, service access and payload variation. Preserve the M1 model
and policies under their exact revisions; do not relabel their evidence as M2.

The [r01 simulation candidate](../platform/README.md) evaluates one powered neck pitch axis and one powered tail yaw axis with fixed arms. M2 physical acceptance still requires the measurements and qualification above.
