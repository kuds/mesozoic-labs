# Engineering baseline

The [stack snapshot](baseline.json) records the exact source revisions inspected on November 6, 2026 (Chicago). All four PRs were open. The #651 head and reviewed SHA match byte-for-byte. The live main branch had merged #661, including the anatomical T. rex stance recipe. Since the common ancestor, main has also changed PPO options, gait reporting, plant manifests, training contracts and tests. This branch starts at the #651 tree. A future main integration must reconcile the shared registry, digest snapshots and training code against that revision. Treating main as the base would silently drop 32 stack commits.

The earlier museum concept and size-study BOMs describe different hardware and load cases. Mass, actuator limits and checkpoint compatibility in this experiment follow its own generated model, ledger and plant contract. The engineering evidence covers a smaller robot. A 3–4-foot design needs new component packaging, actuator envelopes, weight transfer, inertia, structural and power calculations.
