## Known gaps (to close before ordering)

- **The foot.** PR #669's foot (pitch-only passive ankle, sole rigid in roll) fails the crawl at 60 cm as at 75 cm; a passive roll hinge helps but does not pass (`sim/REPORT_60CM.md`). Chassis and foot decision D5 stays open.
- **Masses are estimates** from PR #669's parts scaled (structure × 0.8^2.5, shells × 0.8^2, head and tail × 0.8^3) plus catalog masses for bought parts. Weigh the first parts as they arrive.
- **X6 variant.** The US store lists the X6 at 0.78 kg (the X6-50-L); the model uses the X6-60 catalog mass, 0.82 kg (F9). Question M4 asks which variant ships.
- **TTL servos.** XC430 / XL430 are TTL-bus parts; keep the bus adapter in the head or add an RS-485 converter for the neck run.
- **Compute** (D3) is assumed, not decided.
