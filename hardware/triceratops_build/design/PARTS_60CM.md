# Parts list, 60 cm Triceratops (the adopted design)

Generated by `design/parts60.py` on the date of its commit (regenerated 27 Nov 2026 from the adopted model). **Planning estimates: nothing here is quoted, bought, weighed or measured.** Masses and positions come from the adopted simulation model's mass ledger (`sim/generated/mass_ledger_trike60_C_closed_capsule_fore128.json`, written by `sim/build_model60.py --capsule-foot --foreleg 128`); prices come from `budget/size_budget.py` (option C, full expression), whose header gives each source tag: PL = the kickoff parts list (`hardware/triceratops_kickoff/PARTS_PREMIUM.md`, read 7-18 Nov 2026), RC = the repo catalog (maker pages read 7 Nov 2026), WS = web-search summaries read 18 Nov 2026 (listing pages not opened), EST = estimate; plus the radio stop (D15) and the battery cut-off (D47) added here.

The design (`DECISION_LOG.md`): 60 cm option C, 10 X4 and 2 X6 drives on 12S (D2, D4), the capsule sole (D19), the 128 mm upper foreleg (D25), adopted for building as the model `sim/generated/trike60_C_closed_capsule_fore128.xml` (D41); full expression (D14), fail-safe radio stop (D15), an automatic battery cut-off wired into the stop chain (D47), budget counted landed and all-in (D12). Adopting the design orders nothing (D41). Open: compute (D3; the Jetson Orin Nano Super is assumed) and the rest of the chassis (D5).

## Known gaps (to close before ordering)

- **Packaging.** The model's chassis is schematic: both crossmembers and the rails pass through drives, the hind knee drive sits on its own strut's centreline (38.7 mm overlap), the tail pitch servo meets the pelvic crossmember, and the bay's tightest gaps are 0.5-1.0 mm (`design/PACKAGING_PUSHROD_60CM.md`, `sim/check_packaging60.py`). One trial layout there (shortened, inset rails with both crossmembers on their ends, two bay blocks moved 20 mm and the tray stays re-angled to the rails, layout C) puts every part it moves clear of every solid part over the legs', tail's and head's ranges, by 0.60 mm or more; the parts it leaves in place keep today's gaps, and it does not fix the hind knees. It adds drive brackets and needs a new tail mount, inside the frame line's estimate (EST). Chassis CAD under D5. The legs also meet each other within their ranges: the fore and hind legs on one side under the body, at some poses with the fore leg pitched aft and the hind leg forward (soles 48 mm, the whole capsule; lower struts and ankle hubs 19.2 mm), and the two hind soles at the -18 deg inward roll stop (8.49 mm); either needs a joint stop or a coupled limit, which changes the training model's ranges (the report's items 9 and 10).
- **The pushrods** carry up to about 3.1 kN at the front (the X6's peak at the 43 deg transmission angle): sized in `design/PACKAGING_PUSHROD_60CM.md` (steel M8 rod ends and rod; crank pins in a clevis, where bending governs); the "4 pushrod linkages" line above is the budget's estimate, not re-priced for that sizing.
- **The foot.** The capsule sole is chosen (D19); the part to make is a moulded rubber half-capsule rocker with no design yet (`sim/REPORT_FEET60.md`); a bench test of one belongs in the bench plan.
- **The battery cut-off** (D47): wire the BMS's discharge-enable into the watchdog so a trip runs the sit-then-cut sequence (PARTS_PREMIUM.md:303); the bench plan's stop test (B8) has no BMS trip input yet. The Chargery BS12 cell alarm in the power block overlaps the BMS's cell monitoring and could go (not decided; kept in the total).
- **Mass and the hind knees.** The model is over the budget's mass (above). The budget's rule put the hind X4 knees at 1.00 of their continuous rating at 12.69 kg (F12, an estimate); the four 128 mm policies' gait reports put them at 0.55-0.90 of it (RMS over the continuous rating, mean of episodes; worst episode 0.95; `rl/analytics/results/fore128-s*/gait_report.json`, simulation), and the worst drive of any kind at 0.83-0.95 in the judged case (F20). Fallbacks: a 1.2:1 crank ratio, X6 hind knees (+$318 of hardware, +0.92 kg) or a lighter build.
- **Masses are estimates** from PR #669's parts scaled (structure × 0.8^2.5, head and tail × 0.8^3; the body shell at the parts list's 0.85 kg × 0.8^2), PR #697's estimates for its added parts, plus catalog masses for bought parts. Weigh the first parts as they arrive.
- **X6 variant.** The US store lists the X6 at 0.78 kg (the X6-50-L); the model uses the X6-60 catalog mass, 0.82 kg (F9). Question M4 asks which variant ships.
- **TTL servos.** XC430 / XL430 are TTL-bus parts; keep the bus adapter in the head or add an RS-485 converter for the neck run.
- **Compute** (D3) is assumed, not decided.
