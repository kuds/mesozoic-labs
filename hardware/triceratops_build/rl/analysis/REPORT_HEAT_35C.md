# Heat in a 35 C room (D48): the demo course's winding temperatures. Report

27 Nov 2026 (final text 28 Nov 2026). **Desk estimates on simulated torques.** The temperatures come from the build's first-order thermal model
(`telemetry/thermal.py`, a calculation), run on drive torques stored from simulated demo courses
(`analysis/results/d22/demo/`, `analysis/results/d22/demo_walkonly/`). Nothing is measured: nothing is built. No
pre-registration covers this batch, so nothing here is a verdict. Where a rule exists (bench B3, the 100 C reference)
the figures are shown beside it, with the case it was written for. Tables: `analysis/results/heat35/thermal_ambient_2026-11-27.md`
and `.json`, generated by `analysis/thermal_ambient.py`. Decision D48 (`../DECISION_LOG.md`, D48) sets the room the
heat rules assume: "every heat rule in degrees not yet judged is checked at 35 C". Paths are relative to
`hardware/triceratops_build/rl/` (this report is `analysis/REPORT_HEAT_35C.md` there).

- **A 10 C warmer room raises the hottest winding by 11.5-13.3 C** (method A, 8 min, all twelve stored demo courses).

- **The 25 C column reproduces the stored results exactly**: every temperature figure, for every drive and time
  constant, on all twelve stored demo courses (8 default loop, 4 walk-only). The script stops if one differs.
- **Open air, no shell.** The model has no shell, no housing node and no drive-electronics losses
  (`telemetry/thermal.py:33-35`). A shell would raise every figure here. Bench B3 is the measurement that would

## 1. The room option

  fitted at the drive's `ambient_c` field (:64), which defaults to `RATING_AMBIENT_C = 25.0` (:48), the rating test's
  room. `demo_temperatures(..., room_c=None)` (:144) passes the room through. Its `steady_at_thermal_eq_c` stays the
  calibration's own figure, in the 25 C room (:181).
- `demo_course.py --room-c C` (:354; `run(..., room_c=...)`, :233 and :245). The result JSON records `room_c`. The
  printout and the chart's subtitle name the room only when it is not 25 C. In any other room the chart's y-axis takes
  in every trace with 5 C to spare, and the legend moves to the lower right with the axis bottom lowered, in 5 C
  steps, until every trace clears it by 3 C over the minutes it spans (:306-317; `_bottom_clear_of_legend`, :274). At
  25 C the chart is drawn as before.
- **Left out, nothing changes.** `tests/test_thermal_room.py:37` runs `demo_course.main` on fore128-s3's stored segments
  with the stored command. Every stored key comes back exactly; the only new key is `room_c`, 25.0; the stored printout
  comes back line for line. Redrawn from their stored JSON with the current code, all twelve stored demo charts are
  byte-identical to the stored PNGs (checked 27 Nov 2026 and again on 28 Nov 2026 after the legend change; not a
  test). Other tests in the file: the option reaches the command line (:53); a warmer room keeps each drive's R_th
  and C and raises only the air (:87, :165); the chart keeps a warmer or cooler room in frame, with no trace or end
  label behind the legend (:137); B is the optimistic bound (:73, :187); the analysis reproduces the stored 25 C
  figures (:200).

## 2. The method, and why B is only a bound

The model has one thermal node per drive, its winding, with thermal resistance R_th to the air and heat capacity C. The
copper loss is 3 R (tau / Kt)^2, with R rising 0.393 % per K. It is calibrated so that the maker's thermal-equilibrium
torque, held for 90 min, takes the winding from 25 C to 100 C. That torque is 7.5 N m for the X4-36-L and 12 N m for
the X6-60, the X6-50-L's table standing in (`../sim/data/drives.json:6`, `:14-15`; maker datasheets read 7 Nov 2026). The thermal time constant is taken
as 4, 8 and 15 min (`telemetry/thermal.py:10-25`).

- **Method A, the consistent one.** R_th and C are fitted once, at the rating test's room, and then the room is raised.
  R_th and C describe the drive and how its surface sheds heat to the air; to first order a warmer room does not change
  them. The maker's tables start at about 26-27 C for the X4-36-L and about 26 C for the X6-50-L
  (`../../trex_form_study/designlab/data/actuators.json:126`, `:248`; maker data read 7 Nov 2026). So 25 C is a
  round, slightly cautious reading of the rating room. Fitted at 26.5 C instead, the 35 C peaks are 0.9-2.3 C lower
  (method A, 8 min, all twelve demo courses; tables, "Sensitivity" lines).
- **Method B, the optimistic bound.** The drive is refitted as if the maker's test (the thermal-equilibrium torque for
  90 min, ending at 100 C) had started in the warmer room. The same load must then reach the same 100 C from a warmer
  start: a 65 K rise instead of 75 K at 35 C. That needs an R_th 0.867 times A's at the 8 min time constant (0.933
  at 30 C, 0.800 at 40 C; `steady_loads` in the JSON). In other words, a drive that cools better because the room is hotter. This takes the
  maker's rating to hold in any room, which no physical drive does. So B is a lower bound on the temperature, not an
  estimate.
- B can even put a drive at or above its rating below its 25 C figure as the room warms. On 160 mm seed 0, whose
  hottest drive is at 1.58 of rating while standing, B gives 108.4 C at 35 C against 109.9 C stored at 25 C. A warmer
  room cannot cool a winding, so the optimistic figure is the larger of B and the stored 25 C figure. Such cells are
  marked * in the tables; the JSON path is `sets.<set>.runs.<run>.rooms.<room>.B.optimistic`.

The tables use A throughout and show B beside it.

**Beside bench B3 and the 100 C reference** (method A, 8 min, 35 C):
- **What B3 was written for.** B3 (`../bench/BENCH_PLAN_FORELEG.md:61`) passes a drive at "Winding ≤ 90 °C at 35 °C".
  It was written for a measured winding (the winding from the drive's own report, the case by thermal camera) on the
  75 cm T20 foreleg, shell on, over a 30 min loop with two 2 min held paw poses. The model here is the 60 cm robot in open air, with no
  shell, over the whole demo. So these figures are shown beside B3, not judged by it.
- **128 mm seed 2** reaches 90 C at 29 min. It is at or over 90 C for 49 of its 84.3 min in all, dipping under 90 C in
  the standing breaks. Its peak in the first 30 min, B3's loop length, is 90.6 C.
- **128 mm seed 3** reaches 90 C at 21 min and is at or over it for 68 of its 89.6 min; first 30 min, 95.2 C.
- **128 mm seeds 0 and 1** stay under 90 C: 74.7 and 72.9 C in the first 30 min, 76.3 and 74.4 C over the pack.
  On each 128 mm seed, one drive at most is over 90 C.
- **No 128 mm winding reaches 100 C** in the default demo at 35 C. The highest is 98.3 C at the 8 min time constant
  (99.5 C at the top of the 4-15 min range). At 40 C, seed 3 passes 100 C after 27 min (104.5 C).
- **160 mm:** seed 0 reaches 90 C at 12 min and 100 C at 16 min, with two drives over 100 C. Seeds 1 and 2 reach 90 C
  at 21 and 30 min. Seed 3 stays under 90 C (80.6 C).
- **Walked without a break** at 35 C, every stored walk-only case passes 100 C, after 14-19 min.
- **The demo's own no-break figures.** In the nominal walking segment, the worst drive is at 0.867, 0.847, 0.998 and
  1.006 of its rating on 128 mm seeds 0-3. Walked without a break, that drive would settle at 89.7, 86.6, 112.5 and
  114.2 C in a 35 C room (tables, last section).

**Steady loads** (desk, method A, the same model). The ranges are over the time constants; the X4-36-L and X6-60 agree
to the printed 0.1 C.
- Held at 0.85 of its continuous rating, a drive settles at 87.1-87.5 C in a 35 C room (75.1-75.5 C at 25 C).
- Held at 1.00, it settles at 112.9-113.7 C (100.0-100.7 C at 25 C).
- In a 35 C room a steady 0.866-0.870 of rating settles at 90 C, and 0.927-0.931 at 100 C.
