# The foreleg comparison (D22): 128 against 160 mm under the RL policy. Report

26 Nov 2026. **Simulation only.** Nothing here was measured on hardware. Rules: `PREREG_FORELEG_D22.md` (D22, D23,
D24). The judged measures and the main reported figures: `REPORT_FORELEG_D22_APPENDIX.md`, generated by
`d22_appendix.py` from the stored tool output. The full per-run record is each run's
`analytics/results/<run>/gait_report.md` and `robustness/results/<run>/summary_<run>.md`. Run history:

## The answer

**The pre-registered judge chooses 128 mm.** 128 mm is the only length whose four policies all pass both walking
rules. Both rules are judged in the gait report's target case: a 0.25 m/s command, randomised model and floor, a
10 ms delay, 5 episodes.
- **F1, flat walking:** a mean speed of at least 0.225 m/s and no falls.
- **F2, drive heat:** every drive's RMS torque, averaged over the 5 episodes, below its continuous rating.

160 mm failed F2 on three of its four seeds, each time on a different drive: 1.014, 1.013 and 1.094 of rating.
Under D24's order a length that fails F1 or F2 on any seed loses when the other passes on all four. So the
foot-load, fall and heat tests on the four-seed means were never reached.

Had they been applied, the loads and heat tests would also have kept 128 mm:
- **Loads:** 160 mm's mean front-foot load was 117.6 N against 128 mm's 121.6 N, a ratio of 0.967. The rule
  needed 0.85 or less.
- **Heat:** 160 mm's mean heat was worse overall (H_all 1.000 against 0.898; the rule allowed +0.035) and on the
  front drives (H_front 0.940 against 0.803; allowed +0.10).

This is a simulation result about this setup. It is an input to the owner's decision under D23 ("128 mm unless
beaten"), not the decision itself.

**What it does not say.** "128 mm chosen" means 160 mm did not clearly beat it. Here, that is because 160 mm did not
keep its drives within their ratings on every seed. It does not mean 128 mm is better in every respect. Where
128 mm did worse:
- **Drift:** it drifted sideways past run 3's terrain and slippery-floor limits (T3, T4) on all four seeds. 160 mm
  failed each limit on two of four seeds (T3 on s0 and s1, T4 on s0 and s3). Four seeds do not separate the
  lengths on drift (section 4).
- **Falls:** it fell more in the sweep, a mean of 18.5 falls against 14.25. The falls test would have passed
  160 mm.
- **Pushes:** it fell more under the hardest sideways pushes, 55 of 64 episodes at +-1.25 m/s against 42 of 64.

**128 mm runs its drives close to their continuous rating.** In the judged case, 128 mm's worst drive is at
0.83-0.95 of rating. In the cases that are reported but not judged:
- **The nominal model:** two 128 mm seeds reach or pass the rating. Seed 2 is at 1.005 in the gait report with
  observation noise off. Seed 3 is at 1.006 in the demo course's walking segment and 0.997 at the sweep's nominal
  point.
- **Conditions typical of training:** 31 of the sweep's points are inside the training ranges and like what training
  produced. On these, 128 mm stays under its rating on seeds 0-2. Seed 3 is at or just over it on 7 points
  (1.000-1.018: a 3 cm step, friction 1.0 and five pushes). 160 mm goes over on 26, 23, 29 and 0 of the 31, and
  run 3's leg on none.
- **Beyond what training produced:** six more points are nominally inside the training ranges.
  - The sweep's ramps (3, 5 and 8 deg) do not end, so the robot climbs for the whole episode: about 4 m and 18 s.
    Training ramps are 0.8-2.0 m up (3-8 s), then a 0.4-1.5 m top and a ramp down.
  - The sweep's x0.9 and x1.1 masses apply to every body at once, and its x0.85 force limit to every drive at once.
    Training draws each body's mass and each drive's limit separately, so the whole robot's mass stayed within about
    x0.92-1.08.

  On these six points three of the four 128 mm seeds go over: on the ramps seed 1 at 1.020 (8 deg), seed 2 at
  1.035-1.155 and seed 3 at 1.096-1.216; at x1.1 mass 1.006-1.064. 160 mm goes over on 5, 5, 6 and 1 of the six,
  and run 3's leg on none.

- **Beyond the training ranges,** every 128 mm seed goes over:
  - at servo (leg-command) delays of 20-30 ms: 1.07-1.22. D9 set 10 ms as a conservative estimate until the bench
    measures it.
  - with every body 20 % heavier: 1.04-1.12;
  - with 2 kg of payload: 1.02-1.11.

  Run 3's leg goes over at those delays too, and by more (1.20-1.26). It goes over on one seed at x1.2 mass (1.03,
  against 0.93 on the other) and on one seed with 2 kg (1.01, against 0.91).

**Straight walking only.** All eight policies walk straight. PR #697's case for 160 mm rested on lower turning and
pivoting loads and a more legible elbow, and these runs test neither.

Eight runs: four seeds (0-3) for each of the two forelegs, `sim/generated/trike60_C_closed_capsule_fore128.xml`
(12.751 kg) and `..._fore160.xml` (12.808 kg). The two models are PR #697's repackaged forelegs with length-scaled

  - steps up to 4 cm, ramps up to 10 deg, sideways pushes up to 1.0 m/s;

Each judged run had one session, from step 0, completed at 10,002,432 steps, with a finite final pair. Training
took 3.57-5.13 h at 542-779 env steps/s.

The judged pair is each run's final pair. Its sha256 is in the run log and in each tool's provenance.

**Run 3 is a reference, not a third arm.** Its 188 mm foreleg cannot be built (F18). Its fore-assembly also differs

## What this can and cannot conclude

- **It can say:** under run 3's training, with these two models and four seeds each, 160 mm's policies did not keep
  every drive under its rating on three of four seeds; 128 mm's did on all four.
- **On the F2 margins:** they are small. 160 mm failed by 1.3-9.4 %, and 128 mm's worst seed passed with 5.5 % to
  spare. With other seeds or other training settings either length could land on either side. The gate is strict by
  design (D24).
- **On foot loads:** the study cannot separate a difference of a few percent between the lengths. The power estimate
  above is rough, built from one pair of seeds on another leg.
- **On turning:** nothing. These policies walk straight. Turning loads and heat under the RL policy are for the
  steering run (D20), which has not been trained yet.
- **The heat and battery figures are model estimates:**
