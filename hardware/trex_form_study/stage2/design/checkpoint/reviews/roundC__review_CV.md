FINDINGS (most serious first)

1. minor - after an env's exception in step or reset the pool stays usable, and which envs advanced depends on W. V:354-368 (the worker replies with the error and goes on), V:672-681 (the parent raises after all replies; _broken is not set). My probe e8, env 1 of 4 made to fail inside step: "DummyVecEnv: ValueError; steps taken per env [1, 1, 0, 0]; usable after: yes" against "PoolVecEnv W=2: ValueError; steps taken per env [1, 1, 1, 1]; usable after: yes" (the other block stepped and its results were dropped). Likewise env_method(indices=[7, 3, 4]) raises env 3's error (first in worker order, T:484-487) where DummyVecEnv raises env 7's. It reaches a run only if the trainer catches an env exception and carries on. Fix: set _broken when a step or reset exchange returns an error (keep get_attr / set_attr / env_method errors non-fatal: has_attr and VecEnv's render_mode probe need them); or say in CT's brief that an exception out of step/reset ends the pool.

2. minor - a child that connects and then says nothing blocks the constructor past CONNECT_TIMEOUT_S. V:606: listener.accept() runs the key handshake with blocking reads; the POLL_S timeout is on the listening socket only. The docstrings V:31-33 and V:586-589 say the wait ends at its limit. Probe e4 (limit set to 3 s, a child that connects a raw socket and sleeps 25 s): "the constructor was still blocked after 15.1 s", then "raised after 26.5 s: RuntimeError: vecpool worker 0 ... left before it connected: exit code 0". A real worker answers at once, so this needs a stopped or foreign process. Fix: a threading.Timer at the deadline that terminates the children still pending (the blocked handshake then ends with EOF and the existing "left before it connected" path names the worker); or word the docstring to what holds.

3. minor (tests) - two behaviours the brief names are held by no test. (a) the atexit hook (V:546): with `self._finalizer.atexit = False` added, "41 passed, 1 skipped in 18.88s" (the workers leave on EOF anyway and the directory is gone since connect). Fix: in T:711-735, case "exit", assert _gone(pids, 0.0) right after proc.wait() (the hook waits for the workers; without it they are still leaving). (b) the connect limit (V:601-604): with the check disabled, "41 passed, 1 skipped in 19.19s". The path itself works: probe e6 (limit 2 s, two children that sleep) "raised after 3.3 s: vecpool workers [0, 1] did not connect within 2 s", children left [], no socket directory. Fix: that probe as a test with CONNECT_TIMEOUT_S monkeypatched to 1.0.

4. minor (tests) - the tests assume a short temporary directory that is theirs alone. With TMPDIR set to a 154-character path: "2 failed, 39 passed, 1 skipped in 17.25s" - test_close_leaves_no_child_process_and_no_socket_directory (T:630 compares with tempfile.gettempdir() while the pool rightly falls back to /tmp) and test_a_worker_that_cannot_start_fails_the_constructor (T:741 opens its own Listener under tmp_path: "OSError: AF_UNIX path too long"). The pool itself worked in the other 39. Also _socket_dirs() (T:279-280) globs <tempdir>/vecpool-* of the whole machine, so a pool built by any other process between two snapshots fails `_socket_dirs() <= before` (T:634, 751, 767, 780, 802, 819); not observed, read from the code; other agents' train2 tests will build pools on this machine. Fix: give these tests their own short directory (monkeypatch tempfile.tempdir to a tempfile.mkdtemp(dir="/tmp"), TMPDIR for the two child scripts) and take T:741's path from it.

5. minor - two clean-up gaps on a kill, neither stated. (a) a trainer killed while its workers start leaves the socket directory: probe e3 "socket dirs left: ['/tmp/vecpool-2nate123'] | contents: [['s']]" (V:545-576; I removed mine). Nothing can clean after SIGKILL; a few bytes each time; accept and say so. (b) a forked copy of the trainer keeps the workers alive after the trainer's SIGKILL, because it holds the connections: probe e2 "3 s after the trainer's SIGKILL, forked copy alive: True | workers alive: True True", gone 0.00 s after the fork ended. Children started with subprocess do not hold them (close-on-exec). Fix: one sentence in the docstring and in CT's brief: no fork-start multiprocessing and no os.fork in the trainer while a pool is open.

6. minor (nit) - the PPO test leaves two empty /tmp/SB3-<time> directories per run (stable-baselines3's default logger folder; T:853-877); the brief: "Tests write only under tmp_path". 764 such directories are in /tmp now from all PPO tests of the study. Fix: monkeypatch.setenv("SB3_LOGDIR", str(tmp_path)).
