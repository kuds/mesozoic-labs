The source duty budget for the processor tray is 31 W including module, storage, fan and carrier. With a 10 K air-temperature rise and ambient air at about 1.2 kg/m³ and 1,005 J/(kg·K), ideal throughflow is 2.57 L/s. A design inlet carrying this flow at 2 m/s needs about 1,285 mm² **net open area**, plus an outlet and room for flow to turn. This is a packaging target. Pressure drop, leakage, hot-air recirculation, throttle behavior and motor heat require a closed-cover bench measurement. A blocked upward ray can be accommodated by a side duct if that duct is dimensioned and tested.

Unsupported boards and brake/disconnect hardware must be selected, weighed and physically gauged. The manufacturing sequence should start with a one-to-one tray/front-camera/harness mock-up, then verify disassembly in a supported frame and test thermal soak with the selected covering and full electrical load.
