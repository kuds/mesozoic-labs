THE TEST (pre-registered)
- An exact unpaired permutation test, one-sided (the blind arm lower), alpha 0.05. Statistic: the difference of arm
  means, control minus blind (with 4 per arm, equivalently the control arm's sum). p = the share of the C(8, 4) = 70
  ways to choose 4 of the 8 primaries as "control" whose statistic is at least the observed one (the observed split
  included; a tie counts against the reading). Exact rational arithmetic on the recorded values: two sums tie only if
  equal as exact rationals of the recorded doubles (score_bs.perm_test).
- Minimum attainable p = 1/70 = 0.0143 (the observed split strictly above all others, e.g. complete separation).
  Attainable p <= 0.05: 1/70, 2/70, 3/70 (0.0429); 4/70 = 0.0571 is not. Size under exchangeable continuous values:
  3/70 = 0.0429 [computed, WORK power_bs.out].
- Beside it, report only (never a line): the four paired differences control - blind (same seed), their exact
  one-sided sign-flip p (16 sign vectors; minimum 1/16 = 0.0625, so it can never reach 0.05), and the as-counted p.
- Pairing and the unpaired test: if the shared env seeds correlate a pair positively, splitting each pair across the
  arms makes the unpaired test conservative [inferred]; with no correlation it is exact.
- THE FILL (missing never helps the hypothesis): a MISSING control primary counts as -infinity and a MISSING blind
  primary as +infinity (compared infinite part first). This is the supremum of p over every value the missing ones
  could have had (p cannot fall as a blind value rises or a control value falls), so a fill never makes REDUCED
  easier than the complete data would. With 4 per arm any single MISSING continuation gives p >= 36/70 = 0.514, so
  REDUCED cannot be read once one is missing; the as-counted p is then printed (report only).
