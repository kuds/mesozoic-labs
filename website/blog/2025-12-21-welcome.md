# Welcome to Mesozoic Labs

We're excited to announce Mesozoic Labs—an open-source platform for dinosaur-inspired locomotion research through simulation and reinforcement learning.

<!-- truncate -->

## Our Mission

We use dinosaur-inspired simulated bodies to explore legged locomotion and reinforcement learning. The current project focuses on MuJoCo research models; they are not validated anatomical reconstructions or physical robots.

## What We're Building

- **Physics Simulations** - Articulated MuJoCo models inspired by dinosaur morphology
- **RL Training** - PPO, SAC, and staged curricula for learning simulated locomotion
- **Hardware Roadmap** - Future prototypes and sim-to-real validation; no hardware-transfer results are currently published

## Stay Tuned

This is just the beginning. Follow our progress on [GitHub](https://github.com/kuds/mesozoic-labs) and star the repository to get updates.
